# the_walk: continuity bible (r3)

## World
- **Place:** a new place to claim, `gullwick` ("Gullwick"): a shore hamlet on the West March coast, within ~200
  blocks of the Ridge tower (-2000, -445), clear of Leo's House and the stronghold islets.
  - The library hut (a librarian, a lectern, an anvil), a dock with a tied oak boat, a short shingle shore at the
    dock's foot, and an island **60 blocks** out across open cold ocean (not frozen: Frost Walker needs water).
- **Day:** 2300.
- **Coordinates:** dock-relative everywhere. (0, 0, 0) is the dock's end, its deck flush at sea level (stand at sea
  level + 1); -z is out to sea (west). Grandma's window is (0, 3, -60). The hut's door is about 20 blocks inland.
- **New canon facts:** Gullwick; Grandpa and Grandma (players, a couple apart for ten years); Mom (their daughter, a
  player) and the kid; the ice path on day 2300 (Frost Walker ice melts, so nothing is stamped after the day).

## Accuracy (for the owner)
- Rerolling a librarian by breaking her lectern before any trade is vanilla Java; Frost Walker is a treasure
  enchantment librarians can sell, max level II.
- **Exposure:** three thousand rerolls without Frost Walker is effectively impossible. A novice librarian offers a
  random enchanted book about 2/3 of the time, from ~37 tradeable enchantments, at a random level, so Frost Walker II
  comes up about 1 roll in 110 (any level: about 1 in 55). Players who know rerolling may comment "you'd get it in a
  hundred tries". Options for the owner: keep 3,000 (a kid's exaggeration; comment bait), or "three HUNDRED" (about
  a 7% chance of no Frost Walker II in 300 rolls: unlucky but real).
