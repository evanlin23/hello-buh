# the_walk: spine, beats, clue map (r6, the Roblox story format)

## The ending first
**The reveal frame:** a high wide from behind an old man as he steps off the end of a dock onto open sea at dawn.
The water freezes into a white disc under each step, and the path points at one lit window on an island, with
Grandma standing in it. Cut.

**The first frame it re-explains:** Grandpa breaking the same lectern, again.

## Spine
- **Every day,** Grandpa breaks the librarian's lectern, reads her new book, says "Not THAT one", and breaks it
  again: three hundred times, and he won't say why. His grandkid thinks he just hates that librarian.
- **Because of that,** we learn that Grandma lives on the island, that he won't take a boat, and that when she left
  she told him "Come back when the sea freezes." Mom says it never freezes here.
- **Until finally,** the librarian holds up a new book, and Grandpa doesn't break the lectern: he puts it on his boots,
  walks off the dock, and the sea freezes under his feet.

## Twist checklist
- [x] **Changes line 1:** from a grudge against a librarian to keeping her to her word.
- [x] **Fair:** the condition (L07) comes before the book; Frost Walker and lectern rerolling are vanilla; 300 rolls without Frost Walker II is unlucky but real (bible "Accuracy").
- [x] **In the picture:** the empty boat (S05), Grandma in her window (S04, S08, S11).
- [x] **Naive:** the kid never hints, never sounds afraid, never connects the lectern to the window.
- [x] **Said, then shown:** L10 says it (boots, dock, the sea froze) over the ice forming; the tooltip is a bonus for gamers.
- [x] **One step:** "Frost Walker freezes the sea under him, so her words come true."
- [x] **Causes:** the kid buys the book (S10-S11), the anvil clank on the cut, L10 "put the book on his boots".
- [x] **Rewatch:** L03, L03b, L04's "scared of the dark", L05, L08; L11 sends you back to L01.

## "Why?" interrogation
- **"Why not build a bridge?"** She said come when the SEA FREEZES; a bridge or a boat doesn't freeze it (L05 + L07 + L08).
- **"Why doesn't it freeze in winter?"** Vanilla: cold ocean never freezes (only frozen ocean and snowy biomes do), so Mom is right.
- **"Why didn't she come back?"** Never said; it doesn't move the question.
- **"Why does the kid trade?"** Shown, not said: a merchant screen exists only for the player who opens it.
