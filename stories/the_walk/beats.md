# the_walk: spine, beats, clue map (r3)

## The ending first
**The reveal frame:** a high wide from behind an old man as he walks off the end of a dock onto open sea at dawn. The
water freezes into a white disc under each step, and the path points at one lit window on an island, with Grandma
standing in it. Cut.

**The first frame it re-explains:** Grandpa breaking the same lectern, again.

## Spine
- **Every day,** Grandpa breaks the librarian's lectern, reads her new book, says "Not THAT one", and breaks it
  again. He has done it three thousand times.
- **One day,** the librarian holds up a new book, and Grandpa says nothing.
- **Because of that,** we learn (in the middle) that Grandma lives on the island, that he won't take a boat or build a
  bridge, and that when she left she told him "Walk the water to me. Or don't come." Mom says that means never.
- **Until finally,** he walks off the dock, and the sea freezes under him.

## "Why?" interrogation
- **"Why not build a bridge?"** She said walk the WATER; a bridge isn't the water (L05 + L07 + L08).
- **"Why didn't she come back?"** Never said; it doesn't move the question.
- **"Why does the kid trade?"** Never said; it's shown (the kid at the counter, Grandpa reading over the kid's
  shoulder). A merchant screen exists only for the player who opens it.
- **"Boots?"** In the kid's inventory in S10, the anvil clank on the cut, glinting on his feet in S11.
- **"3,000 rolls for one book?"** See bible "Accuracy": a known exposure, flagged to the owner.
