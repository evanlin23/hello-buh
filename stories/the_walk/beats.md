# the_walk: spine, beats, clue map (r1)

## The ending first
**The reveal frame** is a high wide behind an old man walking off the end of a dock onto open sea at dawn. The water
freezes into a white circle under every step, and a path grows behind him toward one lit window on an island. Cut.

**The first frame it re-explains** is Grandpa breaking the same lectern, again.

## Spine
- **Every day,** Grandpa breaks the librarian's lectern, reads her new book, says "Not THAT one", and breaks it again.
  That's three thousand times.
- **One day,** the librarian holds up a new book, and Grandpa says nothing.
- **Because of that** (the grandkid tells us), Grandma lives on the island, won't come back, and told him "you'll
  WALK here, or don't COME", and he won't take a boat.
- **Until finally,** he walks off the dock, and the sea freezes under him.
