# forty_one_cats: spine, beats, clue map (r1)

## The ending first
**The reveal frame** is a wide over a snowy lane at night. A creeper walks through a gap in the row of cats on the
wall, past an open door with a villager inside (it doesn't look), straight to one glass window, and swells. Inside, a
boy in bed turns his head to it. The cat sleeps by the fire, too far away. Cut on the hiss.

**The first frame it re-explains** is forty-one cats on the wall, all facing out.

## Spine
- **Every day,** Grandma's forty-one cats sit on the village wall facing out, and none may come in.
- **One night,** the coldest, the boy brings the cat by his window in by the fire.
- **Because of that,** there's a gap in the wall.
- **Until finally,** a creeper walks through it, past every villager's open door, to the only player in the village.
