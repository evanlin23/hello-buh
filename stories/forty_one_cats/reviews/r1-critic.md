# forty_one_cats: critic r1

Verdict: **REVISE** (blocker: too obvious, and the reveal breaks the game's rule. If r2's cold read is still sure at the midpoint, **kill**.)

## Cold read
Grandma keeps 41 cats on the wall, facing out, "to keep the quiet ones out". The boy takes the cat by his window up to bed, and a creeper walks through the gap, past the villagers' open doors, to his window and swells. Twist: the cats keep creepers out, and the wall is for him.
- **Mystery test:** the question is set by 3 s. **The midpoint fails:**
  - Players say "creepers" at L01 and "for him" by L05.
  - Casual viewers get "QUIET ones" plus the hiss, which spells it by 60%.
  - Both are sure.
- **The cut fails:** S10's walk and swell take about 4 s, not 1.2 s.
- **Why?** **The cat is on his bed, about 3 blocks from the window.** A creeper flees a cat within 6 blocks, and it can only swell within 3 blocks of him. The ending breaks the story's own rule, and players will say so.

## Must-fixes
1. **L03:** write **"They keep YOU safe, dear."** It's literally true (singular "you") and removes the creeper giveaway.
2. **L07-L08:** write "I took the cat by my window / IN by the FIRE." / "She purred. I went to BED."
   - The cat stays at the hearth, 7+ blocks from his ground-floor window.
3. **S10:** start with the creeper passing the last open door, villager inside, 3 blocks from his window. It swells at the window. Cut 1.0 s later.
4. **Notes:** "[indignant on their behalf]" becomes "[indignant]", and "[cosy and pleased with himself]" becomes "[cosy]". Drop "sleepy" (breathy).
