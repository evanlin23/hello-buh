# thirty_one_raids: continuity bible (r3)

## World
- **Scene order and time** (ticks). The story covers many days and ends on the final night:
  - S01 dawn after raid 31, 23500 (Grandpa hangs the 31st totem; morning light through the east window).
  - S02 a later day, dusk 12500 (sunset glow in the **west**, behind the camera; the ridge in the east dark against a
    pinkish sky).
  - S03-S04 the same evening he came home, 12600 (after S02's 12500: time never runs backwards across a cut).
  - S05 dawn after raid 31, 23800 (the same morning as S01: he walks out and the village thanks him).
  - S06 a sunset on another day, 12000 (looking east: the ridge in warm side light).
  - S07 inside, 14000. S08-S09 at the gate, 13000 (a typical raid night: "He drinks it at the GATE").
  - S10 inside, another night, 14000.
  - S11-S14 **the final night**: S11 12400 (at his door), S12 12500 (the drink), S13-S14 13100 (the raid starts 30 s
    after the drink: 600 ticks, while the sky still reads; the S12→S13 cut carries it, the horn marks it).
- Weather clear throughout.
- **Numbers the script says:** THIRTY-ONE raids (L01) = 31 totems in the frames (S01) = 31 filled frames of 32.
  The raid: five pillagers and one ravager on screen (not said). **Staged, not wave 1:** vanilla's first wave is 4
  pillagers with a bannered captain, and ravagers come from wave 3; we show the threat, not the wave count.

## Positions and screen direction
- **East = out, toward the ridge and the raiders. West = home, the village.** In every gate shot the road and the
  ridge are ahead (east) and the village is behind.
- S02: Grandpa walks toward camera (west) on the road. S05: he crosses the square left to right.
- S08-S09: Grandpa in the gate faces east; the narrator is behind him (west). In S09 he turns his head back over his
  right shoulder.
- S12-S13: the narrator in the gate faces east, the raiders coming up the road ahead. S14: he turns about 25 degrees
  right (east-south-east): Grandpa 2 blocks east and 2 south of him, just outside the south post, facing east, at
  about +23 degrees (frame right, MS at 2.8 blocks); the raiders at about -22 degrees (frame left). Keep every gate shot on the south
  side of the road (the 180-degree line is the road).

## The narrator
- Hand empty S01 to the end of S11; the bottle from "FIGHT" in S11 through S12; empty S13-S14. Hearts full; no armor.
- He is a player (only a player can drink an ominous bottle and start a raid). He never speaks to Grandpa on screen.
- Where he got the bottle: from Grandpa's slack hand at his door (S11 shows it leave his hand and come up in mine).
