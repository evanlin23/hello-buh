# thirty_one_raids: continuity bible

## World
- **Scene order and time** (ticks). The story covers a few days and ends on the final night:
  - S01 dawn after raid 31, 23500 (sun just up in the east, behind the gate side).
  - S02 a later day, dusk 12500 (sunset glow in the **west**, so the ridge in the east is dark against a pinkish sky).
  - S03-S04 the same evening, 12000. S05-S06 that night inside, 14000 (lantern-lit).
  - S07 a sunset on another day, 12000 (looking east: the ridge in warm side light).
  - S08 inside, 14000. S09-S10 at the gate the same night, 13000 (dusk: these are "every raid", a typical night).
  - S11-S14 **the final night**, dusk 13000 then 13600 (the raid comes 30 s after the drink; cut it: S12 to S13 is a
    time cut carried by the horn).
- Weather clear throughout.
- **Numbers the script says:** THIRTY-ONE raids (L01) = 31 totems on the wall (S05) = 31 filled frames of 32. Nothing
  else counted.

## Positions and screen direction
- **East = out, toward the ridge and the raiders. West = home, the village.** In every gate shot the ridge is ahead
  (east) and the village is behind.
- S01: Grandpa crosses the square left to right (west to east, heading home to his yard by the gate).
- S02: he walks toward camera (west) on the road.
- S09-S10: Grandpa at the gate faces east; the narrator is behind him (west). In S10 he turns his head back over his
  right shoulder.
- S12-S13: the narrator at the gate faces east. S14: he turns round (west); Grandpa is now between him and the village,
  facing east toward him. Keep the camera on the south side of the road for all gate shots (the 180-degree line is the
  road).

## The narrator
- Hand empty S01-S11 (he's a kid who carries nothing); the bottle from S12. Hearts full; no armor.
- He is a player (only players can drink an ominous bottle and start a raid). He never speaks to Grandpa on screen.
