**Counts:** 14 shots, close-ups: none. Three EWS (road, hill) and WS establishing shots for the house, yard, square
and gate. No torches and no banners anywhere in the raid.
**Screen direction:** the ridge and the road out are always **east**, beyond the gate. Grandpa walks home west (toward
camera in S02) and crosses the square left to right (S05). At the gate he faces east (out). The boy faces east at the
gate in S12-S13 and turns right (south) to Grandpa in S14; the raiders stay at frame left.
**Readability at 300 px:** S01 (the empty frame must read: end of the bottom row, lit), S13 (a ravager is the biggest
shape; the pillagers' grey against the dusk road; check at 300 px on the shot sheet).
