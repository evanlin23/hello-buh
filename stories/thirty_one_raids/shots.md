**Counts:** 14 shots, close-ups: none. Three EWS (road, hill, raid), all carrying a clue (the tower in two, the raid in
the third).
**Screen direction:** the ridge and tower are always **east**, beyond the gate. Grandpa walks home west (toward camera
in S02); at the gate he faces east (out). The boy faces east at the gate in S12-S13 and turns west (back to the
village) in S14.
**Readability at 300 px:** S05 (the empty frame must read: put it at the end of the bottom row, light it), S13 (torches
and flags against a dusk sky: keep the sky lighter than the ridge).
