## Who knows what
- The kid: thinks the danger at night is the zombies, and that Grandpa is the one in danger (slow). Knows he hasn't
  slept in 300 days and is proud he isn't tired. Does not know phantoms exist.
- Grandpa: knows the kid hasn't slept (phantoms come for players who don't sleep; villagers never count). Asks every
  day for the night (he can't sleep either: beds only work at night). L13 "YOU need sleep, kid" is the warning.
- The viewer: a Minecraft player knows phantoms; L11 and L13 plant it; the death screen confirms it.

door faces east, so in S15-S17 the kid stands on the porch facing east, down the slope, toward the tree.
