# scarecrow r1: critic

Verdict: REVISE. Overall 7.0/10.

**Cold read.** For 63 mornings a snow trail has run from Grandpa's scarecrow to a boy's window. He hears groaning, finds rotten flesh, stays up, and the scarecrow is gone. With the S11 picture, the twist is that the scarecrow is a snow golem that has guarded his wall from zombies all along. With sound only, it reads as a ghost Grandpa.

**Scores**
- Hook 7: snow on green grass is a real image, but at 300 px it's a thin white line seen at a low angle. Snow trail plus pumpkin says "snow golem" to a player at second 1.
- Second beat 6: L02 restates a rule. The fix that fails (L03) only comes at 7 s.
- Mystery 7: one clue per beat, but players have "zombies" at L05 (17 s, 40%).
- Twist 7: flips L01 (stalker to guard), one step, the game shows it. It's predictable, and it's the slate's sixth "the creepy thing was Grandpa's love" (broken_rail, gold_for_the_portal, lantern_bay, slow_falling, thirty_one_raids).
- Fairness 7: every clue is shown and misread. Unanswered "why": he looks at a snow body every morning (S08) and never says so.
- Rewatch 8: L04 "STOOD there all night, STARING", L06 "GROSS little GIFT", L08 "keep an EYE on me".
- Agency 7: he shovels, stays up and opens the window. No accidents.
- Character 7: narrator only, Mom quoted with "said", L08 "NOT helping" has voice.
- Delivery 6: the request ignores the Pip recipe, and L11 is directed "soft".
- Visual 7: full-scale farmhouse on a meadow slope. S10's "GONE" is an unlit spot 21 blocks away at night.
- Continuity 7: the state of the S03 dug gap at night isn't pinned. The golem's return to the wheat each dawn isn't vanilla.
- Owner fit 8: no "...", the out-of-context read passes, 113 words, ~42 s.

Against runaway: worse. Runaway hides its answer in the POV. This one stands in the field in daylight.
