## The ending first
S16: from the path, Jonah walks away empty-handed down the torch-lit street toward the sunrise. He set his own torch
down by our door and waved; he said nothing. Pip, bright: "He FORGOT to SAY, "See you TOMORROW."" Cut on "TOMORROW".

## Spine
- Every night, Jonah, the village's only player, walks me home with his torch so I'm never in the dark, and says
  "See you tomorrow!"
- One morning there are six thousand torches on my village, and I'm sure a party is coming.
- Because of that every clue makes me happier: his stuff in our chest (presents!), his house gone (he's moving in!).
- Until finally the last growing row leads to my door, where Jonah sets down his own torch, waves, and walks away, and
  forgets to say "see you tomorrow".

- [x] Fair play: the habit, the words, the gifts, the house, the row all come before L14; the reveal adds no fact.

- [x] Misread, not hidden: each clue's innocent reading is on a line; the kid never hints or sounds afraid.
