# six_thousand_torches: beats (r1)

From stories/nine_thousand_torches (r2, critic 7.8) and docs/writers_room_2026-11-10.md, with the batch-2 premise
audit's must-changes (docs/pitches/batch2_premise_audit_2026-11-11.md #6):
1. the ending is the **missing "see you TOMORROW"** over Jonah walking away: no chat line, no quit, no "left the game"
   (that was Dog on the Hill's ending);
2. no claim that mobs stop spawning forever: the gesture is in the kid's own line, **"So I'm never in the DARK"** (L06),
   said about the nightly torch, where it's true;
3. the decoy **"Something BIG must be COMING"** stays (L04).
Also: every name set up before it's used (Jonah, L05); no unseen person named (r1's "Mom made me PROMISE" is now
"he only had one LAST night", so no Mom off screen); Jonah's one line in his own voice (L07), while on screen.

## The ending first
S16: from beside our door, the last torch burning at frame bottom, Jonah walks away down the torch-lit street toward
the sunrise. He waved; he said nothing. Pip: "But this TIME, he didn't SAY, see you TOMORROW." Cut on "TOMORROW".

## Spine
- Every night, Jonah, the village's only player, puts a torch by my door so I'm never in the dark, and says "See you
  tomorrow!"
- One morning there are six thousand torches on my village, and I'm sure something big is coming.
- Because of that I find the clues and misread every one: his "one last night" (grounded), his stuff in our chest
  (hiding it), his house gone.
- Until finally the last growing row leads to my door, where Jonah puts down the last torch on the nightly spot, waves,
  and walks away without "see you tomorrow".

## Twist checklist (docs/story_craft.md 3)
- [x] Changes what line 1 means: "SOMEONE" is Jonah; "COMING" becomes going.
- [x] Fair play: the habit (L06/S06), the words (L07), the last night (L08), the gifts (S10), the house (S12) and the
  row (S13-S15) all come before L16. The reveal adds no new fact.
- [x] Planted at least twice, once in the picture (S06, S08, S10, S12, S15).
- [x] Misread, not hidden (L04, L09, L11).
- [x] Someone gets ahead (~30 s, L12) without being sure (nothing says "leaving" until the missing words).
- [x] Agency: the kid follows the row; Jonah's every act has a reason (the habit).
- [x] No pedant hole: the story never claims anything about spawning beyond "one torch lights a yard" and "never in the
  dark" (both true while lit).
