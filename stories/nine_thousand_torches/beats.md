## The ending first
**The reveal frame:** sunrise, through a villager kid's low eyes. Jonah (Steve, his name over his head) stands on the
kid's doorstep, a fresh torch burning on the grass beside the door, the spot where he put one every night. In the next
frame he is gone, and the chat says in yellow **Jonah left the game**, with no "<Jonah> see you tomorrow" above it.
Past the house, every block of the village carries a torch.

**The first frame it re-explains:** a dawn crane over a whole village covered in flames. Before the reveal it reads
as a fortress against something coming. After it, it's Jonah's nightly torch, done for every night he won't be here.

## Spine
- **Every day,** Jonah, the player who lives in the village, plays with his best friend, a villager kid. Every night
  he puts a torch by the kid's door, types "see you tomorrow" and logs off.
- **One day,** Jonah says his mom made him promise, one last night. Next morning there's a torch on every block of the
  village, six thousand of them, and the kid decides something big must be coming.
- **Because of that,** he finds all of Jonah's things in his family's chest and Jonah's house gone. One row of torches
  is still growing round a corner, and he has to know who's placing it.
- **Until finally,** the row ends at his own door. Jonah places the last torch on the nightly spot, waves, and leaves
  the game without "see you tomorrow".

## The second the viewer gets ahead
~28.6 s (L11, 72%). By then the viewer has "one last night" (L07), his whole inventory, bed included, in our chest
(S09), and his house taken down (S11). A player knows what that looks like: a friend quitting. L12-L14 are suspense:
the viewer can guess where the row ends and what Jonah will do there. And because S06 showed the nightly torch and the
ordinary "left the game", the viewer can also guess what the last torch is.

## The twist checklist (docs/story_craft.md section 3)
- [x] **It changes what line 1 means.** Before, someone fortified the village against something coming. After, my
  best friend did his nightly torch for every night he won't be here.
- [x] **Fair play.** The rule (L03), the nightly torch and "see you tomorrow" (L06/S06), "one last night" (L07), the
  gifts (S09), the house (S11) and the row to my door (S13) all come before L15. The reveal adds no new fact, and the
  ordinary "Jonah left the game" was already shown in S06.
- [x] **Planted at least twice, at least once in the picture.** Two plants in lines (L06, L07) and four in the picture
  (S06 the door torch and chat ~4 s, S09 ~2 s, S11 ~1.9 s, S13 ~1.5 s).
- [x] **Misread, not hidden.** L04/L10 "something's coming / he's hiding it" and L08 "grounded a lot". The kid has no
  theory for the house (L11), which is honest.
- [x] **Someone gets ahead.** ~28.6 s (72%).
- [x] **The game says it.** A player quits and the server prints "Jonah left the game". S06 set the pattern ("<Jonah>
  see you tomorrow" then the leave), so S15's missing line is visible in the chat itself. No spoken "he quit forever".
- [x] **One step.** No "see you tomorrow" means no tomorrow. The torches, the chest and the house all follow.
- [x] **Readable at 300 px.** The chat is small, so the caption track repeats "Jonah left the game" in yellow during the
  hold, and the picture says it too: he's there, then he isn't.
- [x] **Visible causes.** Every torch has a placer: Jonah with torches in S06-S08, his arm at the corner in S12, the
  placement on screen in S14. The vanish is a quit, which the game shows by its own rule.
- [x] **A rewatch plays differently.** L04 "Something BIG must be COMING": something big was going. L06 "Every
  night, he puts a TORCH by my door": the whole video is that line. L08 "He's grounded a LOT": in a way, forever. L10
  "He's HIDING it": he's giving it. L13 "to MY door": his last stop.
