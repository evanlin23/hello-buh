# nine_thousand_torches: critic, round 1

Verdict: **REVISE** (overall 7.0/10; Twist and Fairness under 8)

**Cold read.** A villager kid wakes to a torch on every block, thinks something is coming, then finds his player-friend Milo's stuff in his chest and Milo's house gone, and follows a row of torches home. Milo places the last one, waves and leaves the game: he's quitting for good. That I get. Why torches mean goodbye takes a second step the script never sets up.

**Scores**
- Hook 9: one picture, a number, one breath, "why tf" asks itself.
- Second beat 7: L02 widens the picture but doesn't raise the stakes.
- Mystery 7: one idea per beat. The clues build (chest, house, row).
- Twist 6: L04 flips ("coming" becomes "going") and the game says it. But the torches' meaning rests on a hole (MF1).
- Fairness 6: "one last night" (L07, misread as grounded), the chest (L09, misread as hiding), the house (L11, no theory) and the row (L13) are all fair. Never shown: that Milo ever protects anyone.
- Rewatch 8: L04, L06, L10.
- Agency 6: the kid never states a want. He only follows.
- Character 7: L07 is good subtext. Milo stays unvoiced, as the rules ask.
- Delivery 8: 117 words, ~36.5 s, no "...", L01 one breath, L15 slowed.
- Visual 7: wide by default, S01 is the count shot. S12/S13 can't be staged as written (MF3).
- Continuity 6: where is Milo during S13?
- Owner fit 7: natural terrain, narrator-first, the ending explains itself, the out-of-context read is clean. It fails the "why?" test.
- Against runaway: worse. Runaway's rule (creepers hiss) explains every clue. Here the rule (L03) doesn't explain the motive.

**Must-fixes**
1. **L06, the "why?" hole.** "Every night he says see you TOMORROW and logs OFF": the village already survives every night without him, so why spawn-proof it because he's leaving? Make his nightly habit the torch. L06: "Every night, he puts a TORCH by my door. Then, see you TOMORROW." Then 9,000 reads as "he did every night at once", the last torch by the door (S14) rhymes, and L04's misread gets a reason. Restage S06: Milo places a torch, then waves.
2. **Originality vs light_sleeper** (approved): "My village is covered in NINE THOUSAND CARPETS." / "The ROADS. The STAIRS. Even the DOGHOUSE." Same number, same L02 pattern. Change the count, and L02 to: "Fences. Roofs. Hay bales. Not one dark BLOCK."
3. **S12-S14 staging.** The torches appear in open grass, placed by someone "hidden behind the corner": impossible. Grow the row round the corner instead (an arm's swing). In S13, show Milo kneeling at the doorstep once we clear the corner. End the row one block short, so the S14 torch closes it.

**Should-fixes**
- S06: let him vanish with "Milo left the game" (the nightly logoff). Then the reveal is the same message with no "see you tomorrow".
- L12: give the kid a want: "I had to know WHO."
- Expect comments that mobs only spawn near a player.
