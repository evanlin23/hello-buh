# nine_thousand_torches: critic, round 1

Verdict: **REVISE** (overall 7.0/10; Twist and Fairness under 8)

**Cold read.** A villager kid wakes to a torch on every block and finds his player-friend Milo's stuff in his chest and Milo's house gone. At the kid's door Milo places the last torch, waves and quits the game for good. The leaving is clear. Why the torches mean goodbye is a second step the script never sets up.

**Scores**
- Hook 9: one picture, a number, one breath.
- Second beat 7: L02 widens the picture but doesn't raise the stakes.
- Mystery 7: one idea per beat.
- Twist 6: L04 flips ("coming" becomes "going") and the game says it. The motive has a hole (MF1).
- Fairness 6: L07, L09, L11 and L13 are fair. We never see Milo protect anyone.
- Rewatch 8: L04, L06, L10.
- Agency 6: the kid states no want. He only follows.
- Character 7: L07 is good subtext.
- Delivery 8: 117 words, ~36.5 s, no "...".
- Visual 7: wide by default. S12/S13 can't be staged as written.
- Continuity 6: Milo's position in S13 isn't set.
- Owner fit 7: terrain, narrator-first and the out-of-context read pass. It fails "why?".
- Against runaway: worse. Its rule explains every clue; L03 doesn't explain the motive.

**Must-fixes**
1. **L06 "why?" hole.** If Milo logs off every night, the village already survives nights without him. Why light it because he's leaving? Make his nightly habit the torch. L06: "Every night, he puts a TORCH by my door. Then, see you TOMORROW." Then 9,000 is every night at once and S14 rhymes. Restage S06: he places the torch, then waves.
2. **Originality vs light_sleeper** (approved): "NINE THOUSAND CARPETS" / "The ROADS. The STAIRS. Even the DOGHOUSE." Same number, same L02 pattern. Change the count, and L02 to: "Fences. Roofs. Hay bales. Not one dark BLOCK."
3. **S12-S14 staging.** Torches appear in open grass, placed by someone "hidden behind the corner": impossible. Grow the row round the corner (an arm's swing). In S13, show Milo kneeling at the door once we clear the corner. End the row one block short so S14's torch closes it.

**Should-fixes**
- S06: let him vanish with "Milo left the game", so the reveal repeats the message without "see you tomorrow".
- L12: give the kid a want: "I had to know WHO."
