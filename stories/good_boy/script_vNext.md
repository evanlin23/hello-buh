Format rules:

- **One narrator says every line,** including the other characters' quotes.
- **Captions:** every ` / ` chunk is 2-3 words with exactly one CAPS key word (validated).
- **Not voiced:** the narrator voice is being re-cast.

- **Arc:** T4 Who Am I (runaway's shape, the clues thinned); a cause-and-effect loop.
- **The question (by 3 s):** why can't he ever get more than 12 blocks from this guy?
- **Loop:** Cause and effect. The reveal (he's the guy's tamed wolf, red collar, leaping at the zombie) is why line 1's 37 escapes all end next to him.
- **The reveal and the cut:** The camera pulls out of POV: a pale wolf with a red collar, eyes red, leaps at the zombie; the zombie drops. Cut 1.0 s after (no "GOOD boy", no "Tomorrow I'm leaving").
- **What changes from the current version:** The giveaways are cut: SIT, raw porkchops, the door, "Come on IN, you" and "GOOD boy" (the owner: "sensual asmr" and "wtf is the beat at the end"). The man's lines are now narrated (one voice); he is Buh at 16 per canon plant 7, never named. ~22 s: short on purpose (the old 60 s was the problem). It needs re-recorded shots for the pull-out reveal; the rest reuses POV.
- **Length:** 100 words, ~19 s spoken at 5.25 words/s, plus ~1.5-2 s of reveal.
