# light_sleeper: beat sheet, clue map, writer's checks (r2)

r2 (2026-11-10) applies the r1 critic's must-fixes: the carpet rule holds to the end (he steps off the carpet onto the
well's bare stone to dance), the well wears a white wool lid from S01, pulling the wool off is the vibration (the egg
is gone), "Wells ECHO" gives the jukebox its reason, our door is on the plaza 8 blocks from the well (S13 is
shootable), the disc goes in on screen, S01 is a street-level wide with the hill crane moved to L02, and the Darkness
only starts once the cameras are close.

## The ending first
The reveal frame: a small boy, back to camera, jumping on the bare stone rim of a well beside a jukebox, the lid torn
open, and the warden rising out of the carpet on the far side, roaring, the square pulsing black. The first frame it
re-explains: a carpeted street running down into a carpeted plaza, a wool-boxed bell and a well with a white wool lid.
Everything between plants silence.

## Spine
- **Every day,** the boy's village whispers: nine thousand carpets, three hundred sheep sheared every morning, a bell
  in a box of wool, a well with a wool lid, and Mom's "walk soft, the ground's a light sleeper".
- **One day,** a skeleton shooting at him hits a creeper, and he gets a music disc in a village where nobody has ever
  heard a song.
- **Because of that,** he pulls the lid off the well (wells echo), puts a jukebox on it and the disc in, and ignores
  the "fox" screaming at the bottom, and Mom's whisper, and the lights going dark.
- **Until finally,** he steps off the carpet onto the bare stone rim to dance, the fourth shriek calls the warden, and
  the game says what the carpet was for.

## "Why?" interrogation
- Why the carpet, the bell box, the lid? The twist: silence over an ancient city.
- Why did he pull the wool off the first time? A kid's curiosity about a covered well ("Once" = once, as kids do).
- Why does Mom lie? A mother protecting a small child from a monster under the village; "a fox" is a thing foxes do.
- Why the well for the jukebox? "Wells ECHO": he wants everyone to hear it, and the well is the middle of everything.
- Why doesn't he stop at the scream? He was told it's a fox, and tonight is the best night of his life ("I didn't CARE").
- Why doesn't Mom run to him? "Don't. Move." is what you do near a warden; she obeys her own rule, frozen in the door.
- Why does he climb onto the rim? To dance on a stage: the one bare stone in the village, next to the music.
- Why did the skeleton shoot a creeper? It was shooting at him; the creeper walked into the arrow (S09 shows it).
