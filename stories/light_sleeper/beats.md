# light_sleeper: beat sheet, clue map, writer's checks

## The ending first
The reveal frame: a small boy, back to camera, next to a jukebox on a well, and the warden risen out of the carpet
between them, roaring, the whole square pulsing dark. The first frame it re-explains: a rainbow carpet village seen
from the hill. Everything between plants silence.

## Spine
- **Every day,** the boy's village whispers: nine thousand carpets, three hundred sheep sheared every morning, a bell
  in a box of wool, and Mom's "walk soft, the ground's a light sleeper".
- **One day,** a skeleton shoots a creeper and he gets a music disc, in a village where nobody has ever heard a song.
- **Because of that,** he puts a jukebox on the well, the one place everyone will hear it, and ignores the "fox"
  screaming at the bottom, and Mom's whisper, and the lights going dark.
- **Until finally,** he dances, the fourth shriek calls the warden, and the game says what the carpet was for.

## Twist checklist (docs/story_craft.md section 3)

- [x] **Fair play.** The shriek, its Darkness and the glow are on screen before the reveal; the warden is the
      vanilla consequence of a shrieker.
- [x] **Planted twice, one in the picture.** Bell box (S04), glow (S06), shriek and Darkness (S07, S12, S14).

- [x] **The game says it.** The warden rising, the roar, the sonic boom, the death message.
- [x] **One step.** Noise wakes the thing under the ground.

      the caption track too.

      PARTY", L15 "I'd never DANCED before".
