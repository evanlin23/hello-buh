# wall_grows: beat sheet (r2)

Notes only. The script is `story.json` (the build and the voice read only that).

## The ending first (the reveal frame)
From the top of the wall at dawn, a long lens 40 blocks down: a player (Steve) at the door of a glass-roofed trading
hall, a 2-high dirt pillar started beside him, dirt in his hand. He turns his head up to us. Cut on the look.

## Spine
- Every morning, old Tomas the mason raises the 40-block wall one block above the brown thing that grows over the rim.
- One day the kid notices a block went missing and that Tomas hides from the old minecart.
- Because of that, he asks what's outside, gets "Nothing we want to trade with", and climbs Tomas's ladder before dawn.
- Until finally he sees "another village" where everybody gets their own room (a trading hall), and the player at its
  door looks up at him.

## Twist checklist
- Naivety (STANDARDS 0a): the kid is proud and never afraid, never hints, never explains; every clue has its innocent read above.
- Turns line 1 over: the wall isn't for monsters; it's for players (the viewer).
- Planted 6+ times, two in the picture only (the dirt rising; the dirt in his hand).
- Said in no line: the picture does it (the hall, the player).
- One mechanic: players box villagers into trading halls. Every clue is a real player habit.
- Agency: the kid climbs because Tomas won't answer; nothing happens by accident.
- Vanilla: zombies jump one block, not two (L02 is true); endermen can't pick up stone bricks; any mob climbs a ladder it
  walks into (the kid's climb); villagers placing blocks and talking are the style choices (Tomas is a mason).
