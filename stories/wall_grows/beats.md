Notes only. The script is `story.json` (the build and the voice read only that).

## The ending first (the reveal frame)
From the top of the wall at dawn, over the kid's head, down the wall's OUTSIDE: a player (Steve) standing on top of a
dirt column against the wall, just under the rim; behind him the glass-roofed trading hall, a hundred villagers in
one-block cells. The kid SAYS it: "A man was standing on the brown plant, and behind him were a hundred of us, each in a
tiny BOX." One beat: he looks up and puts down ONE more (line 1's "ONE block every morning"), rising level with the rim. Cut ~1 s after.

## Spine
- Every morning, old Tomas the mason raises the 40-block wall one block above the brown thing that grows over the rim,
  and nobody will tell the kid why (line 1).
- One day the kid notices a block went missing and that Tomas hides from the old minecart.
- Because of that, he asks what's outside and gets "Nothing we want to trade with."
- Then one morning Tomas, whom he loves, is too tired to get up; the kid takes his brick and climbs to do it for him.
- Until finally he sees the man on the brown plant and the hundred in boxes, and the man looks up and puts down one more.

## Twist checklist
- Naivety (STANDARDS 0a): the kid is proud and never afraid, never hints, never explains; every clue has its innocent read above.
- Turns line 1 over: the wall isn't for monsters; it's for players (the viewer).
- Planted 6+ times, two in the picture only (the dirt rising; the dirt in his hand).
- Said in one plain line (L15, docs/story_format.md beat 6), over the picture that proves it; the kid says what he sees, never what it means.
- One mechanic: players box villagers into trading halls. Every clue is a real player habit.
- Agency: the kid climbs because Tomas can't (and because he wants to see); nothing happens by accident.
- Vanilla: zombies jump one block, not two (L02 is true); endermen can't pick up stone bricks; any mob climbs a ladder it
  walks into (the kid's climb); villagers placing blocks and talking are the style choices (Tomas is a mason).
