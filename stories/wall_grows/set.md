# wall_grows: set brief and the narration's claims (r1)

Two sides of one wall: the village inside (S01-S10) and the trading hall outside (S11-S12). Built on real terrain (a
shallow plains valley, STANDARDS 3.1: no superflat), at full scale with `/fill`. Every build follows
`docs/building_guide.md` (7+, the wall 8+ as the focal build). Coordinates: bible.md "Places".

## Pieces
| piece | notes |
|---|---|
| the wall | square ring, 2 thick, 40 high above the village ground (follow the terrain: the top is level at y0+40, so the wall is taller where the ground dips, never shorter than 40 inside); stone bricks with age bands: rows 1-12 mossy + cracked mixed, 13-30 plain with a few cracked, 31-40 clean; capping single bricks at y0+41 every 3-6 blocks along the north rim only |
| Tomas's ladder | on the north wall's inner face at x -4, rows 1-40, ending on the rim |
| the village | a plains village of 10+ houses (library pieces), plaza with bell and well, paths, lanterns, a wheat farm along the north wall with a 2-high cobblestone wall around it |
| the rail stub | inside: rail from the farm north to the wall's foot at x 6, an empty minecart at its end; outside: the same line continues from the wall's outer foot to the hall |
| the trading hall | 52 x 12, stone-brick base, glass roof at y0+3 and a glass south front; 4 rows x 25 cells, each 1 x 1 x 2 with a villager and a workstation (lectern, composter, smithing table, barrel... mixed); an oak door centred in the south front facing the wall |
| the player's dirt stub | 2 dirt blocks beside the hall door (S12); for S04 a separate dirt pillar against the wall's outer face at x -10, topping out at y0+41..42 |
