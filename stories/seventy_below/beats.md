# seventy_below: beat sheet (r2, 2026-11-11; r2 cut L07 and changed L08 and L12 after the first cold read)

**Ending first (the reveal frame, S15):** a low wide from the room's south-east corner. Seventy zombies, the baby
in the front row, all with their faces turned up to one square of moonlight in the ceiling. An old man's dark head
fills half the square. "Seventy." The lid goes back on and the room goes black. Cut.

**Spine.** Every night the general opens the hole in his yard and counts the seventy of them, and the littlest
zombie is sure they're his army, waiting for orders. One morning he opens it in daylight and Brother Seventy runs
to him, into the sun, and burns: "Sixty-nine." Because of that, a spider climbs the general's wall that night (the
narrator thinks it came looking for Brother Seventy), and the next night the general brings a new zombie and drops
it down the hole himself. Until finally the count is "Seventy" again and nothing climbs the wall: they were never an
army. They are the seventy monsters that fill his mob cap, so nothing else can spawn near him or the village.
