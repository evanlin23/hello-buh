# seventy_below: beat sheet (r3, 2026-11-11)

**Ending first (the reveal frame, S16):** a lens on the room floor beside the hole, looking nearly straight up the
shaft. Upturned zombie faces ring the frame; in the centre, a square of moonlight and an old man's dark head in it.
"Seventy." The lid goes back on and the frame goes black. Cut.

**Spine.** Every night the general opens the hole in his yard and counts the seventy of them, and the littlest
zombie is sure they're his army, so many that the world must be full of them. One night he's too sleepy to count and
comes in the morning instead: Brother Seventy runs to him, into the sun, and burns. "Sixty-nine." Because of that,
that night they hear a spider on the general's wall, the first one ever, and the next night the general brings a
new soldier and tips it down the hole himself. Until finally the count is "Seventy" again and they never hear
another: they were never an army. They are the seventy monsters that fill his mob cap, so nothing else can spawn
near him or the village.
