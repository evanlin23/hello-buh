# red_sheep: continuity bible (r1)

## World
- **Place:** Ravenford (canonical: `ravenford` in docs/world/map.json): the pasture inside the log palisade, the east
  gate, Grandpa's house with the totem wall, and the Ridge woods east of the gate toward the old tower.
- **Day:** 2100, after Thirty-One Raids (1900). Kid Buh is about 12. Grandpa and Buh have fought raids together since
  raid 32.
- **New canon facts** (add to canon.md when this is made):
  - from ~1950 Ravenford's flock is dyed blue;
  - an evoker lives in the Ridge woods (where it came from is never said: a leftover raider is the fan theory);
  - the lamb is lost.
- **Never:** say "Buh"; name the evoker; explain the dye.

## Positions and screen direction
The woods are east, and the village is west of them:
- the gate shots look east;
- in S11 Buh faces east, with the evoker ahead between the trunks and the lamb between them, ~5 blocks ahead of Buh
  and ~5 from the evoker. That puts the lamb inside the evoker's 16-block spell range.
