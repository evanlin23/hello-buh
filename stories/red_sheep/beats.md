# red_sheep: beat sheet (r4, 2026-11-11)

**Ending first (the reveal frame, S13):** a low wide over the kid's shoulder in the birch woods. The blue lamb 2.5
blocks ahead; 8 blocks beyond it an evoker with its arms raised, brown-orange particles at its hands. The lamb turns
RED (the red one she came to find). The arms drop, it turns to us, a line of fangs snaps up out of the ground toward
the camera. Cut.

**Spine.** Every day Dad dyes every sheep blue and counts them every night, and his kid thinks he just loves blue.
One spring a sheep came back from the woods red, the prettiest one ever, and Dad sat at the gate with a crossbow
(waiting for more, she thinks). Because of that, when she forgets the gate and her lamb gets out, she follows it into
the woods Dad is "so boring" about, happily, to find more red ones. Until finally her lamb turns red in front of her:
the alarm goes off with her standing in it.

**Naivety (STANDARDS 0a, r:rb89776).** Every clue gets the kid's innocent reading on a line; she is bright and happy
to the last word; she never hints, fears or explains. The viewer sees past every reading.

**Where the attentive viewer gets ahead:** L07 (a crossbow for a week is not about wanting more red sheep): "red means
danger in the woods". Players may get the evoker at L08 (the fang snaps) or L12 (the wololo). Nobody can be sure what
turns sheep red until S13.

## How it differs from Light Sleeper (same shape: a hidden mob shown at the end)
- **The reveal is a visible rule, not a monster.** The colour change happens on screen, cause and effect in one
  frame; the evoker confirms what the red already said. Light Sleeper's reveal is the warden itself.
- **Nobody lies.** Light Sleeper's Mom hides the truth ("it was a FOX"). Here Dad says it straight ("They're our
  EYES"); the kid just reads it wrong, and the irony is her wish: she goes in to find a red one and gets one.
- **She causes nothing.** Light Sleeper's kid wakes the warden with music. Here the alarm goes off with her standing
  in its range.
- **Look and sound.** A bright pastoral day and a blue/red colour key, the scare outside in low sun (not a carpeted
  night village, not a sound key, not underground).
- **Release.** Keep them apart in the order (the audit's must-change 3).
