# monsters_nearby: beat sheet and clue map

**Logline.** For nine nights a boy's bed has refused him because a monster is near, and when he finally sends his
grandma away to keep her safe, the bed lets him sleep.

**The reveal frame (written first).** Lightning behind the garden gate. Under the gate-post lantern, Grandma has
stopped and turned back toward the bedroom window: a witch, hat and purple robe, one block past the torch ring that
marks the bed's reach. The bed has just let him lie down.

**The first frame it re-explains.** An empty, lantern-lit bedroom and the action bar: *You may not rest now; there
are monsters nearby.* After the twist: the monster was never hiding. She was sitting in the chair beside the bed.

**Spine.**
1. Every night, my bed refuses me: a monster is within eight blocks, and I can't find it.
2. One day the phantoms find me, and I've searched every block in range.
3. Because of that, I send Grandma to the neighbors so she'll be safe while I catch it.
4. Until finally, as she walks past eight blocks, the bed lets me sleep, and the lightning shows me what she is.

**The vanilla rules it runs on** (all real, all things players know):
- A bed refuses you with *"You may not rest now; there are monsters nearby"* if a hostile mob is within 8 blocks
  horizontally and 5 vertically. Witches are hostile.
- A villager struck by lightning turns into a witch.
- Witches drink a healing potion when they're hurt, and giggle (their ambient sound).
- Witch huts come with a black cat and a cauldron.
- Players who don't sleep for three nights get phantoms.

## Twist checklist (docs/story_craft.md section 3)

- [x] **Changes line 1.** "My bed has said there are monsters nearby": the monster is the person who tucks him in.
- [x] **Fair play.** The rule (L02), the storm (L06), lightning rod (S04, S06), the giggle, cat and cauldron, the potion
  (L08) are all shown before S14. The reveal adds no fact: it only shows her.
- [x] **Planted twice, one in the picture.** Lightning: S04 rod, S06 roof (picture) and L06 (line). Witch: giggle
  (sound) x2, cat and cauldron (picture), potion (picture + line).
- [x] **Misread, not hidden.** Each plant is read by the narrator as a grandma thing (scared of storms, old, medicine,
  love).
- [x] **Someone gets ahead.** L08 for players, L12 for everyone.
- [x] **The game says it.** The bed works (no message) the moment she's past the torch line, and the witch is in frame.
  Nobody says "she's a witch".
- [x] **One step.** Grandma is the monster. (The cause, lightning, was already planted.)
- [x] **Readable at 300 px.** S14 is a full shot pushed in to FOV 40: a witch's hat and robe against a lightning sky
  under a lantern; plus the caption track carries "...EIGHT." and silence. Check the frame at 300 px wide.
- [x] **Visible causes.** No accidents. The lightning strike is backstory, shown by its damage (S06). Grandma leaves
  because he asks her to; the storm on the last night is weather (`weather: thunder`).
- [x] **A rewatch plays differently.** L01 (the monster is the narrator's carer), L05 ("In MY house"), L09 ("Where I can
  WATCH you"), L11 ("If you're SURE"), and S01's empty chair.

**Risks for the table read.** (1) `minecraft-audit`: a witch is hostile and would throw potions at a player. The story
makes it a choice (she loves him and keeps her distance from the bed: the chair, not the bed), the same licence
runaway's creeper mom has. (2) If a cold reader thinks the monster is "something in the yard" because the bed works
when she's outside, S14 must frame her alone, clearly the only figure past the torches. (3) Phantoms are themselves
monsters: they are only ever shown high above the roof (out of the 5-block vertical reach).
