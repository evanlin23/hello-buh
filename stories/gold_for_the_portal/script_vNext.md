- **One narrator says every line,** including the other characters' quotes.
- **Captions:** every ` / ` chunk is 2-3 words with exactly one CAPS key word (validated).

- **Arc:** T3 Sinister Reread and T2 (the gifts read two ways); a cause-and-effect loop.
- **The question (by 3 s):** why tf has grandma thrown 9,000 gold ingots into a portal?
- **Loop:** It ends mid-action on "and SOMETHING / stepped OUT". What steps out (a piglin in Grandpa's gold helmet with the blue stripe) is why line 1's gold has gone in every night: it's been trading with him.
- **The reveal and the cut:** The piglin in the blue-striped gold helmet steps out of the portal, shaking; cut 1.0 s after the helmet's stripe reads (no 'sent home' epilogue).
- **What changes from the current version:** "Nico" is gone (the owner: "Who is nico?"). Grandma's line is now narrated with "sweetie". One voice. Same shots.
- **Length:** 131 words, ~25 s spoken at 5.25 words/s, plus ~1.5-2 s of reveal.
