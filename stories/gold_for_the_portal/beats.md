## The ending first
The reveal frame: a piglin, head and shoulders, shaking, lit purple from behind, wearing a gold helmet with a blue
stripe across the front: the helmet we watched Grandpa put on. In the last second a gold ingot sails past its head into
the purple, thrown by Grandma from the ladder, and the piglin's head turns after the gold. Cut.
The first frame it re-explains: Grandma throwing one gold ingot into a Nether portal in a cellar. Not a widow's ritual
for a lost husband: she is paying the piglin that has his helmet, and has for 9,000 nights.
Everything between plants trading: the gifts are the barter list, gold keeps you safe from piglins, "he's still
trading", "one's plenty".

## Spine
- **Every day,** Grandma throws one gold ingot into the Nether portal in the cellar, and every morning a gift comes
  back out of it: obsidian, string, ender pearls, a fire resistance potion.
- **One day** (25 years ago), Grandpa put on her blue-striped gold helmet and went in, and never came back; so the grandson,
  who keeps the gifts in a barrel, knows Grandpa is alive over there, trading.
- **Because of that,** when Grandma says one ingot is plenty and goes to bed, the grandson throws in all 64 to buy Grandpa's
  way home, and sits on the bed to wait.
- **Until finally,** something steps out after the gold: a piglin, shaking, wearing Grandpa's helmet, and Grandma,
  who has known all along, tosses one more ingot into the purple to send it home.

## "Why?" interrogation
- **Why did Grandpa go into the Nether?** For gold and adventure; the cellar portal was his. (Not said; not needed.)
- **Why the helmet?** Grandma's gift for the trip: gold keeps piglins calm (vanilla), so it is the right gift.
- **What happened to him?** Not shown and never said: he died over there long ago, on the ledge in front of his
  portal; the helmet dropped where he fell and a piglin picked it up (piglins pick up and wear gold armour). Nothing
  grim is on screen; the helmet is the only thing that comes back.
- **Why do the barters come through, when a piglin's barter lands on its own side?** The Nether side of Grandpa's
  portal is a narrow ledge over a lava sea. A piglin with no player near throws its barter toward random ground nearby;
  on the ledge that is the strip in front of the portal and the portal itself, and an item that flies into the portal
  comes out in the cellar (vanilla: items cross portals). Most nights it comes through; the misses fall in the lava,
  which is why the barrel holds the best of 9,000, not 9,000.
- **Why does Grandma keep feeding it?** Years ago she went through to look for him and found the piglin by his portal
  wearing his helmet. It was the last thing in either world that had him, and it kept sending a gift back for every
  ingot. She is the only one who feeds it gold.
- **Why doesn't she tell the grandson?** He's nine and loves the gifts; "he's still trading" lets him keep his grandpa. Every
  word she says is true.
- **Where does 9,000 gold come from?** Grandpa's own hoard: the gold chest beside the portal (blocks of gold, broken
  down nightly), now low. Seen, not said (S01's background).
- **Why "Just one, one's plenty"?** One ingot buys one barter; she knows more could pull it through (piglins go after
  gold), and a piglin in the Overworld turns into a zombified piglin.
- **Why does the grandson throw in 64?** He wants Grandpa home and thinks more gold buys a bigger trade: "To buy his way HOME."
  His choice, after she said no and went to bed: agency.
- **Why does the piglin step through tonight, after 25 years?** A whole stack arrives at once; it lands in the portal
  frame on its side, and a piglin goes after every gold ingot it sees: walking into the frame for it takes it through.
- **Why does Grandma come down?** The snort and the hoofbeats on stone through the floor; she knows what that sound
  is, so she comes down with one more ingot, calm.
- **Why does she throw the ingot past it into the portal?** A piglin shakes and turns into a zombified piglin in the
  Overworld after ~15 s; piglins follow gold. She sends it home before it turns. Nobody fights it: the grandson wears the golden
  boots Grandma makes him wear in the cellar (seen in S05, S10, S13: piglins leave a player in gold alone), and
  Grandma is a villager (piglins ignore villagers).
- **Why doesn't the grandson run?** He's sitting, frozen, looking at Grandpa's helmet: the line is what stops him.
