# spawn_room: beat sheet, clue map, writer's checks

## The ending first
The reveal frame: a compass held out at an empty block beside a boy's bed, and a stranger popping into being on
exactly that block, nose to nose with us, as "Noor joined the game" rises in the chat. The first frame it re-explains:
a blank stranger standing by the same bed at night. The sting: twelve of them punching the last trees at dawn.

## Spine
- **Every day,** strangers appear beside the boy's bed, say nothing, walk outside and punch a tree; the forest round
  the cottage floats, and his grandpa, the cartographer, says "everybody starts somewhere".
- **One day,** he notices every compass in the village points at his bed, and Grandpa says compasses point home.
- **Because of that,** he takes a compass and walks for three days to find where the strangers come from, and the
  needle keeps pointing behind him.
- **Until finally,** he follows it home to his bed, and the world's newest players arrive on that block, twelve at a
  time.

## Twist checklist (docs/story_craft.md section 3)
- [x] **Changes line 1.** "Forty-one strangers have appeared next to my bed" goes from a home invasion to "my bed is
      where the world begins".
- [x] **Fair play.** Compasses, respawning, tree-punching and floating trees are shown before the reveal; the join
      message and the spawn are vanilla.
- [x] **Planted twice, one in the picture.** Floating trees (S04, every exterior), the compass wall (S08), the needle
      (S11, S12).
- [x] **Misread, not hidden.** A custom, bad luck, grandpa's wisdom, a broken compass.
- [x] **Someone gets ahead.** Players: "new players" at S04 (~12 s), "world spawn" at S08 (~27 s, 54%). Non-players: at
      L11's needle or the reveal. Early for players: the payoff is the scale and the sting, not the surprise alone (see
      the open questions).
- [x] **The game says it.** The spawn on the needle's block and "joined the game" x12.
- [x] **One step.** My bed is where new players start.
- [x] **Readable at 300 px.** A person appearing in front of the camera and the room filling are big; the chat lines go
      into the caption track as "NOOR JOINED THE GAME".
- [x] **Visible causes.** The strangers' deaths: a zombie in frame (S05). The walk: his choice. No accidents.
- [x] **A rewatch plays differently.** L01, L02 "They just APPEAR", L05 "looking UPSET", L07, L09.

## Delivery plan
- Deadpan narrator (Umbriel) against a gravelly, amused grandpa (Algenib): age, pitch and energy all differ.
- Pauses: before "forty-one" (L01), before "APPEAR" (L02), a held breath before L10, the long beat inside "To my...
  bed." (L13), the sigh before L14. Under S14, no voice: the join chime is silence in vanilla, so the chat lines
  scroll over the room tone, a low NCS bed (-31 LUFS) under the whole story, and the strangers' footsteps.
- Quotable line: "We're gonna need more TREES." (also: "Everybody starts SOMEWHERE.")

## Writer's self-check against the critic rubric (no agent available this run)
Hook 9 (a stranger by a child's bed: horror framing, a number), second beat 8 (they *appear*), mystery 8, twist 7
(players get ahead early; the scale and sting carry it), fairness 9, rewatch 8, agency 8 (a three-day journey by
choice), character 8 (Grandpa's two lines are the best subtext in the script), delivery 8, visual 8, continuity risk:
skins of the strangers across shots, the compass needle direction in every shot, day and night order. Against runaway:
worse on surprise for players, equal on image, better on comedy and character.

**Open questions for the table read.**
1. Does S04 give it away too early? Option: move L04 (floating trees) after L06, and keep the trees out of the window in
   S01 (the floating forest then lands as its own reveal-sized laugh at ~20 s).
2. Is the narrator's own nature a problem (he holds a compass and has a HUD, so is *he* a player)? The story never says;
   the bible treats him as a boy born in this world. The critic may want one line to settle it, or may want it left as a
   follow-up hook ("where did *I* spawn?").
3. Strangers' skins: 9 vanilla defaults (Steve, Alex, Ari, Efe, Kai, Makena, Noor, Sunny, Zuri) need the renderer to
   draw other players with their default skin (engine ask in `set.md`).
