# Saving Up for a Bastion: set (r4)

**Place:** `portal_cottage_nether` (claimed on this branch, docs/world/map.json, `dimension: the_nether`): the Nether
side of the Portal Cottage's cellar portal, about (-16, 80, -118) in Nether coordinates. Day 5000. Crimson Nether fog.
**Unsurveyed:** the Nether at this spot has never been recorded. The shelf, cliff and lake below are what the story
needs; the build reads the real terrain first and places the bastion with `/place structure` if the seed has none in
view (an engine/world gap, see the report).

## Pieces
- **The portal:** the one that formed when Grandpa walked through on day ~175 (obsidian, lit), on a netherrack shelf
  over a lava lake.
- **The barter tower** (the player's, finished day ~4998), about 10 blocks east of the portal, against a netherrack
  cliff:
  - a **base room** 7 x 7 x 4 (nether brick, a door on the west, a lantern), the output chest in its middle under the
    column (room for the camera, the player and the chest);
  - a **3 x 3 column** rising to about 11 blocks, its top level with a ledge on the cliff;
  - the **cell**, a 1 x 2 floor near the top: the piglin's full block straight under the **dropper** (in the ceiling,
    facing down, fed by a hopper on the roof), and beside it the **hopper** slot (where an adult's barters would be
    thrown), which feeds down a hopper line to the chest. The ingot falls into the piglin's own space and is picked up
    on contact. *(Open for the minecraft-audit: an ingot that lands while the baby is still admiring the last one
    could reach the hopper and put gold in the chest. Settle the exact design before the build.)*
  - one **glass window** on the cell's west side, looking down at the shelf, the portal and the lake;
  - the **catch hole**: a 1 x 1 hole on the cliff ledge, trapdoor-edged, chuting down into the cell's hopper side
    (how the baby got in);
  - the **sign** (from S07's day): an oak sign standing on the shelf below the window, facing west to the piglins at the portal: PLEASE / WORK;
- **The bastion remnant** across the lava lake (S03): blackstone, a few gold blocks, big on the skyline.
- **The cutaway** (S06, S11): the column's south wall hidden for the camera (a cinematic cutaway). Engine: per-shot
  hidden blocks (see the report).
