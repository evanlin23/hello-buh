# Saving Up for a Bastion: set (r1)

**Place:** `portal_cottage_nether` (claimed on this branch, docs/world/map.json, `dimension: the_nether`): the Nether
side of the Portal Cottage's cellar portal, about (-16, 80, -118) in Nether coordinates. Day 5000. Crimson Nether fog.

## Pieces
- **The portal:** the one Grandpa lit on day ~175 (an obsidian frame, lit), on a netherrack-and-basalt shelf over a
  lava lake.
- **The barter tower** (the player's, built by day ~4900), about 10 blocks east of the portal:
  - nether brick, 3 x 3 x 7;
  - top: the roof, a hopper over a dropper facing down into the cell (the gold comes from the player's stack);
  - the cell: 1 x 1 x 2 for one piglin; the piglin stands on a full block; the dropper fires down onto it, so the
    ingot reaches the piglin's own space and is picked up on contact before any hopper can take it;
  - one glass window on the cell's west side, looking down at the shelf and the portal;
  - the hopper line beside the piglin's block, where an adult's barters would be thrown, feeding the chest below;
  - the chest room under the cell (a door on the east), the chest straight under the hopper;
  - the catch hole: a 1 x 1 hole at the tower's foot on the west, edged with trapdoors, that drops a piglin into the
    cell (how the baby got in);
  - an oak sign under the window outside: PLEASE / WORK;
  - from S08 on, a 1-wide lava moat round the tower's foot.
  - **Open for the minecraft-audit:** the farm layout. A real design must make the dropped ingot reach the piglin
    before a hopper does. Settle it before the build; the story only needs the four things the lines name: the dropper
    above, the hopper "floor", the chest, the hole.
- **The bastion remnant** across the lava lake (S05): blackstone, a few gold blocks, big on the skyline.
- **The cutaway** (S11): the tower's south wall removed for the crane (a cinematic cutaway, the one non-diegetic view).
