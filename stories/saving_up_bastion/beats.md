# Saving Up for a Bastion: beats (r1, 2026-11-11)

**The reveal frame (written first):** a crane down a cutaway of the barter tower: the farm's chest, open and EMPTY,
straight under the hopper line, the baby piglin straight above it hugging an ingot; the dropper clicks, one more ingot
falls, the baby catches it. Cut.

**Spine.** Every six seconds a gold ingot falls on a baby piglin in its little room, and it keeps every one. One day
it chased an ingot down a hole and found the room. Because of that, someone up there keeps sending gold and gifts (a
new floor, a lava moat, a note), and the baby saves up for a bastion of its own. Until finally we see what the gifts
were: the baby is stuck in a player's barter farm, and babies never barter, so his chest is empty.
