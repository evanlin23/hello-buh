# Saving Up for a Bastion: beats (r4, 2026-11-12: the Roblox story format)

**The reveal frame (written first):** the barter tower's cutaway, as an L: the player slumped at his open, empty chest
bottom left; the hopper line up the left; the baby piglin top right, hugging an ingot under the dropper. The baby says
"All the gold he sent was up here, with ME." The dropper clicks, one more ingot lands in its
arms: "Four hundred and ONE." Cut.

**Spine.** Gold falls from the sky onto a baby piglin, four hundred so far, and it doesn't know why. One day a guy
threw it the first ingot and it chased it down a hole into its room. Because of that, the guy (its "biggest fan") keeps
sending gold and gifts (a new bowl, a note). Until finally the friend gets sad, the baby looks down to find out why,
and says what it sees: all the gold he sent is up here with it.

**The format (docs/story_format.md):** 1 fact + why (L01); 2 the rule in action, two-way clues (L02-L08); 3 the baby
wants to know (L01 "I don't know WHY", L09 "I had to find out WHY"); 4 someone loved at stake (its "biggest fan", sad,
L05/L09); 5 it goes to look (L11); 6 the reveal SAID (L12); 7 one beat (L13), cut; L13's "four hundred and one" echoes
L01's "four hundred".

**Where the viewer gets ahead:** a gamer at L06-L08 (a hopper, PLEASE WORK, bartering); a casual at L10 (his box gets
nothing). The reveal says the last piece out loud: the gold went to the baby instead of the box.

**The one sentence (casual):** "The gold was for the guy's trading machine, but the baby piglin kept all of it instead
of trading, so he got nothing." Fans' second layer: baby piglins never grow up, so "for when I'm big" never comes.
