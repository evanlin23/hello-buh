# Saving Up for a Bastion: beats (r2, 2026-11-11)

**The reveal frame (written first):** the barter tower's cutaway: the player slumped at his open, empty chest; straight
above it the hopper; beside the hopper, the baby piglin on its block hugging an ingot. The dropper clicks, one more
ingot drops onto the baby, it catches it. Cut.

**Spine.** Every six seconds a gold ingot falls on a baby piglin, and it keeps every one, saving up for a bastion.
One day somebody threw it the first ingot and it chased it down a hole into its room. Because of that, the somebody
keeps sending gold and gifts (a new floor, a lava moat, a note). Until finally we see what the gifts were for: the
baby is stuck in his barter farm, babies never barter, and his chest has never had a thing in it.

**Where the viewer gets ahead:** a gamer at L08 (the sign) or L09 (bartering); everyone at L10. The reveal adds the
last piece: the baby itself, right above his chest, is why it's empty.

**Why the first half hides the farm (table read r1):** the cold reader guessed "a barter farm" at 65 % from the
dropper, "generous" and the gravel line. r2 keeps the dropper off-frame in S01, moves "generous" and the gravel line
after the midpoint, and adds a fair false lead (the staring grown-ups: are they after its gold?).
