# pulled_away: set plan (r2)

- **Oakend** (map.json `oakend`, claimed; no pieces yet; its NitW bible puts the origin at z -1030, map.json at -1054:
  settle before either set is built). Needs: the lane's two cottages side by side (the kid's and Mom's on the left,
  Uncle's on the right as seen from the bank; S07, S07b, S08), doors shut and dark in the present; the short grassy run
  from the lane to the east bank (about 13 blocks, one small bump for S03); the bank path (S06).
- **The crossing**: Oakend's east edge to Fernbank's dock's end is 10-15 blocks of water; S09 needs at least the
  length of L09 (~4 s rowing). Never a bridge or pillar in the water. The man's own boat waits at the bank's edge.
- **Fernbank in the S04 flashback (about day 5700)**: the canon village, all dark except the librarian house's
  **west-wall** window (her house faces south; the `home` cottage at (-396, -1050) blocks a straight line from the bank,
  so S04's camera sits north of the shore path; check on a still). The librarian stands in that window.
- **Fernbank on day 6000** (a story stamp `fernbank_alive`, since 6000, added at the build): the **new quarter**, about
  8 oak and spruce cottages on the rise east of the church, hidden from Oakend's bank by the old houses and the church
  (check on a still from S04's camera position with the stamp on: nothing of it shows), every window and door lit,
  lanterns along the path from the dock's foot and over it; villagers with AI walking in to bed; the librarian stamp
  gets `until: 6000` (she is at the dock's foot in S11). The canon cameos stay: the golem by the well, the empty boat
  at the dock's end, the headstones.
