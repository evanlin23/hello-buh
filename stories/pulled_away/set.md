  two cottages side by side (the kid's and Mom's, Uncle's) and a small fenced field in a hollow; the dark oak ridge

  the claim farther west, never closer). No name tags on the family.

  along Oakend's north edge (never in frame) to the inlet's west bank at about (-452, 66, -1086), north of Oakend's
  footprint; the man's own boat waits there.
- **The crossing**: the bank to Fernbank's dock; S09 needs at least the length of L09 (~4 s rowing). Never a bridge
  or pillar in the water. Oakend's shore in S09's frame shows only trees, no villagers.

  house's **upstairs west window** (glass x -369, y 72-73, z -1052..-1051); the librarian upstairs at (-368.5, 71,
  -1051.5) facing west. S04/S04b's camera at about (-452, 66, -1076), clear of the `home` cottage (checked by building
  the pieces with lib/sets offline, continuity r2); confirm on a still.

  blocks of the dock's foot lit (lanterns inside, doors open), lanterns along the path and over the dock's foot,
  villagers with AI on the path going in to bed (25+ readable from S11's camera), new cottages for forty placed at the
  build with their footprint claimed in map.json (they don't fit between the church and x -323: put them on the
  north or south slope); the librarian stamp gets `until: 6000` (she is at the dock's foot in S11). The canon cameos
  stay: the golem by the well (a resident), the empty boat at the dock's end (the one easter egg, S10), the headstones (not framed).
