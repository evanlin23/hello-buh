# pulled_away: set plan (r1)

- **Oakend** (map.json `oakend`, claimed; no pieces yet): the lane of cottages east of the oak fence, the meadow
  climbing west to a dark oak treeline, the east bank down to the inlet. Needs: the kid's cottage (S07's doorway),
  Mom's and Uncle's cottages side by side on the lane (S08, doors shut, windows dark), a grassy run from the lane to the
  bank about 20-40 blocks long with one small rise (S03). Coordinates come from the Names in the Woods set if it is
  built; else claim the pieces with `node scripts/world.js` before the build.
- **The North Inlet crossing**: read the bank-to-dock width off the survey before the build (the hub map suggests
  open water between Oakend's east bank and Fernbank's dock; Fernbank's footprint starts at x -446). The swim must
  take at least the length of L09 (~4 s at swim speed: 8+ blocks of water). Never a bridge or pillar in the water.
- **Fernbank** (canon pieces): on day 6000 a new stamp `fernbank_alive` (since 6000): every existing house lit
  (lanterns inside, doors open), lanterns on posts along the dock and the main path, a lantern over the dock's foot,
  **new cottages** on the slopes (about 8 more, oak and spruce, so forty villagers look possible), villagers in
  doorways and walking in; the librarian moved from her door to the dock's foot for S11 (her S04 position: in her lit
  doorway, the canon stamp). Before 6000 (S04 at 12000 the same day): the old dark village. **S04 and S10 are the
  same evening**: the stamp must switch between them (S04 is staged with every new cottage's light off and its
  villagers inside and dark: the kid sees "the old EMPTY village" from 60+ blocks in shadow, with one lit door).
  Owner decision flagged: is it fair that the village is already full but dark at sunset (villagers indoors, lights
  out until dusk)? Alternative: S04 frames only the old part of the village, the new cottages behind the church.
- The canon cameos in place: the golem by the well, the fisherman's empty boat at the dock's end, the HELLOBUH
  headstones (all world.build stamps).
