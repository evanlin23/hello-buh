**The reveal frame (written first).** At dusk the boat bumps Fernbank's dock. The village the kid called empty has
every window lit; villagers walk in to their beds. At the dock's foot, under a lantern, Mom (fletcher hat), the uncle
(butcher) and the old librarian turn to the boat. Cut.

**The first frame it re-explains.** A man dragging a villager kid across the grass in a boat: to every player, a
villager on the way to a trading hall. After the reveal: a villager on the way home to her family, and to the
librarian who has been alone across the water for years.

**Spine.**
1. Every few days, a man pulls one of Oakend's villagers away in a boat, and none of them comes back.
2. One evening it's the kid's turn: number forty.
3. Because of that, she gets the best trip of her life: over the grass, across the water.
4. Until finally the boat bumps into the old empty village, every window lit, and Mom is waiting at the dock.

**The vanilla rules it runs on.** A villager put in a boat can't get out on its own; since 1.21 a boat can be put on a
lead and pulled, on land (slowly, scraping) and on water, as long as no player sits in it
([Boat#Lead](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Boat#Lead)). Dragging villagers in boats is how players stock trading halls and
breeders. At dusk villagers walk home to their beds.

**Where the mystery lives.** The viewer's own habit is the misdirect: a man, a villager in a boat, "number forty",
"none of them came back" all read as a trading hall. The one counter-clue is the man's line, said looking at the one
lit window across the water.

## The twist checklist
- One sentence: he isn't stocking a trading hall; he's filling the empty village across the water, so the old
  librarian isn't alone, and the kid's Mom and uncle are already there.
- Planted twice where a rewatch catches it: the lit window (S04, S04b), the man's line (L05), "They must LOVE it there".
- Shown by the game, not said: the lit village and Mom at the dock; no line explains it.
- Agency: every motion has a visible cause (the man pulls, swims; the boat bumps the dock).
- Fair to the canon: Fernbank has been empty but for the librarian and the golem since day 520 (Respawn Tax).
