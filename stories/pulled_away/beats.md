# pulled_away: beat sheet and clue map (r5)

r5 (2026-11-11): rewritten to the Roblox story format (docs/story_format.md, STANDARDS 0y, 0z) after a first viewer
was lost on Gold for the Portal. Line 1 now says the impossible fact and the question; the kid goes to find out; the
reveal is said in one plain sentence over S11; one beat that echoes line 1; cut. Kept: the premise, the twist,
Bramble Hollow / the inlet / Fernbank, the cast and voices, the lead's calls (a player, one egg, `pulled_man`), naivety.
Cut: S03 and "It's the best TRIP ever", "He's GOOD at counting", "My boat finally got to SWIM!".

r4: the family lives at Bramble Hollow (a homestead behind the ridge, out of sight of Fernbank), not Oakend.
r2: critic r1 and continuity r1 applied (reviews/).

**The reveal frame (written first).** At dusk the boat bumps Fernbank's dock. The village the kid called empty has
every window lit; villagers walk in to their beds. At the dock's foot, under a lantern, Mom (fletcher hat), the uncle
(butcher) and the old librarian turn to the boat. The kid says what she sees: "MOM was there, and my UNCLE, and the
lady from the WINDOW, and every house was FULL."

**The first line it re-explains.** "A player has taken thirty-nine villagers away in his boat. None came back." To every
player, a trading hall; to anyone, a kidnapper. After the reveal: none came back because they live with the lonely lady
now, and the kid isn't going back either.

**The vanilla rules it runs on.** A mob that walks into a boat gets in and can't get out on its own; since 1.21 a boat can
be put on a lead and pulled, on land and on water, as long as no player sits in it
([Boat#Lead](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Boat#Lead)). Dragging villagers in boats is how players stock trading halls. At
dusk villagers walk home to their beds.

## The twist checklist
- One sentence: he isn't stealing villagers; he's moving them, one by one, into the empty village across the water so
  the lonely lady in the window isn't alone, and the kid's Mom and uncle are already there.
- The reveal is said by the kid naming what she sees (L08); the viewer makes the one jump (the lady from the window +
  every house full = he filled her village). No mechanic needed; "trading hall" is a bonus read for players only.
- Planted twice where a rewatch catches it: the lit window (S04, S04b) with the man's line (L05); "They must LOVE it
  there" (L03).
- Loop: L09 "None of them came BACK" echoes L01 "NONE came BACK", and answers its "WHERE?".
- Agency: she got in herself (L02); every motion has a visible cause (the man pulls, rows; the boat bumps the dock).
- Fair to the canon: Fernbank has been empty but for the librarian and the golem since day 520 (Respawn Tax).
