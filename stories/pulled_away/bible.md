## World
- **Day 6000.** Places: **Bramble Hollow** (map.json `bramble_hollow`, claimed 2026-11-11 for this story: a family
  homestead of two cottages and a field in a hollow behind the dark oak ridge west of Oakend, origin (-598, 70, -1062),
  ground y a placeholder; the ridge hides Fernbank and the inlet: verify on the survey and on stills) and **Fernbank**
  (canon; the well (-380, 64, -1030), the dock (-423, 63, -1026) facing west into the inlet, the dock's end and the
  fisherman's empty boat at about (-436.5, 62.6, -1025.5)). Between them: about 140 blocks of grass over the ridge and
  along Oakend's north edge (Oakend itself never in frame), to the inlet's west bank at about (-452, 66, -1086), north
  of Oakend's footprint; then the crossing.
- **Canon before:** Fernbank has been empty but for the librarian and the iron golem since day 520 (Respawn Tax). The
  empty boat floats at the dock (since 400); the HELLOBUH headstones in the churchyard (since 520).
- **Canon this story makes** (canon.md "Planned"; not canon until made): Bramble Hollow and its family (no name tags:
  not Oakend's custom, so no conflict with Names in the Woods). A man (a player, nameless) first came about day 5700;
  the kid followed him down to the water, where he looked across at Fernbank's one lit window. From then on he brought
  villagers to Fernbank one at a time, from the Hollow and elsewhere; the kid is the 40th. He builds or relights houses
  near the dock for them. The ridge hides Fernbank from the Hollow, so the kid has never seen it lit and still calls it
  "the old empty village". From day 6000 Fernbank is a living village again, the librarian with neighbours; the golem,
  the empty boat and the headstones unchanged. The Hollow is left empty.
- **Easter egg (one, canon rule 5; background only, never named):** `empty_boat` (S10). The golem in S11 is a
  Fernbank resident, not an egg. No graves.
- **Stands alone:** nothing in the lines needs another Short.
- **The man** is nameless, no name tag, a new plain skin (the lead commissions original skins at build), never Steve.
