## World
- **Day 6000.** Places: **Oakend** (map.json `oakend`, claimed by names_in_the_woods for day 2700; a cleric's hamlet on
  the North Inlet's west bank at (-497, 63, -1054), facing Fernbank across the water) and **Fernbank** (canon; the well
  (-380, 64, -1030), the dock (-423, 63, -1026) facing west into the inlet, the dock's end and the fisherman's empty boat
  at about (-436.5, 62.6, -1025.5)).
- **Canon before:** Fernbank has been empty but for the librarian and the iron golem since day 520 (Respawn Tax). The
  empty boat floats at the dock (since 400); the HELLOBUH headstones stand in the churchyard (since 520).
- **Canon this story makes (in canon.md "Planned", not canon until made):** a man (a player, nameless) brings
  villagers to Fernbank one at a time; the kid is the 40th; from day 6000 Fernbank is a living village again (every
  house lit, about 40 villagers, beds in every room), the librarian with neighbours, the golem still by the well, the
  empty boat and the headstones unchanged. Oakend loses the kid's family. Fernbank needs more houses than its 4 canon
  buildings for forty villagers: new cottages (since ~5800, built by the man) go on the slope north and south of the
  existing ones (set.md).
- **Oakend's name tags (names_in_the_woods: every Oakend villager has one, always visible in that story).** Here no
  name tag is drawn (vanilla shows a mob's name only under the crosshair; there is no player POV): no names on screen
  (owner: "Who is nico?"). If Names in the Woods is made with always-visible tags, this is a conflict for the owner to
  settle (the report flags it).
- **Easter eggs (background only, never named):** `fernbank_golem`, `empty_boat`, `hellobuh_graves` in S04 and S10.
- **Stands alone:** nothing in the lines needs another Short; "the old empty village" is explained by its picture.

## Accuracy
- Boats on leads: 1.21 (Boat#Lead, Lead#History). A lead does nothing while a player sits in the boat: the man never
  gets in. A leashed boat moves on land (slowly, scraping) and floats behind on water.
- A villager can't leave a boat on its own (it needs to be broken or the player to get it out).
- Villagers walk to their beds at dusk (S10's motion).
- A baby villager fits in a boat.
- Villagers don't wave: the librarian turns her head (S11); no "wave" in the script.
- No bell: Fernbank has none and nobody on screen would ring it (dropped from the premise's ending).
