- **Day 6000.** Places: **Oakend** (map.json `oakend`, claimed by names_in_the_woods for day 2700, no pieces yet; the

  **Fernbank** (canon; the well (-380, 64, -1030), the dock (-423, 63, -1026) facing west into the inlet, the dock's end

  east edge and Fernbank's dock's end.
- **Canon before:** Fernbank has been empty but for the librarian and the iron golem since day 520 (Respawn Tax). The
  empty boat floats at the dock (since 400); the HELLOBUH headstones in the churchyard (since 520).
- **Canon this story makes** (canon.md "Planned"; not canon until made): a man (a player, nameless) first came to
  Oakend about day 5700, looked across at Fernbank and its one lit window, and from then on brought villagers to
  Fernbank one at a time (from Oakend and elsewhere); the kid is the 40th. He builds or relights houses near the dock for them. From day 6000 Fernbank is a living village again, the librarian with neighbours; the golem still by the well;
  the empty boat and the headstones unchanged. Oakend loses the kid's family.
- **Open for the owner: Fernbank's lights from Oakend.** Only 10-15 blocks of water lie between them, so from day ~5800
  Oakend could see lit houses across the inlet. The kid still calls it "the old empty village" (her naivety; she has
  never been). No present-day shot looks across before S10, so the picture never contradicts her. If that isn't fair
  enough, the fix is the bible's name-tag option (b): a new homestead farther west, out of sight.
- **Oakend's name tags** (names_in_the_woods, not made: every Oakend villager is tagged at birth, always visible in
  that story). Here no name tag is drawn: no names on screen (owner: "Who is nico?"). **The owner settles it:** (a) keep
  Oakend and drop the always-visible tags (NitW is unmade), or (b) move the kid's family to a new untagged homestead on
  the west bank (claim it). Either way no tagged villager appears in S11.
- **Easter eggs (background only, never named):** `empty_boat` (S10), `fernbank_golem` (S11), `hellobuh_graves` (only if
  a still shows them). The brief names all three as fan details; the critic would keep one.

  in hers. A player riding his own boat can hold a lead (as a rider leads a horse); verify the towed boat follows on
  water in the engine test.

- Villagers don't wave: the librarian turns (S11).
