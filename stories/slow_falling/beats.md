# slow_falling: beat sheet, clue map, writer's checks (r2)

r2 (2026-11-10) applies the r1 critic's and continuity's must-fixes (reviews/r1-response.md): no bottle or drink in
S01 (the juice first appears in S03), "For MY trip" (L07), "I'm not LIGHT" (L09), "Grandpa BREWED her wings" (L13)
with tomorrow's bottle in Grandpa's hand, the step moved onto a 3-block jetty 2 blocks west of the cliff face, every
fall timed to vanilla Slow Falling (23 blocks in 4.4 s), the cellar dressed as a potion lab, Pip's recipe requests.

## The ending first
The reveal frame: a wide over Grandpa's shoulder on the market bench, tomorrow's peach bottle in his hand, looking up
at Grandma and the kid floating down past the glass column, swirls on both, the windmill on the cliff above. Just
before it, the game says what the "tea" is: "Potion of Slow Falling" over the hotbar, the effect icon, the slow drop
with no damage. The first frame it re-explains: Grandma stepping off the end of the windmill's jetty with empty hands.
She isn't brave or magic: every one of those 11,000 steps was a bottle Grandpa brewed the night before.

## Spine
- **Every day,** Grandma rides Grandpa's fountain to the top of the windmill and steps off, sure she's light as a
  feather, after the juice he packs her "for the trip"; every night Grandpa sits at his tea machine in the cellar.
- **One day,** on his birthday, the kid takes one forbidden tea "for MY trip" while Grandpa dozes, and next morning
  rides up behind Grandma.
- **Because of that,** he's stuck at the top of a fountain that only goes up, and he isn't light; then he sees her juice
  is the same color as his tea.
- **Until finally,** he drinks it and steps off after her, the game names it, he floats down beside her, she beams that
  he got it from her, and he tells us who really gave her wings.

  The attentive viewer gets it at L10's "Same color as my TEA" (~30 s, ~72%), a beat before the kid acts on it; L12
  confirms she doesn't know; L13 says it.

## "Why?" interrogation
- **Why does Grandma go up every morning?** She loves to fly. It's her morning, for thirty years.
- **Why does she jump instead of climbing down?** There's no way down: the fountain is a bubble column and only lifts
  (L04, S04), and the windmill has no ladder or stairs (set: the piece's inside ladder is removed).
- **Why did Grandpa build only up?** "She was born to fly" (L05): he gave her a reason to fly every day.
- **Why doesn't she die?** The juice is slow falling (the twist). Without it the drop is 23 blocks: lethal.
- **Why doesn't Grandma know?** It comes as juice from Grandpa every morning; she never skipped one. She believes she's
  light as a feather (L02) and that it runs in the family (L12).
- **Why does Grandpa keep it secret?** So it stays true for her. The bible's backstory; the script lets L05 carry it.
- **Why brew every night if a stand makes three?** He keeps a shelf of spares (S06 shows it) and has never let it run
  low. Every night is his habit. (Also: why the kid's theft doesn't leave Grandma without one.)
- **Why does the kid take the tea?** It's his birthday and he wants to fly too: he copies Grandma's ritual (she gets
  juice for the trip, he takes a tea "For MY trip"). He doesn't know it's the juice; it's the grown-up drink he's not
  allowed. So it isn't luck: he takes it on purpose for the flight.
- **Why does he ride up when it only goes up?** He planned to fly down like her (L07); only at the top does he see the
  hole in the plan: he isn't light (L09). Nobody stops him: Grandpa is at the bread stall talking to the baker, back
  turned (on screen in S08), Grandma is above him, facing up.
- **Why doesn't Grandma see him in the column?** She's above him, facing up, and the ride takes about 2 s.
- **Why does he step off a 23-block drop?** He's stuck (L09), he has worked out that her juice and his tea are the
  same (L10), and he drinks first and sees the effect icon. The "So" says it.
- **Why doesn't Grandma stop him?** She stands at the jetty end with her back to him (S10) and steps off half a second
  before him; she sees him only in the air (S12).
- **Why is Grandpa on the bench at the end?** He watches her land every morning (S05), and he's already holding
  tomorrow's bottle (S13).

## The out-of-context read (each line, 3 s, a stranger)
- L01-L05: a funny grandma story. L06 "I'm not allowed any" (tea): fine. L07 "he fell asleep at the tea machine. I took
  ONE.": a kid sneaking a tea; it says "tea machine" in the same breath, and the cellar is dressed as a potion lab (a
  lit brewing stand, potions in item frames, no barrels or casks in frame), so it never reads as moonshine. No adult hands
  the kid anything in secret (r1 dropped a "don't tell your grandma" bribe beat for exactly this reason). L11 "So I
  drank mine and stepped off too": played bright, mid-air with Grandma in the next frame; never "jumped" from a kid.
- No hushed lines anywhere: every direction says full volume.
