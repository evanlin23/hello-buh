# slow_falling r1: critic

Verdict: **REVISE** (overall 7.0/10; Twist 7, Agency 6, Continuity 6)

- Mystery 6: the "how" is gone by S02.
- Twist 7: one step, flips L02 "light as a FEATHER". Players are ahead at 3 s, not 70%.
- Fairness 8: bottle, swirls, column, stand and name are all shown first.
- Rewatch 8: L02, L03 "for the trip", L05 "BORN to fly".
- Agency 6: the stolen tea rescues him by luck.
- Character 7: Grandma's and Grandpa's quotes say the same thing.
- Delivery 8: 119 words, about 42 s, no "...".
- Visual 7: wide, real relief.
- Continuity 6: the step at (0,24,1) drops onto the cliff and glass, not the market.
- Owner fit 7: why-tf, self-explaining end. Whimsy over mystery.
- Against runaway: worse. Close the gap by hiding the how until about 70%.
