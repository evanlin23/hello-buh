# statue_younger: set plan (r2)

- **Stonefold** (map.json `stonefold`, claimed 2026-11-12; no pieces yet): the High Meadow's open top west of
  Larkfield. Natural terrain, no clearing: keep the grass, oaks and flowers.
- **The statue**: a giant stone face (head only, no body), about 22 wide x 24 tall x 8 deep, stone with a smooth
  stone brow, facing east, on a 2-tall stone-brick plinth under the chin. Eyes of polished andesite set 1 deep, a
  broad nose 3 wide, a straight mouth. **Wrinkles**: 1-tall rows of cobbled deepslate across the brow (8 rows) and
  the cheeks (11 rows, two columns), 19 in all on day 3892, each row 5-9 long, readable at phone size from S01's
  25 blocks. A removed row becomes **calcite** (a pale clean stripe that reads against stone at 300 px), so "younger" reads on a still.
- **The bench**: two oak stairs facing east, sign arms, at the plinth's east foot.
- **The cottages**: four small oak-and-cobble cottages in an arc 12-25 blocks east; the kid's (east-most) has a
  bedroom window facing west at the statue (S03's lens), a table by a west window (S04), a front door facing west (S06, S07).
- **Gate:** a still from S01's camera at 300 px wide reads (a) a giant face, (b) dark lines on it and pale stripes,
  (c) one grey head at its foot. If (c) fails, Dad stands in a MS foreground at frame bottom-left and the statue stays centred.
