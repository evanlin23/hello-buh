**Kept: v5** (mean ~48%). Any line where the neighbor shows interest in her mine, or the allay praises him, sends
readers to him (60-65%). The grumpy line with a misreading is the least pointed. L07's reversed order ("when I bring it
diamonds, it sings") hides "who plays it" for casual players; experts still ask, since a note block can't play itself.

**v5 casual reader, after the reveal:**
- One sentence: "The neighbor has been tapping a note block in his hidden room next to her mine, so her allay has been
  flying her diamonds to him the whole time, and he's wearing them."
- Surprised: a little. Its second guess was right, and the lava red herring "got me".
- Share: 8. Comment: "bro was 'trying to sleep' in full diamond armor 💀 the allay just doing its job 😭".
- The ending reads without words, if his hand on the block and his face (matching the window) read.
- Naive all through: "It never sounds knowing."
- Its notes: the lava fake-out is never paid off; a non-Java viewer gets "he's stealing", not how.
