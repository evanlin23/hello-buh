one pass, midpoint first (through L12), then the ending.

the empty hands, and "she showed me on day one" (confusing: did she build the box?). Fixed in v2: the box "sings every
time I come close" (the allay's misreading), a lava red herring with the hole's place, a grumpy neighbor instead of a kind
one, and "every allay knows that's where your finds go" in place of "she showed me".
