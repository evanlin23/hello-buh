# singing_box: beats (r1)

## The reveal frame (written first)
Through the 1x1 hole in the mine wall: a hidden torch-lit room. The neighbor, in full diamond armour with a diamond
pickaxe and sword, taps the note block beside the hole; the allay drops diamond 32 at his feet. Cut 0.8 s after the tap.

## Spine
- **Every day** her TNT blasts diamonds loose and her allay flies every one to the singing box in the mine wall.
- **One day** (every night) she climbs out with empty hands: "Nothing again. It's empty."
- **Because of that** the allay decides to find her the biggest one yet.
- **Until finally** it carries diamond 32 through the hole, to the neighbor wearing the other 31.

The vanilla rule the twist uses: an allay that hears a note block played within 16 blocks delivers its items to that
note block instead of its player, for 30 s, and each note resets the timer; it only collects items matching the one
it holds ([minecraft.wiki Allay#Behavior](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay#Behavior)).
