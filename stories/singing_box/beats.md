# singing_box: beats (r4, the Roblox story format: docs/story_format.md)

## The reveal frame (written first)
Through the 1x1 hole in the mine wall: a hidden torch-lit room. The neighbor, in diamond chestplate, leggings and boots
with a diamond pickaxe and sword, taps the note block beside the hole; the allay drops diamond 32 at his feet. Said
over it (L15): "And there was the grumpy man, PLAYING the box, wearing every diamond I ever FOUND." His one beat (L16):
"Thirty-TWO." Cut 0.8 s after.

## Spine
- **Every day** her TNT blasts diamonds loose and her allay flies every one to the singing box in the mine wall.
- **One day** (every night) she climbs out with empty hands: "Nothing AGAIN. Tomorrow, I QUIT."
- **Because of that** the allay won't let her quit, and follows the biggest one yet through the hole to see where they go.
- **Until finally** it finds the grumpy man next door playing the box, wearing the other 31.

## Clue map

| gone when I drop them | L08, S08 (jump cut) | it's hungry | he picks them up |
| the grumpy man sleeps all day, the booms wake him | L10, L11, S10 | poor sleepy man | he's awake behind the wall all day; the booms are his cue |

The vanilla rule the twist uses: an allay that hears a note block played within 16 blocks delivers its items to that
note block instead of its player, for 30 s, and each note resets the timer; it only collects items matching the one
it holds ([minecraft.wiki Allay#Behavior](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay#Behavior)). The casual viewer needs only L07.
