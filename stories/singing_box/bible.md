right (S03, S06), the hole in the north (back) wall. S13 crosses through the hole north into his room; the shot from
inside looks south back at the hole. S10 looks north-east over her shoulder to his window.

## Who knows what
- **The allay** never knows. It misreads every clue (box happy to see it, box hungry), and its last words are its happy
  delivery.
- **She** doesn't know: she never goes near the lava, so she has never seen the hole, and she thinks the mine is empty.
- **He** knows everything. His only line complains about her noise.
- **The viewer** can guess lava (the red herring) or someone (the cold read: lava 65%, the neighbor 25%), and sees
  him only in S13.
