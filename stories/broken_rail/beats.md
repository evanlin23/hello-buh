# broken_rail: beat sheet, clue map, writer's checks (r1)

r1 (2026-11-10) applies the critic's pitch fixes: the pig stuck in its cart is on screen at ~6.9 s and misread ("He
LOVES it"); the screen answers why nobody walked the line in 22 years (Mom says Grandma left him, villagers never
leave the village, Milo wasn't allowed past the bridge, and Grandpa believes she's coming, so he waits); the kid's act
causes the ending (he bridges the crater, lays rail, relights the gold rail and pushes); the last frame is her cart
knocking into the buffer stop where Grandpa waits, with one 4-word line from Grandma.

## The ending first
The last frame: dusk at the rail town's station. Grandpa, a villager in a black apron, stands at the buffer stop where
he has stood every evening for 22 years. A minecart rolls in from the right and knocks into the buffer. Grandma sits in
it, in her straw hat, facing him a block away. "Sorry I'm late, dear." His head tips toward her. Cut.

The first frame it re-explains: the same spot, Grandpa's back, an empty rail running into the hills. "The same minecart"
was literal, and "She's coming" was true the whole time.

The reveal frame (the twist): across a grassed-over creeper crater on a valley floor, a minecart stopped on a dark gold
rail at the end of the broken line, a villager in a straw hat sitting in it, facing home. A Minecraft player reads it in
one thought: mobs can't get out of a minecart. She never left him. She was stuck on her way back.

## Spine
- **Every evening,** for 22 years, Grandpa waits at the end of an empty rail line and says "She's coming", while Mom
  says Grandma left him for her sister's village and kept the cart.
- **One night,** Milo turns ten, old enough to cross the bridge, and walks the line with a stack of rails.
- **Because of that,** 3,000 blocks out he finds the rail broken by an old creeper crater, and past it Grandma, still
  sitting in her cart, facing home.
- **Until finally,** he bridges the hole, lays rail and pushes, and that evening her cart rolls into the buffer stop
  where Grandpa is waiting.

Payoff = L14 (the arrival). The twist is *seen* at L11, but it *lands*, and line 1 flips, when she arrives and speaks:
the score drops 0.45 s before the buffer knock, the hit is on the knock, and the Short ends 0.6 s after "dear". Nothing
after the payoff.

The time cut: the critic suggested night to dawn; this draft uses morning to dusk instead, because the arrival must
land at Grandpa's hour ("every evening"), and the numbers work (bible, "Every number"): he leaves at midnight, sprints
3,000 blocks to the crater by mid-morning, and her cart, at the powered-rail top speed of 8 blocks/s, takes ~6.3
real minutes (~7,500 ticks) to get home, which lands it at dusk. The bible has the tick arithmetic. If the critic wants night to dawn, S13-S14 move to 23500 and L13 becomes
"That MORNING" (Grandpa would have to be at the stop at dawn, which breaks "every evening": not recommended).

## Twist checklist (docs/story_craft.md section 3)
- [x] **It changes what line 1 means.** Before: a sad old man waiting for a cart that won't come. After: he was waiting
      for the same minecart, and it was coming; he was right and everyone else was wrong.
- [x] **Fair play.** The mob-in-a-cart rule (S03-S04), the missing cart (S06), why no one went (L05, L07), the working
      powered line (S09) and the crater (S10) are all shown before L11. The reveal adds nothing new except her.
- [x] **Planted at least twice, at least once in the picture, each 1 s+.** The pig in its cart (S03-S04, ~5 s, picture),
      ROSE's empty spur (S06, ~1.8 s, picture), "She's coming" (line), "the same minecart" (line).
- [x] **Misread, not hidden.** "He LOVES it", "Won't even get out for a CARROT", "Kept the CART, too. RUDE.", "She's
      coming" heard as denial.
- [x] **Someone gets ahead.** ~29 s, 68% (above).
- [x] **The game says it.** A villager sitting in a minecart that has stopped where the powered rail went dead, at a
      creeper crater: real vanilla rules (mobs can't dismount carts; an unpowered powered rail brakes a cart; creeper
      explosions break rails and torches). Nothing is explained aloud after it: "Still facing HOME" is what we see.
- [x] **One step.** "She couldn't get out" gives the whole truth at once: she didn't leave him, she was on her way back.
- [x] **Readable at 300 px.** S11b is the one close-up (her face and straw hat in the cart, filling the frame). The ROSE
      sign text goes in the caption track too.
- [x] **Visible causes.** The crater (creeper, grown over), the dark gold rail with its torch gone (why the cart stopped
      at the gap and didn't roll into it), the lit gold rails (why the cart can get home), Milo's push (why it moves).
- [x] **A rewatch plays differently.** L01 "the SAME MINECART"; L02 "She's COMING"; L03-L04 the pig; L06 "Kept the
      CART, too. RUDE."; L07 "Villagers never leave the VILLAGE".

## The "why?" interrogation

| action / fact | why? |
|---|---|
| Grandpa waits at the buffer every evening | She left on the evening cart and was due back on it. He believes her ("She's coming"). |
| Grandpa never went to look | Villagers never leave the village (L07, S07); and he believes she's coming, so he waits where she'll arrive. |
| Mom never went to look | She believes Grandma left him (L05), and villagers never leave the village. |
| Nobody asked the sister's village | Villagers never leave their village: nobody carries word either way. The sister's village is past the crater. |
| Milo never went | He wasn't allowed past the bridge (L07) until he turned ten (L08). |
| He leaves at midnight | It's the first minute he's allowed: he turned ten at midnight. |
| He packs rails | He's the kid who laid the pig's track (S03); you take rails when you walk a rail line. They're in his hotbar from S04. |
| The pig sits in a cart all day | Milo put him in it on his yard loop; mobs can't get out of a minecart by themselves. Milo thinks the pig loves it. |
| The cart stopped at the gap and didn't fall into the crater | The last rail before the gap is a powered rail; the blast took its redstone torch, and an unpowered powered rail brakes a cart to a stop. |
| Grandma faces home | She was coming back from her sister's when her cart hit the dead rail. |
| Grandma didn't climb out and walk | Mobs can't get out of a minecart (the pig), and villagers don't leave the village anyway. |
| She's unchanged after 22 years | Villagers don't age or need food in the game; it's a fable. |
| The cart reaches home | The line's powered rails still work (S09); Milo bridged the gap, relit the gold rail and pushed. |
| Milo isn't at the station at the end | He's walking home behind her; the camera shows what he couldn't (house style). He narrates it after. |
| Grandma says "Sorry I'm late" | She was due on the evening cart, 22 years ago. |
