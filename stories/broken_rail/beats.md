# broken_rail: beat sheet, clue map, writer's checks (r2)

r2 (2026-11-10) applies the r1 critic's and continuity's must-fixes (reviews/r1-response.md): L02 "She's on her WAY",
L07 "Grandpa won't leave the STATION", L08 gives the kid a reason to go and to pack rails, S01 shows Grandpa's face,
L12 gets 3.8 s with a jump cut, Grandpa's mark is fixed north of the buffer, the S13 cart comes off the bridge, the
easter egg moves under the sunset. Should-fixes taken: Mom and the cart come before the pig (the pig is still on screen
at 6.9 s, in S03's foreground), and an activator rail at our buffer sets Grandma down at the end (how she got out at her
sister's). The narrator is Pip's recipe, single speaker.

## The ending first
The last frame: dusk at the rail town's station. Grandpa, a villager in a black apron, stands where he has stood every
evening for 22 years, just north of the buffer stop. A minecart rolls in from the right, over the red activator rail,
and knocks the buffer. The activator sets Grandma down, standing in her straw hat, facing him across the buffer stop.
"Sorry I'm late, dear." His head tips toward her. Cut.

The first frame it re-explains: the same spot, Grandpa's face in three-quarter, the lantern at his feet, an empty rail
running into the sunset. "The same minecart" was literal, and "She's on her way" was true the whole time.

The reveal frame (the twist): across a grassed-over creeper crater on a valley floor, a minecart stopped on a dark gold
rail at the end of the broken line, a villager in a straw hat sitting in it, facing home. A Minecraft player reads it in
one thought: mobs can't get out of a minecart. She never left him. She was stuck on her way back.

## Spine
- **Every evening,** for 22 years, Grandpa waits at the end of an empty rail line and says "She's on her way", while
  Mom says Grandma left him for her sister's village and kept the cart.
- **One night,** Milo turns ten, old enough to cross the bridge, and works it out like a kid who believes his grandpa:
  if she's on her way, the rail's broken. He packs rails.
- **Because of that,** 3,000 blocks out he finds the rail broken by an old creeper crater, and past it Grandma, still
  sitting in her cart, facing home.
- **Until finally,** he bridges the hole, lays rail and pushes, and that evening her cart rolls into the buffer stop
  where Grandpa is waiting.

Payoff = L14 (the arrival). The twist is *seen* at L11, but it *lands*, and line 1 flips, when she arrives and speaks:
the score drops 0.45 s before the buffer knock, the hit is on the knock, and the Short ends 0.6 s after "dear".

The time cut: morning at the crater to dusk at the station (not the critic's pitch-stage night to dawn), because the
arrival must land at Grandpa's hour ("every evening"). The numbers work: he leaves at midnight, sprints 3,000 blocks
to the crater by mid-morning, and her cart, at the powered-rail top speed of 8 blocks/s, takes ~6.3 real minutes
(~7,500 ticks) to get home, which lands it at dusk (bible, "Every number").

## Twist checklist (docs/story_craft.md section 3)
- [x] **It changes what line 1 means.** Before: a sad old man waiting for a cart that won't come. After: he was waiting
      for the same minecart, and it was coming; he was right and everyone else was wrong.
- [x] **Fair play.** The mob-in-a-cart rule (S03, S05-S06), the missing cart (S04), why no one went (L03, L07), the
      working powered line (S09) and the crater (S10) are all shown before L11.
- [x] **Planted at least twice, at least once in the picture, each 1 s+.** The pig in its cart (S03 fg 2.5 s, S05-S06
      ~5 s), ROSE's empty spur (S04, ~1.8 s), "She's on her way" and "the same minecart" (lines).
- [x] **Misread, not hidden.** "LOVES it", "Won't even get out for a CARROT", "Kept the CART, too. RUDE.", "She's on
      her way" heard as denial.
- [x] **Someone gets ahead.** ~24 s, 55% (above).
- [x] **The game says it.** A villager sitting in a minecart stopped where the powered rail went dead, at a creeper
      crater: mobs can't dismount carts; an unpowered powered rail brakes a cart; creeper blasts break rails and
      torches; an activator rail ejects passengers. Nothing is explained aloud after it.
- [x] **One step.** "She couldn't get out" gives the whole truth at once.
- [x] **Readable at 300 px.** S11b is the one close-up (her face and straw hat in the cart). S01 now starts close on
      Grandpa's face. The ROSE sign text goes in the caption track too.
- [x] **Visible causes.** The crater (grown over), the dark gold rail with its torch gone, the lit gold rails, Milo's
      push, the activator rail at the buffer.
- [x] **A rewatch plays differently.** L01 "the SAME MINECART"; L02 "She's on her WAY"; L04 "RUDE"; L05-L06 the pig;
      L07 "Grandpa won't leave the STATION".

|---|---|
| Grandpa waits at the buffer every evening | She left on the evening cart and was due back on it. He believes she's on her way. |

| Mom never went to look | She believes Grandma left him (L03). |
