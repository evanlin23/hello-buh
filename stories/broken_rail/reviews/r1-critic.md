# broken_rail r1, critic

Verdict: **REVISE**. Overall 7.0/10.

**Cold read.** Grandpa has waited 22 years at an empty rail saying she's coming, and Mom says Grandma left him. Milo walks the line, finds a creeper hole with Grandma stuck in her cart past it facing home, fixes it, and she arrives. The twist means she never left him: mobs can't get out of carts, so she was stuck on her way back. It reads clearly.

## Scores
- Hook 7: an old villager's back 12 blocks off and an empty rail is a speck at 300 px. Villagers standing still breaks no rule.
- Second beat 6: L02 repeats L01, then L03 swerves into a pig joke.
- Mystery 7: one idea per beat. L07 "Villagers never leave the VILLAGE" contradicts L05, where she went to her sister's.
- Twist 8: one step, shown by the game, flips L02 "She's COMING" and L06 "RUDE". Predictable early: two pig beats plus "Kept the CART" give it away by about 16 s (37%).
- Fairness 8: pig in cart (L03-L04, "loves it"), ROSE spur (L06, "rude"), dead gold rail (S11a). All are shown before the reveal.
- Rewatch 8: L02, L04 "Won't even get out", L06.
- Agency 6: L08 never says why he goes or why he packed rails (and dirt and a torch) for a break he can't know about.
- Character 6: Grandpa is one quoted belief. Grandma's line is good.
- Delivery 7: 117 words, about 43 s, pauses planned. The direction says "nine-year-old", but he turns TEN.
- Visual 7: wide, full scale, natural terrain. S12a/S12b fit 7 dirt, 7 rails, a torch, a walk and a push into 2.6 s.
- Continuity 7: cart passengers don't move in recordings (staging lessons) in S12b-S14. Grandma must face north in every shot.
- Owner fit 7: serious and Minecraft-native. It fails the "why?" rule (L07) and the out-of-context read (L02).
- Against runaway: worse. To close the gap, the kid needs to choose to believe Grandpa, out loud.

## Must-fix
1. **L02** "She's COMING" invites an innuendo joke out of context. Change it to: `He just says, "She's on her WAY."` (look word WAY).
2. **L07** contradicts L05. Change it to: "Grandpa won't leave the STATION. And I wasn't allowed past the BRIDGE till TEN." In S07, the fletcher beat becomes Grandpa at the buffer, small in the foreground, the bridge beyond.
3. **L08** needs a want and a reason for the rails. Change it to: "At MIDNIGHT I turned TEN. If she was COMING, the rail was BROKEN. I packed RAILS." (or "on her WAY" to match fix 1).
4. **S01** hook: start 5 blocks behind and to the side of Grandpa, so his face, apron and the buffer lantern fill the lower-left third. The empty rail runs to the sunset horizon. Moving from frame 1.
5. **L12/S12a** needs 3.8 s. Jump-cut S12a: the dirt row is already 5 blocks across, the last rails go down on "laid RAILS", and the torch lights. S12b's push lands on "PUSHED".

## Should-fix
- Move L05-L06 to straight after L02, which gives a stronger second beat, and the pig after them. That holds back the get-ahead.
- How did she get out at her sister's? Put an activator rail at the buffer in S14 that pops her out beside him.
- Fix the r1-r3 direction: "ten-year-old".
- Originality: this is the fourth "grandparent's lost spouse, decades" story (gold_for_the_portal, lantern_bay, slow_falling).
