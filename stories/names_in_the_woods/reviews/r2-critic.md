# names_in_the_woods r2: critic

Verdict: APPROVE. Overall 8.0/10 (r1: 7.1).

**Cold read.** The three named zombies are the neighbors, and tonight Mom joins them as ROSA. Milo, the only one who can throw, picks up her potion and starts the cure she taught him: "Potion, then APPLE."

**Round 1 must-fixes**
1. Ending: fixed. "I CAN. / Potion, / then APPLE." echoes L07 and needs no decoding. The throw lands on "Potion", the hotbar lands on "APPLE", and the cut comes within 1 s.
2. Second beat: fixed. "At our FENCE, / they STARE / at MOM." raises the stakes with no backstory, and the line about players is gone.
3. Hook: fixed on paper. The zombies are at 14-16 blocks with their arms raised and lamp-lit faces. The nameplate still test is still owed before the render.

**Regressions:** none blocking. The script has no "...", and the out-of-context read is clean.

Against runaway: worse on the twist (8 against 10). The reveal is a name rather than a re-seeing of the whole picture. That gap is the premise's ceiling, not a fix.

**Should-fixes**
1. L13/S13: "She DROPPED / her POTION." reads as luck, since a zombie dropping an item has no in-game cause. Make it a choice. ROSA stops, looks at the bottle, then at Milo, and lets it fall at his feet. Rewrite the line as "She DROPPED / her POTION / for ME." This also gives Mom her last act.
2. S02 and S04 are both at 13100, with Mom at the gate in one and inside at her window in the other. Set S04 to 13200, or label them as different nights in the bible.
3. Delivery: at about 40 s, the pauses can breathe. Use a 0.4 s gap after L11 "walked out" and before "ROSA" in L12.
4. S01: render the nameplate still first. If TOMAS, GRETA and OLD BEN don't read at 300 px, move to 12 blocks.
