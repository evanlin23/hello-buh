## The ending first (r8, the Roblox story format)
She flies UP to find home and her sisters; the camera keeps rising to straight above. She SAYS the reveal over the
settled picture: "And ALL the flowers made one giant BEE. With HOME right in its HEART." The giant is a speck at the
crown planting the last row. Sting: "Fifty-two BOTTLES. Fifty-two ROWS." (echoes line 1's FIFTY-TWO). Cut 1 s after.

## Spine
- Every time the giant takes a bottle of honey, he plants a row of flowers. WHY? (line 1)
- One day the flowers stop ending, and she HAS to know why, so she goes to find the edge.
- Because of that, she flies so far she can't see home, or her sisters (the loved ones at stake).
- Until finally she flies UP to find them, and sees what he's been planting all along: them.
