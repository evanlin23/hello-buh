## The ending first
From straight above, the meadow is one huge bee of flowers, 52 rows long, and the hive sits in a red heart where
the bee's heart would be. The giant is a speck at the tail planting the last row. Cut on the settle.

## Spine
- Every time the giant takes a bottle of honey, he plants a row of flowers.
- One day the flowers stop ending, and the bees fly farther every day.
- Because of that, today the narrator flies so far she can't see home.
- Until finally we rise above her and see what he's been planting all along: them.
