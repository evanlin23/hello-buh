# bell_window: spine, beats, clue map (r7: the Roblox story format, docs/story_format.md)

## The ending first
**The reveal frame** looks over the shoulder of a skeleton in the shade of an oak on the cliff, straight down one
line: the bell, still swinging, and the lit window behind it. The kid is beside the bell now, on the porch roof, clear
of it, waving. The skeleton raises its bow and draws, under the one spoken reveal line, L11: "He had a BOW, and this time the bell wasn't in the WAY." Cut 1.0 s after the draw.

**The first frame it re-explains** is the kid opening his shutters and the bell ringing right in front of his face.

## Spine
- **Every night,** when he opens his window, the bell right outside rings and the whole village runs indoors. He's sure the bell likes him.
- **Up on the cliff** there's a pale guy who never leaves the oak's shade; and one night the bell has a little "feather" sticking out of it, then it's gone.
- **Because of that,** when the bell rings again tonight, he climbs out past it to wave hi.
- **Until finally,** from the cliff we see it, and he says it: "He had a BOW, and this time the bell wasn't in the WAY." The bell that was in the way every time is not in the way now.

## The format (docs/story_format.md)
1. Fact + why, out loud: L01 "Every time I open my WINDOW, the BELL RINGS. And nobody will tell me WHY."
2. Quick two-way clues: the village runs inside (L02), only his window (L03), Mom's shutters (L06), the pale guy in the shade (L08), the feather (L09).
3. He wants to know: "nobody will tell me WHY" (L01), "Mom won't SAY why" (L05).
4. Someone loved at stake: the kid himself, Mom's "honey" (L06): she keeps his shutters shut.
5. The rule turned against itself: when it rings he goes OUT, past the bell (L10).
6. The reveal said in one plain sentence of what he sees: L11.
7. Cut 1.0 s after the draw on 'WAY'; L11's bell runs into L01's bell (the bell rang because it was in the way).

## Naivety (STANDARDS 0a)
The kid is cheerful from the first word to "HI": no hint, no fear, no foreshadowing, no explanation. Every clue has his innocent reading on a line; the viewer sees past it.
