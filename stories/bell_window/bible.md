# bell_window: continuity bible (r1)

## World
- **Place:** Saltmouth (canonical: the generated plains village at (-286, 69, -381)). Use the village's own bell. The
  boy's house is the house nearest the bell, with its upstairs window facing it.
- **The cliff:** the nearest natural rise north of the bell, 15-25 blocks high (read it off the survey). It has a
  stamped oak (`since: 1700`) whose shade covers a 3 x 3 patch on the edge. If no rise is in line, the critic and
  continuity choose another house so that the oak, the bell and the window line up.
- **Day:** 2000. Buh lives in Saltmouth only at 16 (day 3300), so this is another family.
- **New canon facts:**
  - the skeleton under the north-cliff oak, from ~1700, still there;
  - the bell's arrow-scarred post.
- **Never:** say what it is aloud; show the skeleton clearly before S08.

line. Every night shot from the square looks north.
