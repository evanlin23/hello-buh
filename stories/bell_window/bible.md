# bell_window: continuity bible (r3)

## World
- **Place:** Saltmouth (canonical: the generated plains village at (-286, 69, -381)). The kid's house is a stamped two-storey house facing the north rise, its upstairs window facing the bell frame 3 blocks north. Layout and numbers: set.md "The line-up check".
- **Day:** 2000. Buh lives in Saltmouth only at 16 (day 3300), so this is another family (not Buh's; never named).
- **New canon facts:** the skeleton under the north-cliff oak (from ~1700, still there on day 2000); the bell on its two-post frame north of the house; that family's house.
- **Never:** say "skeleton" aloud; show the skeleton readably before S08 (S06: a white shape in shade only).
- **Easter egg:** Saltmouth's ruined portal (`saltmouth_portal`, since 0), small at frame left in S05 by day. Never named.

## Positions and screen direction
The oak and the rise are north and high; the bell 3 blocks north of his window; all on x = 0. S01, S04, S07 look north from the window (the bell centred, the cliff behind it). S02 looks north from high behind the house (the axis down the middle). S06 looks north up at the oak from beside the bell. S08 looks south down the axis from the skeleton (the reverse of S02). S05 looks south-west at the house from the square. In S07 he steps east (screen right), so the bell slides left; in S08 (reversed) he is at frame left of the bell.
