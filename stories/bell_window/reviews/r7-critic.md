# bell_window: critic r7 (the Roblox story format, over the owner-approved r6)

Verdict: **REVISE**. One mechanical must-fix. Average 8.1. Hook, Twist and Fairness are 8 or higher. No score is under 7. Once the fix is in, it's approved, and nothing needs a re-read.

## Cold read
A cheerful kid tells us the village bell rings every time he opens his window and nobody will say why. Tonight he climbs out past the bell to wave at the pale guy on the cliff. The guy is a skeleton with a bow, and the bell has been taking its arrows. Now nothing is between them.
- **Casual reader at the midpoint (L08):** "Some monster up there? Maybe a skeleton? I don't know what it has to do with the bell."
- **Casual reader after L11:** "The bell was blocking the skeleton's arrows the whole time, and he walked out from behind it." That's one sentence.
- **Gamer:** sure it's a skeleton at L08, and probably guesses the shield at L09. That's acceptable, because the reveal still lands as dramatic irony.

## Format blockers
- **Line 1 states the fact and the why:** pass.
- **Two-way clues:** pass. There are five: "so SHY", "only MY window", "Shutters SHUT", "SUPER pale", "FEATHER".
- **The narrator wants to know:** pass. L01 has the "WHY", and L05 is "won't SAY why".
- **Someone loved at stake:** pass, but it's the thinnest beat. The kid himself is at stake, and Mom's "honey" and her shut shutters show he's loved. Both bars put the narrator himself at stake too.
- **Rule turned against itself:** pass. At L10, when the bell rings he goes out past it.
- **Reveal in one plain sentence:** fails on the letter. L11 is two sentences.
- **Cut within 1 s:** pass.
- **Last line echoes line 1:** pass. "the bell wasn't in the WAY" runs into "the village BELL RINGS", so the cause closes the loop.
- **Naivety:** pass. "He had a BOW" is what he sees. "Wasn't in the WAY" still means "now he can see me" to the kid, which is L10's own reason. That flips best of all on a rewatch.

## Must-fix
1. **L11, as one sentence.** Change both `text` and `say`:
   - text: "He had a BOW, / and this time / the bell / wasn't in / the WAY."
   - say: "He had a BOW, and this time the bell wasn't in the WAY."

## Should-fix
1. **L01:** trim it so "RINGS" lands before 3 s: "Every time I open my WINDOW, the BELL RINGS. And nobody will tell me WHY." L02 still names the village.
2. **S08:** a vanilla draw takes about 1 s, not 3. Raise the bow (attack arms) on 'BOW' and hold it. Start the draw so full draw lands on 'WAY'. Pin both marks in the stunt.
