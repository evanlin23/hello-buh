# bell_window: critic r1

Verdict: **REVISE**

## Cold read
Each time the kid opens his window, the bell rings and the village hides. There's an arrow in the post every morning and something white under the cliff oak. He leans out, and a skeleton up there is shooting at him with the bell in the way. **It stumbles:** S07 has him lean *past* the bell, yet S08's arrow still hits it, so cause and effect contradict.
- **Mystery test:** the question is set by 3 s. At the midpoint (L04) a viewer guesses "someone's shooting?" but isn't sure; players are sure at L06 (~65%). After: one sentence. **The cut fails:** S08-S09 run about 3 s after L07.
- **Why?**
  - Villagers are out at 18000 but sleep at dusk.
  - "Only LIT window": skeletons don't need light, so the clue is false to the game.
  - The skeleton's range is 15 blocks, but S08 puts it about 22 from the window.
  - S06 must show the drop that keeps it up there.

## Must-fixes
1. **L07 and S07-S09:** write "Tonight / the bell RANG again. / So I LEANED out / PAST it."
   - S07 (POV): on RANG an arrow hits the bell 3 blocks ahead, and the doors slam. On PAST the camera leans clear of the bell.
   - S08 (the reveal): from behind the skeleton, the kid exposed, the bell off the line. It draws. Cut 1.0 s later.
   - Delete S09. Payoff "L07+1.0".
2. **L05:** write "Every window in the village / is OPEN. / Except MINE." In S05 (day) every shutter is open but his. Skeletons ignore villagers, so this plants "only me".
3. **Site:** prove the window-bell-ledge line, with the ledge 8-10 up and ≤15 blocks from the window, or move the story.
