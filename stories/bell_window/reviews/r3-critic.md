# bell_window: critic r3

Verdict: **REVISE** (one round: one line and two shot notes)

## Cold read
Whenever he opens his window, a skeleton under the cliff oak shoots at him, and the bell hanging on its line takes the arrow. The ring is what sends the village indoors. He leans out past the bell, and now nothing is in the way.
- **Mystery test:** the question is set at 3 s. At the midpoint (L06) a viewer guesses "someone's shooting" but can't be sure. After the reveal they can say it in one sentence. The cut lands 1.0 s after the draw.
