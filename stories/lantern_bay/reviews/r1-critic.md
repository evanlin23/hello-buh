# lantern_bay r1: critic

Verdict: REVISE (overall 6.1/10)

## Cold read
Grandma has spent 300 nights laying lanterns along the bay floor to the shipwreck where Grandpa vanished, and tonight the boy places the last one and finds a chest titled For Mae. It means Grandpa left her something. But the road's purpose is solved at L08, and the chest was never planted, so the ending adds a gift rather than flipping L01.

## Scores
- Hook 7: a strong image (a glowing road on the seabed) and a number, but L01 is 17 words and lands at about 4.5 s.
- Second beat 5: L02 "I'm NEVER asleep" is character, not stakes.
- Mystery 6: one idea per beat, but L05-L07 are three beats about the dark, and after L08 no question is left open.
- Twist 5: one step and shown by the game (the chest title plus a caption), but L01 flips at L08, not at the reveal, and the chest is new information.
- Fairness 6: nothing plants the chest or the gift. "MAE" appears only on a sign that's unreadable at 300 px. L08 "used to dive" contradicts L05-L06 (pitch black and drowned even at noon).
- Rewatch 6: L04 "It's a ROAD", L07 "LUCKY water" and L11 "the LAST one" change meaning, but they change at L08.
- Agency 6: the noon swim is a real try. At the climax Milo is brought along and told where to place it.
- Character and dialogue 6: the two voices contrast, but L11 carries no clue.
- Delivery 7: 119 words, about 42 s, no "...", pauses planned. L01 is too long for one breath.
- Visual 7: wide establishing shots and natural terrain. Risks: the chest GUI renderer, the MAE sign's legibility, drowned in light 0.
- Continuity 6: Grandma's lantern across a 560-block swim, drowned visibility, two sites for the far end.
- Owner fit 6: serious and Minecraft-native, but the "why?" test fails (must-fixes 1 and 3). It's also the fourth lost-elder grief story (gold_for_the_portal, broken_rail, slow_falling), and light stopping spawns was already done in nine_thousand_torches.
- Against runaway: worse. The answer is guessed at 63% and the reveal is a keepsake, not a flip.

## Must-fixes
1. L08 (the "why?" test): if the deep is deadly at noon, how did Grandpa "used to dive" there? Rewrite: "Grandpa dove to that SHIPWRECK one night, for Grandma's PRESENT. He never came BACK." This also plants the gift.
2. L13 (the twist has to flip something at the reveal): "It said, FOR MAE. That's GRANDMA. He GOT there." Now "never came back" means he reached it.
3. S11-S12: why does Grandma take a nine-year-old to the step she sprints away from? Show the bow already lit and the drowned only far behind the hull, with Grandma calm and not sprinting.
4. S03b: "SPRINTS" needs a visible cause. When the light lands, the drowned turns and swims at her.
5. L01: one breath. "There are THREE HUNDRED lanterns on the bottom of our BAY." Merge L02 and L03: "She thinks I'm ASLEEP. Every NIGHT she swims to the END, puts ONE more, and SPRINTS home."

## Should-fixes
- Make MAE readable: a bigger sign, or a push-in on it in S08.
- L13 direction: "bright, reading it out loud", not "slowly" (it risks a hush).
- Confirm the chest GUI renders (docs/engine_features.md) before the build.
- Give Grandma a verbal habit.
