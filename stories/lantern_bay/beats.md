# lantern_bay: beat sheet, clue map, writer's checks (r1)

r1 (2026-11-10) rebuilds critic pitch #8 ("park: false rule, villagers don't become drowned") on the true rule:
**drowned only spawn in dark water** (block light 0), so a lit road across the deep bay is the one safe way to the
place Grandpa vanished. Nobody turns into anything. Grandpa is simply lost at sea; the drowned in the dark are the
reason nobody could go and look.

## The ending first
The reveal frame: the vanilla chest screen, opened underwater on a shipwreck's deck, its title reading **For Mae**,
one Heart of the Sea alone in the middle slot. Behind it, dimmed, the deck lit by the lantern the boy just placed.
The first frame it re-explains: a line of three hundred lanterns glowing on the bay floor, running straight out from
the dock into the dark, and Grandma at the end of the dock with one more. She wasn't decorating the sea. She was
building a road to the last thing Grandpa left her, one lantern a night, through water nobody can swim in the dark.

## Spine
- **Every night,** when she thinks the boy is asleep, Grandma swims out over a road of lanterns on the bay floor,
  dives at the end, puts down one more and sprints home.
- **One day** (one noon), the boy swims out to the end himself: past the last lantern the water is pitch black even at
  midday, with tridents moving in it, and nothing ever comes up inside the light.
- **Because of that** we learn the road points at the old shipwreck where Grandpa dove the night he didn't come back,
  and tonight Grandma says "last one" and takes the boy with her.
- **Until finally** she lets him put the last lantern down: its light lands on the shipwreck and on a chest named
  For Mae, Grandma's name, with Grandpa's present inside.

## "Why?" interrogation
- **Why lanterns, not torches?** Torches can't be placed in water (they pop off); lanterns can (they waterlog and keep
  glowing, light 15). A Minecraft player will ask; the answer is vanilla.
- **Why light at all? Why not just carry a lantern?** A held lantern lights nothing in vanilla (no dynamic light). To
  make water safe you have to place the light.
- **Why does light make it safe?** Drowned only spawn in water with block light 0 (and deeper than 5 below sea level).
  A placed lantern lights the water around it (light drops 2 a block in water), so nothing spawns inside the road. The
  deep part is 13 blocks down, so a floor lantern lights the whole water column above the road up to the 5-block
  surface layer where drowned never spawn: the road is safe from the floor to the surface.
- **Why one a night?** The end of the road is the edge of the dark, where drowned have already spawned and are
  waiting. She can swim out safely over the road, but placing the next lantern means diving to the edge, and the
  moment she's there the drowned come for her (S03b), so she puts one down and sprints home ("SPRINTS home"). Once
  she's 128+ blocks away the drowned at the far end despawn, so the next night the edge is clear again for one more.
- **Why at night?** By day she runs the house and the boy is awake. She goes after he's asleep because it's dangerous
  and she won't take him (L02). And day doesn't help: 13 blocks down, the sky light is 2 even at noon, too dim to stop
  a spawn (L05: "at NOON. PITCH BLACK.").
- **Why not take a boat?** Their only boat, the MAE, went down with Grandpa (the empty mooring, S04, S08). And a boat
  only floats over the dark: the shipwreck and whatever is in it are on the bottom, so you'd still have to dive into
  black water full of drowned. A boat doesn't make the bottom any less dark.
- **Why not ask for help?** The neighbours are villagers (the family is one of the mixed families: Milo and his
  grandparents are players, the village around them isn't). Villagers can't dive or place a block. Nobody else in the
  bay could go.
- **Why didn't Grandpa light it himself?** He dove by day, fast, the way divers do, and one night he went late. The
  bible keeps it at that: lost at sea. The story never says the drowned killed him; the tridents are why nobody could
  go and look.
- **Why is the chest on the wreck?** Grandpa found the Heart of the Sea diving there and hid it on the wreck's deck in
  a chest he'd renamed For Mae (vanilla: a renamed chest shows its name as the screen title): the one place in the
  world she'd never look. A present he never got to give.
- **Why does Grandma let the boy come tonight?** The road is finished but for one lantern; the whole way is lit, so
  it's as safe as that water gets. And it's his grandpa too.
- **Why does she let him place the last one?** L11: a gift. Three hundred nights were hers; the last step is his.
- **Why does the boy swim to the end at noon (L05)?** Curiosity: a kid who's "NEVER asleep" checks the thing his
  grandma does at night. He came back because of the tridents.
- **Why don't the drowned attack tonight?** They're out in the black (S10b, S11): the two swimmers stay inside the
  lit road the whole way, and the last lantern lights the wreck before they get there. The Short ends on the chest; the
  danger is still out there in the dark, which is the eerie half of the mood.
- **Why can the boy read it?** He opens the chest (a player can open a chest underwater); the title is on the screen.
