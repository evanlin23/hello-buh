# lantern_bay: beat sheet, clue map, writer's checks (r2)

r2 (2026-11-10) applies the r1 critic's and continuity's must-fixes (reviews/r1-response.md). Grandma is **Mae**.
The big change: the reveal now flips L07's "He never came BACK". The chest on the wreck, named for her, proves
**Grandpa got there**: he reached the wreck and found her present. Grandma's road leads to that proof.

## The ending first
The reveal frame: the vanilla chest screen, opened underwater on a shipwreck's deck, its title **For Mae**, one Heart
of the Sea alone in the middle slot. Behind it, dimmed, the deck lit by the lantern the boy just placed on the bow.
The frames it re-explains:
- L01's road of 300 lanterns was never decoration. It is the only safe way across water that kills in the dark.
- L07's "He never came BACK" now ends "He GOT there".

Grandpa went down for her present and made it. With the drowned coming he locked it in a chest with her name on it,
because carried items despawn and a chest keeps them forever. It waited 300 nights for the road to reach it.

## Spine
- **Every night,** when she thinks the boy is asleep, Grandma swims out over a road of lanterns on the bay floor, puts
  one more at the end and sprints home from the drowned that come out of the dark at her.
- **One day** (one noon) the boy swims to the end himself. Past the last lantern the water is pitch black even at noon,
  with tridents in it, and nothing is ever in the lit part.
- **Because of that** we learn the road points at the shipwreck Grandpa dove to one night for Grandma's present. He
  never came back. Tonight the road is one lantern short, and Grandma says "Last one. Come see."
- **Until finally** she lets the boy place the last lantern on the wreck. Its light finds a chest named For Mae, with
  her present inside: Grandpa got there.

## "Why?" interrogation
- **Why lanterns, not torches?** Torches can't be placed in water; lanterns can (they waterlog and keep glowing at
  light 15). A held lantern lights nothing in vanilla, so the light has to be placed.
- **Why is the light a road and not just "light stops spawns"?** The bay floor is 13 blocks down. Below 5 blocks under
  sea level the water is deadly in the dark, day or night. Light drops 1 a block in water, so a floor lantern lights 14
  blocks around it, all the way up to the surface layer. A line of them makes one lit corridor across the deep: no
  drowned can spawn inside it, and anything coming from the dark is seen coming. It is the only way across, and it can
  only grow at its end.
- **Why one a night?** The end of the road is the edge of the dark, where drowned are already waiting. Placing the next
  lantern means reaching into it, and the moment she does they come at her (S03b), so she puts one down and sprints.
  Once she's 128+ blocks away the drowned out there despawn, so next night the edge is clear again for one more.
- **Why at night?** She goes when the boy is asleep, because she won't take him (L02). Daytime doesn't help anyway: 13
  blocks down, the sky light is 2 at noon, too dim to stop a spawn (L04).
- **Why not take a boat?** Their only boat, the MAE, went with Grandpa (the empty mooring, S04, S08). A boat also only
  floats over the dark. The wreck is on the bottom, so you'd still have to dive into black water full of drowned.
- **Why not ask for help?** The neighbours are villagers, who can't dive or place a block. Milo and his grandparents
  are players (a mixed family, as in Light Sleeper). Nobody else in the bay could go.
- **How did Grandpa get to the wreck without the road?** He went once, fast, for something he wanted her to have (L07,
  "one night"), and he got there. The chest proves it. He never got back.
- **Why is the present in a chest?** He found it at the wreck. With the drowned coming, he knew he might not make it
  up. Anything he carried would be dropped and despawn in five minutes. A chest keeps it forever, so he put the chest
  down, named it for her (renamed in an anvil, so the screen shows it) and locked her present in. The bible has the
  details.
- **Why does Grandma take the boy tonight?** "LAST one. Come SEE." Tonight the road arrives. The whole way is lit, and
  in S11 she's calm with no drowned near. He's her grandson: she wants him there when she finds out what's at the end.
- **Why does she let him place the last one?** L10: a gift. Three hundred nights were hers. The last step is his.
- **Why is the bow dim, and the deck dark until the last lantern?** Light drops 1 a block in water. Lantern 300 is 12
  blocks short of the bow (light 3 there) and 16 from the chest (light 0). Lantern 301 on the bow lights the deck.
- **Why don't the drowned attack tonight?** They're far beyond the wreck, in the black (S11). The swimmers stay inside
  the lit road, and the last lantern lights the wreck before anything comes. The danger is still out there: that's the
  eerie half of the mood.
- **Why does the boy swim to the end at noon (L04)?** He's "NEVER asleep" and curious about what his grandma does. He
  came back because of the tridents.
