# bed_in_the_square: beat sheet, clue map, twist check

**Logline.** A boy guards the bed his grandma has kept in the middle of the village for forty years, the day his dad
comes to take it down, and finds out what it was for.

**The reveal frame (written first).** Dawn, the square, the sun behind the camera. Grandma smooths the blanket and steps
back; a man in the plainest skin in the game (classic Steve) is suddenly standing beside the bed with nothing in his
hands, the name "Walt" over his head. Ten seconds earlier the chat said *Walt left the confines of this world*.

**Spine.**
- *Every day,* Grandma makes the bed in the middle of the square, the one nobody may sleep in or build around, for the
  husband who walked east forty years ago to see where the world ends.
- *One day* the village wants the square for the market, and Dad, who thinks she's waiting for a dead man, says he'll
  take the bed down at dawn.
- *Because of that,* I sit up with her all night and stand between Dad and the bed.
- *Until finally* the chat says Walt "left the confines of this world": he reached the edge, the world border killed
  him, and because he never slept in another bed, he respawned here. Home. "You're late."

## Twist checklist (docs/story_craft.md section 3)

- [x] **Changes what line 1 means.** Before: a shrine to a dead man. After: an open door, kept for forty years.
- [x] **Fair play.** Bed = spawn (L04), the edge (L05), the spawn never moved (L06), his name (L05). The reveal adds no
      fact: the chat line is the vanilla message for dying outside the world border, and players respawn at their bed.
- [x] **Planted at least twice, one in the picture:** the cleared ring and moved well (S02), the torch line (S05).
- [x] **Misread, not hidden:** L03 (pity), L04 (superstition), L06 (romance), L07 (Dad's "there is no end").
- [x] **Someone gets ahead:** a Minecraft player at L07 (~28 s, ~55%): "there IS an edge". Most viewers at the chat
      line (~43 s, ~80%), a beat before Grandma moves. The gap between the chat (sounds like death) and the respawn
      (it is death, and it's home) is the suspense.
- [x] **The game says it:** the death message (`death.attack.outsideBorder`, en_us 1.21.11: "%1$s left the confines of
      this world") and the respawn. Nobody explains it after.
- [x] **One step:** "he died at the edge, so he came back to his bed."
- [x] **Readable at 300 px:** the chat line is also in the caption track (two chunks: *Walt left / the confines of this
      world*); the respawn is a whole person appearing in a WS; his nameplate.
- [x] **Visible causes:** no accidents. The death is the border's damage, offscreen and named by the game; the axe is
      Dad's choice, stopped by mine.
- [x] **A rewatch plays differently:** L03 "Like he's coming home TONIGHT" (she means it), L04 "he wakes up somewhere
      ELSE" (a game rule, not a superstition), L06 "He never slept in another bed" (a promise *and* a mechanic), L07
      "There IS no end" (yes there is), L11 "I'm SO sorry" (he's apologising at the moment of the miracle).

**Quotable line:** "You're LATE." Runner-up: "He never slept in another bed. Not once."

**Why a Minecraft player rewatches and shares.** The whole story is two rules every player has lived (your bed is
your respawn; the world border kills) turned into a love story: dying is the fastest way home. The comment it should
earn: "he had to die to come home 😭" / "she kept his spawn point for 40 years".

**Against runaway:** equal on image and twist; better on character and voices; slightly weaker on the scroll-stop
(a bed is calmer than a village running from you). The one thing to close the gap: make S01 move (villagers visibly
curving round the bed, smoke, a cat on it?) so the first frame already feels wrong.

**Risks.**
- Grandma, Dad and Walt are players (default skins, names over heads) living in a villager village: keep that
  consistent (the villagers never speak).
- "Forty years" of walking is story time: the real border is 29,999,984 blocks out. Nobody needs the maths; don't say a
  distance.
- The respawn has no effect in vanilla (no particles, no sound): sell it with the cut, the push-in and the hold, not
  an invented sparkle.
- The chat line must read on a phone: caption track, not just the HUD.
