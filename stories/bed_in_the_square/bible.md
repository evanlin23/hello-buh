# bed_in_the_square: continuity bible

## World
- Java 1.21.11 vanilla rules. Difficulty normal. `showDeathMessages` true (the chat line is the reveal).
- Scenes in order; each time jump is said by a line:
  | scene | lines / shots | time (ticks) | sun / light | weather |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | A morning | L01-L07 / S01-S07 | 1500 (sun low in the east, frame right when looking N or NE) | warm, long shadows west | clear |
  | B noon | L08 / S08 | 6000 | overhead | clear |
  | C night | L09 / S09 | 18000 (moon high) | lanterns only; one pool of light in the square | clear |
  | D dawn | L10-L12 / S10-S14 | 23300 rising to ~23700 by S14 | sun rising in the east; S13 has it behind the camera | clear |
- Scene A is "a morning" (any morning); scene B is the day before the dawn; C that night; D the next dawn. No
  season change: no snow anywhere.
- Numbers said: **forty** years (L01, L05). Nothing else is counted. The torch line has no stated count.

## Positions and screen direction
- **East is where Walt went.** When the camera looks N, east is frame right; Grandma always faces east (frame right) when
  she waits (S06). The sunrise in scene D comes from the east.
- **Dad comes from the west.** S10 looks S across the bed: Dad enters from frame right (west), I step in from frame
  left (east). The line between Dad and me runs W-E; S11 and S12 stay on its north side.
- S07: Grandma near (frame left, back to camera), Dad far (frame right). Camera on the east of the line between them.
- S14: camera over Walt's shoulder (he's at the bed's west side, frame right, near), Grandma across the bed facing
  camera (west-facing). Walt is the only person on the west side of the bed in scene D.

## The narrator
- Unseen in scene A and B (the narration is over shots of others). Seen from behind/side in C and D.
- Empty hand throughout: no weapon (he blocks the axe with his body, not a sword). Full hearts, no armor.
- L09 "I sat up with Grandma": he is on the stair-block beside her in S09.
- L10 "I stood in his way": he is between Dad and the bed in S10-S11.
