# stand_up: beat sheet, clue map, twist check

**Logline.** A young man logs back into his childhood server after ten years to find his old dirt hut, joins the
town's queue to try to stand up the dog nobody can move, and remembers too late whose dog it is.

**The reveal frame (written first).** POV at the fence: the crosshair on a grey wolf with a lime collar and the
nameplate BESTFREND; the hand swings; the wolf stands up. Cut wide over Pia's shoulder: the dog on four legs for the
first time in ten years, the queue turning, a steak dropping from someone's hand.

**Spine.**
- *Every day,* players queue in the town square to try to stand up the tamed wolf that has sat on the same block for
  ten years, because only a dog's owner can, and nobody knows who that is.
- *One day* I log back in for the first time in ten years to find my first house, a dirt hut on the hill behind it.
- *Because of that,* I get in line (the path up the hill goes right past him), half joking.
- *Until finally* I click him and he stands. He was mine. I told him to sit, and said I'd be back tomorrow.

## Twist checklist (docs/story_craft.md section 3)

- [x] **Changes what line 1 means.** Before: a server legend, a challenge. After: four hundred strangers tried to do
      what one kid promised to do the next day and didn't.
- [x] **Fair play.** The owner rule (L05), the hut (S01, L07), ten years twice (L02, L06), the collar colour (L07 +
      S08), the name (L08). The click adds nothing new: it is the rule.
- [x] **Planted at least twice, one in the picture:** S01 (the hut behind the plot), S07 (the gaze, the lime roof
      and collar).
- [x] **Misread, not hidden:** L06 (coincidence), L07 (nostalgia), L08 (mockery), L09 (Pia's judgment of "somebody").
- [x] **Someone gets ahead:** a Minecraft player at L07-L08 (~29-31 s, ~58%): lime collar + lime roof + ten years; most
      viewers at L09; the click is dread and hope at once.
- [x] **The game says it:** the dog stands up on his click. No line explains it; L13 is the confession, not an
      explanation.
- [x] **One step:** "he's his dog."
- [x] **Readable at 300 px:** a whole wolf going from sitting to standing in a WS (S12b), the queue reacting.
- [x] **Visible causes:** no accidents; the crater is a creeper's, named in L03.
- [x] **A rewatch plays differently:** L02 "TEN YEARS" (his ten years), L06 "my first HOUSE" (and my first dog), L08
      "Some kid couldn't SPELL" (he's laughing at himself), L09 "Somebody told him SIT" (said to his face), L11 "I
      almost didn't BOTHER" (the cruellest line on a rewatch).

**Quotable line:** "I told him I'd be back TOMORROW." Runner-up: "Some kid couldn't spell."

**Why a Minecraft player rewatches and shares.** Every player has gone back to an old world and found their dog still
sitting exactly where they left it, years later. This is that feeling turned into a mystery where you're the guilty
one. The comment it should earn: "I need to go check on my dog" / "BESTFREND 😭".

**Against runaway:** equal on image, better on emotion and voices, weaker on surprise (runaway's twist is a jolt; this
one is a dawning). The one thing to close the gap: keep L07's lime roof and the S07 gaze shot subtle enough that most
viewers only get it at L09.

**Risks.**
- Engine: the renderer draws wolves but no sitting pose or collar layer yet (grep `renderer/entities.js`): both are
  vanilla visuals (WolfModel sitting pose, the dyed collar layer) the engine worker must add. Without the sitting
  pose the story can't be made.
- Theme overlap with `bed_in_the_square` (someone waits years for a player who left): make only one of them first and
  space them apart. They differ in who you are (the one waiting vs. the one who left), the rule (respawn vs. sit) and
  the feeling (faith rewarded vs. guilt).
- Pia is a kid voice in an adult-sized player body unless the scale attribute renders; the voice carries it either way.
