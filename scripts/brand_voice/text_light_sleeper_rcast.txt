My village is covered in nine thousand CARPETS. The road? CARPET. The stairs? CARPET. The DOGHOUSE? Also CARPET. Even the bell wears a SWEATER. But one time, I pulled the wool off the WELL. Then something down the well just SCREAMED. Mom said it was a FOX. I didn't know foxes lived in WELLS. Then I found a music disc by the FARM. A real SONG. Nobody here has HEARD one. So tonight, I put a jukebox on the WELL. Wells echo, you KNOW. The fox screamed AGAIN. It loved the MUSIC. Then the lights went DARK. Just like a PARTY. Mom froze in the DOOR. But I'd never DANCED before. So I stepped off the carpet onto the STONE.
