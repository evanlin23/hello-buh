The dog on the hill hasn't moved in nine hundred days.

Not for rain. Not for thunder. Not even for zombies.

I bring him a bone every morning. He never even looks.

So every night now, I sit with him. Nobody should wait alone.

And then he stood up!

My village is covered in nine thousand carpets.

The roads. The stairs. Even the doghouse.

We keep three hundred sheep. We shear them every morning.

Our bell is wrapped in wool. I've never heard it.
