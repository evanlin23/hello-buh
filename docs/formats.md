## Ideas for each (to start from, not to copy)
- **race**: grass vs mycelium, moss vs sculk, fire vs water, vines vs glow lichen, a 4-way race; a bracket series.
  Built (lib/race.js): a floating 64x64 field over a deep ocean (nothing outside can spread in), seeds in opposite
  corners, random_tick_speed 300, the edit at 3x (6x for the last dirt). Grass and mycelium spread by the same vanilla
  rule, so the winner is luck: two takes went 54-46 mycelium and 56-44 grass, ~92-97 s of game time. Only grass and
  mycelium spread onto dirt by random ticks; moss and nylium spread only with bone meal, sculk only from a catalyst
  when mobs die, so those pairs need their own driver (makeRace takes the blocks; the spreading is the server's).
- **cells**: a zombie on an endless minecart loop chasing a villager it never reaches, a creeper on a piston treadmill,
  a skeleton forever shooting a target that resets, a pillager stuck in a water elevator, a chicken laying into a hopper.
  Built kinds (lib/cells.js CELLS): cow_pingpong (two pistons), chicken_eggs (an egg dispenser: chicks hatch in there),
  sheep_shears (a shears dispenser beside a wall of wool); finale golem_launcher (a slime launcher in a glass tube).
- **logic**: water bucket vs everything, "gravity is fake" (sand falls, a dirt island floats), boats on ice, beds in
  the Nether (docs/ideas.md "Physics and minecraft logic").
- **fakemod**: "a creeper asks permission", "mobs need a ticket", "your pickaxe has feelings", "villagers unionise".
- **hack**: trapdoor shutters, a hidden piston door behind a painting, a stair-and-sign sofa, a cauldron sink,
  a hidden staircase; each a real trick, demoed in a cosy build.
- **quest**: a desert-pyramid secret chest, a jungle-temple vault, a stronghold library behind a bookshelf; each
  stage a different trick (sticky-piston stairs, a lava parkour, a hidden lever).
  Built (lib/quest.js): an episode is a list of stage kinds on one straight course north (yaw 0, so the camera only
  pitches through the jumps): `tunnel` (a dead end mined through, steps down), `parkour` (1x1 pillars over lava; gap 2
  is easy, gap 3 and +1 are hard), `arrows` (real dispensers fired across the corridor, then a sprint between
  volleys), `vault` (a creeper face whose eye is a button, a piston door, the ONE chest). The start is always the
  dug blue terracotta, the drop into the 4-chest treasure room, and the hidden button. Lessons: a 1x1 hole only drops
  you if you stop over it; a course ceiling must stay 3+ blocks under natural ground (an aquifer flooded parkour 2);
  never put a button next to the block over a piston (quasi-connectivity kept it extended); sprint-jumps chain into
  overshoots, so the air steering also brakes (back key); the yaw must aim at the next pillar (the sway drifted 0.35).
- **love**: a heart of lamps that lights at night, a pixel-art "for you" made of flowers, two sheep in a heart pen,
  a heart-shaped firework show.
- **desire**: a shortcut through a farm, across a moat, over a lava trench, through a mansion; each obstacle
  absurder than the last, the path reappearing every time.
- **friend**: built (lib/friend.js): hand-built oaks that vanish whole in a blink (FR.tree / FR.vanish), the friend as an
  actor bot with his name over his head (stunt.playerNames, cast skin), the vanilla advancement chat flood
  (FR.advancement, FR.DAY_ONE), off-screen build noise (FR.buildNoise) and a tick timeline for his staged speed beside
  my own aimed, vanilla-speed actions. Ideas: the friend who already has full diamond on day 1, who "borrows" your stuff, who builds a castle while
  you build a dirt hut, who is AFK in the worst place, who always dies first.
- **underdog**: every mob can be tamed... but the phantom; every mob has a baby... but the ghast; every mob gets a
  friend... but the lone wandering trader llama.
- **stages**: the 3 stages of a new world (hope, hunger, hoarding), of finding diamonds, of a creeper encounter,
  of an Ender Dragon fight; keep each chapter tight, the whole Short far under 85 s.
  Built (lib/stages.js): S.edit({ chapters: [{ title, color, until }], payoff }) gives each chapter a title tag at
  the top of the game view with a whoosh, and the payoff the drop, a dip, an impact, a flash and a shake, ending on
  settle:0.6; S.chapter(ctx, i, shot) marks ch1..ch3. stages_friends (~20 s): punched into lava by a player actor
  (/damage ... player_attack by him: the actor's own attack packet never landed), a diamond vein in a deep cave (the
  respawn point), his house with TNT floors and a TNT crawlspace on a lever (pvp is a gamerule in 1.21.11; give the
  actor explosion_knockback_resistance 1 and override fr.respawn, or the owner glitch check sees a teleport).
- **dilemma**: the mace and the dog; a totem or your best friend's last golden apple; save the villager or the
  diamonds from the lava; each with a vanilla twist that makes the choice moot.
- **inside**: a library hall (inside_library), a mega storage room of chests and item frames, a greenhouse with a
  waterfall, a Nether-portal lounge, a two-floor bakery; the outside can also go the other way (a mansion that is one
  bed inside). lib/inside.js keeps the entry's light rule (no light within 6 blocks of the door's front cells).
- **faketrick**: carrying a portal, a "secret" diamond recipe, a floating lava tower, a portal frame made of gold (faketrick_gold_portal). The kit: lib/faketrick.js
  (makeStunt: a graded pad, steps as subs, F.fake marks the staged result; every visible action through lib/act.js).
  Anything built above the eye goes from a step or a rise: a top face above the eye can't be clicked (act refuses it).
- **sandart**: a giant TNT block (sandart_tnt), a diamond, a golden apple, a pig's face, the Earth, a Minecraft
  heart; poured in concrete powder, sand, red sand and gravel. Built (lib/sandart.js): makeSandArt({ art, palette, scale })
  pours every pixel as real falling blocks over a flattened field, each landing scheduled so they touch down evenly
  (the vanilla fall worked back to a release tick) and released above the drone, so no block ever pops into view; one
  continuous drone shot (sink into the rain, glide low, pull straight up), no cut while anything falls, the live
  `edit.tally` count of blocks landed, the payoff on the last landing (`settle`). `node lib/sandart.js stunts/<name>.js`
  prints the schedule. Falling blocks land silently in vanilla: the patter is a soft sand place sound.
- **psycho**: the lava mansion (psycho_lava_mansion); a "cosy" treehouse whose rope bridges cross a ravine of
  magma and pointed dripstone; an underwater house whose rooms are all open to the drowned; a TNT cottage (a floor
  of TNT under the rug, redstone torches for candles); a tiny-flat tour on a one-wide skybridge at build height.
  Built (lib/psycho.js): makeStunt({ rooms: [kinds], finale }) builds the burning yard, the fire-ringed door, the
  hall over the lava and each room kind (living_room, kitchen, bathroom, study) on its island; the finale bed_jump is
  a walking jump over a one-block lava gap onto the bed. Fire stays only on netherrack or on a flammable face
  (BaseFireBlock.onPlace drops fire over farmland at once; fire_spread_radius_around_player 0 stops the spread).
- **trap**: a trapped chest over a trapdoor (free diamonds); a "nether portal" frame whose step is a pressure plate
  that pulls the floor with sticky pistons; a fake bed in a hole (sleep = the drop); a tripwire across a diamond-ore
  vein that drops sand on him; a fake end portal frame over a water drop into a glass cage. Every one a real vanilla
  mechanism the friend sets off himself.
- **fakeworld**: built (lib/fakeworld.js): `fakeworld.swap(ctx, { block, x, z, R })` turns every chunk within R of a set
  into one block (ground, stone, sand, logs, leaves and lake beds -> block; plants -> air; water plants -> water; from
  4 under the lowest ground to over the tallest leaves), and `fakeworld.make({ name, block, treasure, caption, red,
  words, near })` is the whole survival episode: a tree of the block mined for "wood", the ground under it dug,
  the turn to a lone treasure block on the shore, dug by hand and held (the challenge sound, dip and flash).
  A new episode only names the block: gold, sponge, melon, glass, TNT (never lit), honey, slime, sculk, pumpkins.
  `near` pins /locate (the seed is fixed): fakeworld_diamond uses near [136, 22] -> the river bank at (512, 62, 22).

  `node scripts/dropper_sim.js <stunt>` (prismarine-physics, the same pilot) before recording; it matched the server's
  cut heights to the hundredth.
