# Run report: story_dog_hill ("The Dog on the Hill"), 2026-11-10

Session story-cine-2 (branch `claude/story-cine-2`), the second cinematic story made in parallel with the pilot.

**Premise (#16, original).** A tamed wolf has sat on the flat top of the hill above a real generated village for 900
days, facing one bare patch of grass in front of an overgrown dirt starter hut. Pip, a villager kid (the narrator),
brings him bones and begs him to stand; Grandpa: "Only one voice gets him up. Said he'd be right back." One night:
"HelloBuh joined the game", the player is standing on the patch (you log back in where you logged out), and the dog
stands for the first time. "Hey, buddy! Sorry... got busy. Okay. Sit, boy. I'll be right back." "HelloBuh left the
game." The dog sits. "Day one." (loops into line 1). Vanilla rules carry the twist: a sitting tamed wolf only stands
for its owner, bones mean nothing to a tamed wolf, the red collar, players reappear where they logged out.

## Tool problems found
1. tts.py's pause-based split lands inside lines written with "..."; my own pause-capping splice cut words (owner heard
   it). Fixed by main's --resplit; scripts/whisper_shim lets it run without torch/openai-whisper.
2. render_on_actions.sh --wait accepts a stale renders/result.json inherited from the base branch (checks status, not
   source == the queued commit). I waited on the source commit by hand.
3. The movie lint's "nobody moves" counts only player actors, so a story cast of NoAI mobs always fails it.
4. hb owner's flat-ground check fights low cameras in a flower meadow: removing flowers near cameras made shots "flatter".
5. Gemini TTS availability: 2.5-flash/pro-preview out of quota by 07:00 (shared key), 3.1-flash-tts-preview refused a
   plain take with SAFETY, 3.8-flash(-lite)-tts worked; gemini-2.5-flash text is retired (404).
6. A chat (or any) mark placed after ctx.tpCheck lands ~1.2 s after the teleport it belongs to.


## What slowed me down
1. **The format changed under me** (the owner's "same title and gameplay in the middle"): my full-frame 9:16 editor and
   its workflow were built and one render queued before the message arrived. ~15 min lost. The six hard rules, the
   voice library, the hb CLI and main's wolf model all landed while I was building, so I merged main twice.
2. **Server flakiness**: the first bot attempt timed out on almost every recording (the server falls behind while it
   generates the far-away chunks the harness uses to refresh), and one setup crashed the server twice (mobs parked
   inside stone). ~10 min.
3. **Splitting multi-speaker takes**: tts.py cuts at the longest pauses, which are inside lines written with "...".
   Word timings from Whisper fixed it (main now has scripts/align.py for this).
4. **Owner rules landed while building** (Milo, narrator-first, the flat-ground gate, pacing): each one was right, but
   each meant another recording. A gate that existed at the start (hb owner) would have saved two recordings.
5. **Actions renders took 25 min**, not the ~10-15 documented (16 slices on the second run).
6. **Cameras without a portrait check tool**: shot_sheet/try_shots are 16:9; I wrote a two-frames-per-shot portrait
   sheet (render.js FRAMES at 360x640, ~3 min) and used it four times.
