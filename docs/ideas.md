# Concept bank (ridiculous, creative, stageable)

Owner, 2026-11-06: "i want more and more ridiculous and creative ideas. we have a lot of headroom
for trying things out on this channel if we post 3x per day".

Every concept follows `docs/process.md`:
- 15–30 s with several escalating beats;
- reads in the first second;
- no still shot of 1 s or more;
- ends within ~1 s of the payoff;
- real survival actions on camera (commands only for the set, or for the scale nobody could do by hand).

Each concept is marked with a status and who builds it.

## Scale and spectacle ("how is that even possible")
1. **Deleting the world around me.** Chunk by chunk, the world vanishes until only the block I stand on is left. It ends as a skyblock, 1 block in the void. Ridiculous, and it reads instantly.
2. **Painting the whole world pink.** Grass turns to pink wool in an expanding ring from my feet, out to the horizon.
3. **500 sand-pillar dominoes.** Break the first pillar's base and a wave of falling sand runs to the horizon.
4. **1 vs 10 vs 100 vs 1,000 pigs in a 1x1 pen.** The pen explodes outward on the last beat (`tnt_scale` structure, with mobs).
5. **A bubble-column elevator to build limit.** 300 blocks of soul sand bubbles, ending above the clouds looking down.
6. **Filling a ravine with water, then lava, then anvils.** Escalating satisfying fills.
7. **The tallest staircase in Minecraft.** A spiral to build limit, then a water-bucket clutch all the way down.

## Rated / ranked / X vs Y vs Z (multi-beat by design)
8. **Rating every way to die (0/10 → 10/10).** Death screens with the real messages, each rated. Cactus, anvil, lava, TNT, drowning, fall, and the final "killed by a chicken jockey".
9. **Every tool vs one diamond ore.** A fist, a fish, a hoe, a shovel, then the pickaxe. Ranked, with the crack overlay showing the speed.
10. **1 second vs 1 minute vs 1 hour builds.** *(worker 6)*
11. **Every clutch from build limit, rated.** Water, hay, slime, powder snow, cobweb, bed, boat, ladder *(every_clutch + worker 4)*.
12. **Breaking bedrock with every item.** It fails until the ridiculous last one: a TNT cannon plus a piston trick.
13. **Taming every animal, ranked by how hard it was.**
