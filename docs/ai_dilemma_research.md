# AI-dilemma Shorts: research (2026-11-09)

The format (cenotshi, 216 Shorts, top ones 3-7M views): a narrator poses a life-or-death dilemma; TV-headed versions of
ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Claude and Gemini answer short and cold or hedging; Grok answers last, deep and slow, a heroic
self-sacrifice line as the music swells. Sometimes only ChatGPT vs Grok (23-43 s). Cinematic Minecraft with shaders,
plain TTS voices, one-word captions on the beat. Our twist: every answer is acted out in the scene, and the hero's
answer is the rescue itself, on camera.
