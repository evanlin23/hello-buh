# FINAL REPORT (written after reading bible.md)

## The whole story, how I'd explain it to my friend at lunch
"Okay so there's this huge tower called Highfall and once a year the teens jump off it and have to do an MLG water clutch into one block of water on top of a pillar called the Needle, and whatever floor you jump from, your family gets to live there. Rich people live up top. Buh's big brother Ash jumped from Forty, the top, and his bucket was empty and he died. Except he DIDN'T. There's a secret: every year one family gets to have a baby, and to pay for it they have to pick one of their kids to die, and that kid gets sent to Forty with an empty bucket and there's a redstone rig that moves the water one block so they miss and fall into the fog. But this old guy Fen who built the rig secretly dug a lake under the fog, so all thirty "dead" kids are alive down there. And Buh's MOM picked Ash. And this year the girl Buh likes, Tilly, is the one picked, by her own family, which is the Highkeeper's family. So on Drop Day Buh jumps from Forty, the rig fires anyway, and he beats it using his practice trick where he aims NEXT to the water, lands on the Needle in front of the whole city, opens the trapdoor, and all thirty dead people climb out. Then the Highkeeper jumps himself to make up for it and lands in the lake alive. They build stairs down to the real world, and at the end you see OTHER TOWERS doing the same thing. Season 2 bait. It's actually really good."

## Did the chapters deliver what the bible intends?
Mostly yes on the plot and the big clue chain (song, 8s, buckets, Floor 6 trick, "Thirty-three"). But a lot of the WORLD RULES never reach the screen, and two of the thematic payoffs depend on them. Everything below is in the bible and did NOT come across from the chapters alone:

1. **The Count (fixed number of beds) is never mentioned.** This is the biggest miss. Without it, "one in, one out" has no reason, and the bible's payoff for "There's always room on Forty" ("it was never full, it was unequal") can't land. I read "always room on Forty" as "because people die there", not as "the Highs are hoarding space". The empty rooms on Forty looked creepy, not like proof of a lie.
2. **Everyone believes the fog is the void ("The Below keeps what it takes").** Never said. So I didn't understand why nobody had looked under the fog in thirty years, and the green world reveal didn't hit as "everything you were taught is wrong", just as "cool, a secret base".
3. **Every 16-year-old jumps once.** Never said. I had to do math from "you'd have waited four years".
4. **You get ONE bucket from the Highkeeper's hand and the Keepers strip everything else at the plank.** Never said. This is why "Don't take his bucket" and Buh taking the Highkeeper's bucket in ch5 both confused me.
5. **Forty is publicly "cursed" (one Forty jumper misses every year), and people "swear" the bell rings twice.** Never said as common knowledge. Fen's tallies show it, but the crowd's superstition isn't on screen, so Buh choosing Forty doesn't feel as brave as it should.
6. **The bell is on Floor 40 and rings once per jumper.** Only half came across ("DONG" = jump, I worked out). Where the bell is and who rings it: not clear until the rig model.
7. **Floors 25-40 are above the clouds, Highs get sun and gold, Lows get rain and mushrooms.** Only hinted ("below the clouds", mushroom sacks, "Highs' halls"). Fine on screen visually, but no number.
8. **Keepers are iron golems.** Never said. I pictured guards (people). Matters for "the Keeper turns" at the trapdoor and "The Keepers stand aside".
9. **Nothing past the Edge is the Founders' law and nobody remembers why.** The smash is shown, the "nobody remembers why" is not. That line is a free series-2 hook (the other towers) and it's missing.
10. **Parents can't name themselves ("a cradle needs its parents").** The rule came across from Mom's line; the "cradle needs its parents" reason didn't.

## Top 5 changes (most important first)
1. **Fix the Highkeeper's jump order.** He can't watch thirty people climb out alive and then step off "never knowing" the fog doesn't kill. Move his line and his step to right after Buh lands on the Needle and the city sees the Well move, BEFORE the trapdoor opens. He falls through the fog believing he's dying; the trapdoor opens while he's under; he bursts up out of the lake to Fen. Everything else in the scene stays.

That one chant fixes: why Buh can't bring his own bucket, why "one in, one out" exists (and lets the empty rooms on Forty read as "it was never full"), why nobody ever looked under the fog, and the ages math. It's the single highest-value 20 seconds in the script.

It turns a confusing beat into a ch2 shock, makes the ch4 study scene readable (label Hollis "her dad" in his first line), and pays off at "Grandpa, no!"

Then in ch5 have Rook physically stop the Keeper at the trapdoor so Buh can pull the lever. That gives "all is lost" a real payoff and gives Rook his redemption on screen.
5. **Give Hollis an ending and clean up the small math and Minecraft wording before dialogue locks.** One silent shot of Hollis in "Weeks later" (last down the staircase with his newborn, Tilly doesn't wait). Then: Buh 13 in the cold open and "three years knowing"; "Pip's birthday" with no number; cold open "through the hole where the Well was"; "FIND an allay" not "tame"; "Rook rips the bucket out of the dispenser"; name the allay Ping on screen; and move the shears into a scene with dialogue so the one clue isn't in the most skippable minute of the movie.
