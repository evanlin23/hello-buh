## Chapter 1: You Fall Once

**1. What I think the story is so far.**
Highfall is a tower 40 floors tall (Forty is build limit) standing in a sea of fog. Once a year, on Drop Day, people jump off the Plank on some floor into a pool far below (the Well, sitting on a pillar called the Needle) with a water bucket, and the floor you jump from becomes the floor your family lives on. Three years ago Buh's big brother Ash jumped from Forty with a bucket the Highkeeper handed him; it was empty and the Well shifted one block, so he fell into the fog and everyone treats him as dead. Now Buh (15) is a gifted water-bucket clutcher, a bell rings in the fog when nobody is jumping, and an allay brings him a lodestone compass pointing at the Needle and then a name tag in Ash's eight-for-B handwriting: "Don't take his bucket." Ash is alive down there.

# FINAL REPORT (written after reading bible.md)

## The whole story, as I'd tell a friend
There's a stone tower in a sea of fog, forty floors up to build limit. Once a year the kids jump off a plank toward a one-block pool on a pillar, and the floor you land from is your family's for good. Every year one kid jumping from the top "misses" and disappears into the fog. Buh's big brother Ash was one of them. Three years later Buh, a Floor 3 kid who's brilliant with a water bucket, starts getting messages from an allay in Ash's handwriting. He and Tilly, a rich girl from the top, break into the Highkeeper's study and find out the miss is a trade: when a family is allowed a baby (the Cradle), it has to give up one of its kids, and a redstone rig moves the pool so that kid falls. Buh climbs down a ladder inside the pillar and finds a hidden green world under the fog. The old Wellwright dug a lake there, and all thirty "dead" kids are alive in it, Ash included. Ash made their mom name him so Buh wouldn't be named. This year the Cradle is Tilly's own family, the Highkeeper's, and Tilly is the one. On Drop Day Tilly dodges by jumping from Floor 1, and her dad rigs the jump for Buh instead. Buh beats the moving pool with his clutch trick, lands on the pillar in front of the whole city, and opens the hatch. Thirty people climb out. The Highkeeper confesses, jumps, and comes up alive in the lake. They build a staircase down, and there are other towers out there, ringing twice.

Great bones. It holds up as long as you don't ask how the world's rules work, and my whole job is to ask.

## Did the chapters deliver the bible?
A lot of what holds the world together lives only in the bible. Here is every rule or clue that does NOT come across from the chapters alone:

**World rules that never reach the screen**
1. **Every 16-year-old must jump, once.** The chapters never say the Drop is mandatory or tied to an age. The only hint is Ash's "four years" line in ch4, and it contradicts Buh's age (see #8 below).
2. **You get one bucket, from the Highkeeper's hand, and the Keepers take everything else at the plank.** Never stated. Without it: "why didn't Ash check / bring his own", "why doesn't Buh bring his own at the climax", and "Don't take his bucket" can't be obeyed.
3. **The fog is believed to be the void ("The Below keeps what it takes").** Never said. We infer death from Mom's curtain.
4. **Forty is "cursed"; most kids jump low.** Never said. Fen's tally implies it.

## Plot holes and contradictions
The full list, numbered with chapter, severity and smallest fix, is in my persona section below. The ones that **BREAK THE STORY**, in order of damage:
- **P1. The Floor 1 loophole.** The Named can just jump low, and nobody in thirty years did. (ch4-5)
- **P2. The Count has no reason, and the story's own clue contradicts it** (empty rooms on Forty). (ch2, ch5)
- **P3. One Cradle a year can't produce sixteen 16-year-olds a year.** (bible / ch2)

## Persona section: the skeptic's hole list
Severity key: **BREAKS THE STORY** / **CONFUSES** / **NITPICK**. Every fix is the smallest one I could find that keeps the story as written.

### A. World rules (who lives where, the yearly count, who decides, why the secret held, why nobody leaves or builds)

1. **The Floor 1 loophole.** (Ch4, Ch5) **BREAKS THE STORY.**
   The hole: "The rig only works from Forty" arrives in ch4 with no reason, and Tilly beats thirty years of murder by jumping from Floor 1. Ash KNEW he was Named and still walked to Forty and took the Highkeeper's bucket.
   Fix, two lines in ch3:
   - Ash: "The one always goes to Forty. Their family sends them: 'our family jumps from Forty', 'there's always room on Forty'. If the one jumps anywhere else, the house names another. I went to Forty so it would never be you."
   - Model scene: "The line's a hundred and fifty blocks of repeaters. It's slow. Only a fall from Forty is long enough for it to fire." That's vanilla-true and gives "only from Forty" a reason.
   Bonus: Tilly's dodge now has a cost. Her house has no other child but the newborn, which is exactly why Hollis turns to "the Buh boy". Her choice causes his attack.
2. **The Count has no reason, and the empty rooms contradict it.** (Ch2, Ch5) **BREAKS THE STORY.**
   The hole: The villain drowns a child a year to keep a number, and we never learn why there's a number. Meanwhile the Highs' halls are "full of empty rooms".
   Fix:
   - One line from Orlo or Fen: "Forty floors, no forty-one, nothing past the Edge. So the beds are counted."
   - In the heist: Tilly: "Grandpa says the tower's full." Buh, at the empty rooms: "Full of what?"
   That connects the Count to build limit and the Edge law (the title and the ending), lands the "unequal" clue, and makes "we build out now" the actual cure.
3. **One baby a year vs sixteen jumpers a year.** (Bible "Cradle"; Ch2) **BREAKS THE STORY** (CONFUSES on screen).
   The hole: If the Cradle is the only baby a year, there's one 16-year-old a year, not sixteen, and not Buh, Rook and Tilly all at once. A city can't exist on one birth a year.
   Fix: Redefine the Cradle as "the one baby a year MORE than the beds that deaths free up". Babies normally wait for a bed. The Cradle lets one family skip the wait, and the price is a name. One line in ch3. Everything else stands.
4. **Thirty years of the Well moving in public.** (Ch5; Ch1 cold open) **BREAKS THE STORY** (the secret).
   The hole: "In front of the whole city, the Well blinks... The city saw the Well move." It blinked in front of the same city thirty times before.
   Fix: Make "Don't look" (Mom, ch1) a city-wide custom. At the second ring you turn away from the one who falls, and Fen's "echo" is the official story. In ch5, Buh's shout and a boy STANDING beside the hole are what make them look. Reword it to "for the first time, someone is standing where the Well was."
5. **Why now?** (Ch3-Ch5) **BREAKS THE STORY.**
   The hole: Thirty people, a ladder and a ferryman. The climax takes one lever pull and works instantly. Why not any Drop Day in the last thirty years? Ch3's "only all thirty, at once, in front of everyone" is a plan they could always have run. The real answer, Wren's refusal, is in the bible but not on screen, and she's never won over.
   Fix:
   - Fen in ch4: "Wren made me swear. She didn't want them back."
   - A 60-second ch4 scene where Buh (or Ash, letting his brother help, which is his bible arc) wins Wren over: "Don't forgive them. Just let them see you."
   That one scene closes three arcs.
6. **Why did Fen never tell anyone there's land below?** (Ch4) **CONFUSES.**
   The hole: The instant the tower knows about a green world, the Count is pointless. Fen sat on that for thirty years.
   Fix: Covered by #5 (Wren's oath), plus his ch4 line "If they find the lake, it ends worse."
7. **The jump rules are never stated.** (Ch1) **CONFUSES.**
   The hole: Who jumps, at what age, is it mandatory, can you choose any floor? The audience has to reverse-engineer it from "the floor I jump from is the floor I live on".
   Fix: One line of kids' talk on the Floor 6 ledge: "Everybody jumps the year they turn sixteen. Once. Any floor you've got the guts for. One bucket, from his hand, nothing else."
8. **Buh's age.** (Ch1, Ch4) **CONFUSES.**
   The hole: Buh was 12 three years ago, so he's 15, but the Drop is at 16 (bible; Ash's "you'd have waited four years").
