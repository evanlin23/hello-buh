# BUILD LIMIT, draft 2.1: the one-hour arc

Draft 1 (the main tab) is the 20-minute script. This is the full movie: about 62 minutes in five chapters of about 12 minutes. Each chapter is released as its own video, with a hook in its first 30 seconds and a twist as its cliffhanger, and then they're cut together as the movie. It's a treatment, so the lines here are the key ones, not final dialogue. Two rounds of blind readers have been through it (below). The screenplay comes next, chapter by chapter, each audited before the next.

## Two blind audits, and what they changed

**Round 1 (draft 1, the 20-minute script).** Five readers who saw only the screenplay: a 13-year-old Minecraft viewer, a plot-hole hunter, a Minecraft veteran, a story editor, and a dialogue-only listener. All would finish it (7 to 9 out of 10). They broke it in the same places:
- why the thirty don't come home;
- why nobody builds out;
- a climax with no danger;
- an Act 2B with no setback;
- clues that only work if you read them.

Draft 2 rebuilt the world and the five chapters around those.

**Round 2 (draft 2, this arc).** Four fresh readers, the same personas without the listener. They rated the chapters 7 to 9 and would click every next one. Draft 2.1 fixes what they found:

| What they found | Draft 2.1 |
|---|---|
| The rules lived in the writer's notes, not on screen: the fog as the void, one bucket, Forty cursed, the Count, the Cradle's price. | Each rule now has a scene where it's said or shown (see "The rules, and where we learn them"). Chapter 1 has a five-line law chant. |
| The numbers didn't work: one baby a year can't make sixteen jumpers a year. | "Two is the Count": every family may raise two children. A third needs the Cradle, and the Highkeeper grants one Cradle a year. |
| The Floor 1 loophole: why did no named one ever just jump low? | The named one must jump from Forty, or the house must name another. And the rig's line is slow: from any lower floor you've landed before the Well moves. |
| The Well had moved in public thirty times, so why does the city only see it now? | From the floors you watch the jumper, not a dot 190 blocks down, and the one always vanishes into the fog. This time a witness stands beside the Well, and a survivor stands where it was. |
| Buh didn't own the climax: the Highkeeper chose to give him the full bucket, and Hollis caused the slide. | Buh wants the second ring, so the city sees the Well move. He forces "Show them it's full." When the floor opens, he chooses the stone over the safe drop, risking his life on a one-tick clutch. Then he opens the hatch himself. |
| The Highkeeper jumped after thirty people climbed out, yet "never knew" about the lake. | He steps off before the hatch opens. |
| The family didn't end the story: the brothers never made up and the reunion was one line. | The brothers make up in Chapter 4, and the plan is Ash letting Buh help. The reunion on the Needle has real lines, and the family has the last dramatic beat. |
| Chapter 4 was crowded, with a soft all-is-lost and a cliffhanger that didn't turn. | It's rebuilt: the lever room is planted in Chapter 2, Rook turns in Chapter 4, the loss is real, and it ends on a reveal: "Named: Tilly." |
| Iron golems can't take items, guard a lever or escort anyone. | The Keepers are the Highkeeper's guards: people in iron armour. |
| Small things: the ages, shears can't cut redstone, dispensers don't drop their buckets, a 3x3 Needle top, Hollis and Wren with no ending, the allay and compass dropping out. | All fixed (see Production notes for the mechanics). |

## Chapter 1: You Fall Once (12 min)

**Hook (0:00).** We open on the Plank on Floor 40, at night, three years ago. A Keeper pats Ash (16) down and takes his own bucket. The Highkeeper: "How many in your family?" "Four." "Which floor?" "Forty." He holds two buckets, one full and one empty. He keeps the full one and puts the empty one in Ash's hand. Ash, looking down at the drop, never checks it. We see it; he doesn't. "You fall once." Ash: "The floor I jump from is the floor I live on." He grins, and steps off.

1. **The fall, intercut (1.5 min).** POV: the toll rolls down the tower, DONG. Intercut with that night's earlier scene: Ash and Buh (13) at the note block on Floor 3. The four-note song, low, low, high, low. Ash: "Play it every night and I'll hear it from the top." Over the door, 8UH'S ROOM. Buh: "You wrote my name with an eight again." Ash: "Eights are cooler." Back to the fall: DONG, a second toll. The Well blinks one block over. The bucket in his hand is empty, held for a full second. He drops past the Needle into the fog.
2. **Floor 3 (20 s).** Buh at the window. A hand pulls the curtain shut. Mom: "Don't look." TITLE.
3. **Now: Highfall (40 s).** The establishing wide: the tower, the clouds splitting it, the Needle and its cap, the Well, the fog sea.
4. **The Floor 6 practice ledge (2.5 min).** Buh aims at the stone beside the Puddle and places water a tick before he hits. The kids lose it. The rules come out as kids' talk:
   - "From Forty it's a dot."
   - Rook: "Forty's cursed. One a year. Six days, Buh."
   - Buh: "Then I'll jump from forty-one." Rook: "There is no forty-one. That's build limit, genius."
   - A kid places a block out past the Edge to show off, and a Keeper breaks it.
   - A kid drops a block into the fog and listens. It never lands.
5. **The market (2 min).** Highkeeper Orlo comes below the clouds, with his son Hollis at his shoulder, Hollis's small boy (Tilly's brother) and two Keepers. The city chants the laws: "You fall once. The floor you fall from is your family's, forever. One bucket, from his hand. Nothing past the Edge. The Below keeps what it takes." Rook: "All three of us are moving to Forty." Orlo, smiling: "There's always room on Forty." The census: "How many in your family?" "Four." "Four?" "Mom, Pip, me. And Ash." Gently: "Three, son." He writes.
6. **Mom's stall (1 min).** Pip (3) asleep among the mushroom sacks, and the curtain still shut. Mom: "Floor 10. Real windows." Buh: "Ash jumped from Forty." Mom: "And look where it got him." Later, to a customer: "Pip was a Cradle baby. We were lucky."
7. **The ledge by the fog, night (2 min).** A thud: Tilly lands on a hay bale. "Ow. Hay bales are a lie. I'm Tilly. Floor 40. Don't tell." Her mom got the Cradle this year, due on Drop Day. "Grandpa says our family jumps from Forty. Family rule. I'm jumping from Floor 1. Imagine his face." Far off, a single bell rings, though nobody is jumping.
8. **Buh's room (1 min).** He plays the song, like every night. An allay rises out of the fog, hovers over the note block and drops a lodestone compass. It points at the Needle.

**Cliffhanger.** The next night the allay drops a name tag. Buh reads it aloud: "Don't take his... bucket. With an eight." Only one person ever wrote B's as eights.

## Chapter 2: The Seventeenth Bucket (12 min)

**Hook (0:00).** POV: Buh steps off Floor 12 in practice, switches to his bucket, and it's empty. He grabs a ladder on the tower wall at the last second. Someone emptied it, and the tag said "Don't take his bucket."

1. **Following the compass (1.5 min).** Buh and Tilly follow it to the walkway from Floor 1 to the Needle's cap: a 7x7 stone platform, the Well in its centre, Fen's hut (redstone dust and torches: a Wellwright's tools), and an iron trapdoor. An allay sleeps in the hut, unseen.
2. **Fen (2 min).** His tally marks: thirty, one a year, never two, never none, with a floor beside every mark: 40, 40, 40. "They all jumped from Forty." Fen: "It rings twice for the one." Buh: "Twice?" Fen: "Echo." He sends them off.
3. **Training (1 min).** Floor 15, Floor 20, at night, with Tilly timing him. "From Forty you get one tick. Sometimes none."
4. **The heist on Floor 40 (3.5 min).** Past the Keepers, through the Highs' halls. Tilly: "Half of Forty's empty." Buh: "Then what are they counting?" They pass a locked door, the lever room: two levers, a bell rope, a line of string running into the wall. Then the Highkeeper's study and the ledger: years, houses, "+1 (Cradle)" and "Named:". Three years ago: "Marla's house. +1 (Pip). Named: Ash." This year's page is torn out, and in the margin before it, in a different hand: "the Buh boy asks too many questions." The bucket chest: Tilly counts aloud, "Fifteen. Sixteen. Seventeen? There are sixteen jumpers." One is empty. The door opens. Orlo looks at his granddaughter for a long moment. "Go to bed, Tilly." She runs.
5. **The roof (1 min).** Buh: "Named. It's not a miss. Somebody picks." Then: "It's me this year."
6. **Night (1 min).** Buh watches Fen open the trapdoor and climb into the Needle with a sack.

**Cliffhanger.** Buh slips into the hut and down the ladder inside the Needle. Ten seconds of white. Under the fog, at sunrise: a green world, a lake at the Needle's foot, campfires, people. A voice: "Took you long enough, Buh." It's Ash (19).

## Chapter 3: The Below (12 min)

**Hook (0:00).** The reunion plays as a fight. Buh punches him: "Three years!" Then the hug. Ash: "Took me three years to get an allay to come when I call."

1. **The camp (2 min).** Thirty ones, plus kids born below. Wren, the first one: "They threw us away. I'm not climbing back up to say thank you."
2. **The rig, as a model (2.5 min).** Ash builds it in cobblestone: a little tower, a slow line down its side, a bell, a one-block Well, two dispensers and a piston. One pull: ding. Two: the Well empties, the floor under it drops away, and a new Well fills one block over. "Two buckets. One empty, one full. That's the whole trick. And the line's slow: jump from anywhere lower than Forty and you've landed before it moves." Then the flashback, slowed: the cold open's fall, and this time we watch the Well.
3. **The rule (1.5 min).** Wren says it plainly. "Two is the Count. A third needs the Cradle. The Cradle costs a name. The house names one of its own, and the one jumps from Forty, or the house names another. Parents can't give themselves." Buh, slowly: "Pip was a Cradle baby."
4. **Why they stay (1 min).** Ash: "We could dig out any day. Then our families name another, and he finds the lake and fills it. Nobody'd see us come out of the fog anyway. It has to be all of us, out of the Needle, with the whole city watching."
5. **Night (1.5 min).** Mobs at the wall. Buh fights beside Ash. The Below is beautiful, and it isn't easy.
6. **The fire (2.5 min).** The brothers alone. Ash plays the four notes. "I said I'd hear it from the top. I heard it every night. From the bottom." He laughs, then he doesn't.

**Cliffhanger.** Buh: "Why you?" Ash: "Pip." Buh: "So he picked you." Ash: "He doesn't pick. The house names one." A beat. "Mom named me."

## Chapter 4: One In, One Out (13 min)

**Hook (0:00).** "She named you. Over me." Ash: "I made her. You were thirteen; you'd have spent three years knowing." Buh climbs back up the ladder alone, furious. Fen is waiting at the top. "You were never here."

1. **Mom (2.5 min).** A real fight. "You named him." Mom: "I said take me. They don't let you choose yourself." Buh: "He's alive." Mom: "Don't." She doesn't believe him. "Go to bed." Alone, she sits at the note block and can't play it.
2. **Rook (1.5 min).** Buh corners him. Rook confesses: he emptied Buh's practice bucket, "to scare you off Forty, not to kill you." Buh tells him what Forty is paid with. Rook: "My family's moving to Forty." Then: "What do you need?"
3. **Down again: the plan (3 min).** Buh goes back down with Tilly.
   - The brothers make up. Buh: "You don't get to do it alone again." Ash: "Then don't do it alone either."
   - The plan, built as they talk. Tilly overheard her father: "Name the Buh boy. Nobody misses a Low." So someone will ring twice for Buh.
   - Buh: "Let him. Let the whole city watch it move." Ash: "If the floor opens, just drop. The lake'll catch you." Buh: "Then nobody sees."
   - Rook jumps first and stays on the Needle as a witness. Buh jumps from Forty. Fen opens the hatch the second Buh lands. The thirty wait on the ladder all night.
   - Wren votes no. Ash: "Then I go alone." Wren, at last: "Not alone."
4. **The Highkeeper alone (1 min).** Night, with the ledger. He takes shears from a drawer. Tripwire string can be cut without setting it off.
5. **All is lost (2.5 min).** The night before the Drop, Hollis comes down with Keepers. "The Wellwright is unwell." They take Fen up to 40, and with him the hatch's lever. The iron trapdoor is shut with no power, and a Keeper is posted on the cap. Then Ping comes up with Ash's message, "READY", and Buh has no way to tell them it's off. At home Mom says: "Floor 10, or you don't jump."
6. **Tilly (1.5 min).** In her father's room she finds the torn page. "Named: Tilly." It's her own grandfather's hand.

**Cliffhanger.** "Named: Tilly." Her "Floor 1. Imagine his face" was never a joke.

## Chapter 5: Thirty-Three (13 min)

**Hook (0:00).** Drop Day dawn. Mom kneels at the note block for the first time in three years. Buh climbs the stairs past every floor. The crier: "Drop Day!"

1. **The montage (1.5 min).** One toll, one splash, over and over. Tilly jumps from Floor 1 to a roar, half laughing, half outrage, and her father's face falls: now the house owes another name. Rook jumps from Forty, lands it, and stays on the cap beside the Keeper.
2. **The lever room (1.5 min).** Tilly runs up the stairs. The string is cut cleanly. Then her father, kneeling, bridging the cut. She could stop him. Remembering Buh's "let him", she says: "Do it, then. Everyone's watching."
3. **The Plank (2 min).** The Highkeeper holds two buckets. "How many in your family?" Buh: "Thirty-three. Mom, Pip, me, and the thirty you sent down." The Highkeeper's hand hovers. Buh, loud enough for the floor below: "Show them it's full." Orlo tips it: water. He hands it over. Buh steps off. DONG.
4. **The fall, in slow motion (2 min).** DONG. In front of the whole city, the Well empties and the floor under Buh drops away. A hole straight into the fog; the lake would catch him. He doesn't take it. He aims at the stone and places water a tick before he hits: the Floor 6 trick, from Forty, with one tick to do it in. Splash on stone. He lands on the cap. Rook, beside him, points at the new Well a block over and the hole where the old one was: "It moved! It's a trick!"
5. **The Highkeeper (1.5 min).** On the Plank, with the city roaring, Tilly faces her father. "You rang it. For him." Orlo looks at his son, his granddaughter, and the boy on the Needle. "My grandson was born this morning. For thirty years I made houses name one. Not this year." Tilly: "Grandpa, no!" He steps off, past the Well, into the fog.
6. **The hatch (1.5 min).** The trapdoor has no power. Rook shoulders the Keeper aside. Buh grabs a redstone torch from Fen's hut and sets it beside the trapdoor, which swings open. "Everybody! Come see where they go!" Ash climbs out, then more and more, and last, Wren. Thirty people on the cap and the walkway, waving up at the city.
7. **The family (1.5 min).** On Floor 3, Mom plays the four notes, and Ash hears them. Mom runs down the stairs to the walkway. "I named you." Ash: "I made you." She holds him. Buh and Mom look at each other: no Floor 10 now.
8. **Fen's lake (1 min).** The Highkeeper surfaces in the lake, alive, alone in the empty camp. Fen, freed by Tilly in the chaos on Forty, climbs down with Wren. "Thirty years, Orlo. I couldn't stop your bell. So I dug a lake." Orlo laughs, or cries. Both.
9. **Weeks later (1 min).** The Keepers stand aside. A staircase grows down from the Needle, through the fog, to the grass. Every floor walks down into the sun. Pip's fourth birthday: Ping brings the cake. Last of all, Hollis, alone on Forty, looking down.

**Cliffhanger (the series button).** Buh and Tilly on a hill. Buh: "Told you there was a forty-one." Tilly: "We build out now. Not up." Far away, the base of another tower rises into the fog, then another. Faint, across the world: DONG. DONG. Tilly: "The echo." Buh: "That's not an echo." CUT TO BLACK.

## Left open on purpose (each gets exactly one clue)

- **Who cut the string?** The shears (Ch4) point to Orlo, and nobody on screen confirms it.
- **Would Orlo have handed Buh the empty bucket?** His hand hovers before "Show them it's full."
- **Did Ash lie to his brother?** He promised "from the top" when he already knew.
- **Do Mom, Fen and Orlo deserve forgiveness?** The movie doesn't decide.
- **The other towers.** Do they have a Fen?

## Production notes (from the Minecraft veteran)

- **Heights.** Floors are 5 blocks apart: Floor 40 at build limit (y 320), Floor 1 at about y 125. The Needle's cap is level with Floor 1, beside the tower, so the fall from 40 is almost 190 blocks, about five seconds. The fog runs from just under the cap down about 30 blocks; the Below's ground and the lake are below that.
- **The cap.** A 7x7 stone platform on a 3x3 shaft. The Well is in the middle, with Fen's hut and the iron trapdoor over the ladder shaft on the sides. The walkway runs to Floor 1.
- **The rig, buildable in vanilla.** The lever room on 40 has lever one (the toll: a bell every five floors, wired, because a bell only carries about 32 blocks) and lever two (the Well line). The line runs through a tripwire in the lever room, which is why shears disarm it, then down a redstone staircase with repeaters set slow, to the cap. There:
  - a dispenser with an empty bucket scoops the Well;
  - a sticky piston pulls the stone out from under that spot;
  - a second dispenser under a bottom slab one block over pours the new Well.

  The renderer currently glides the Well; change it to empty-and-fill.
- **The clutch.** At about 3.6 blocks a tick, water can only be placed with the stone within 4.5 blocks of your eyes, which is one tick. About one fall in five has no valid tick at all. "A tick before he hits" is literally true.
- **The Keepers** are people in iron armour (actor bots). Iron golems can't take items, break blocks or guard a lever.
- **The allay** hovers and delivers to a played note block in earshot (about 16 blocks). It doesn't land or dance. "Tame" isn't a vanilla word: Ash "gets it to come when he calls."

## Choices for you

1. **Rook for Dace.** "Dace" sounds like "days" in a movie with a days countdown. A rook is the chess piece shaped like a tower.
2. **The climax.** Buh chooses the one-tick stone clutch over the safe drop, so the city sees. The alternative: he takes the empty bucket himself and hits the one-block Well with no backup. That's darker and more about choice, with less Minecraft skill on show.
3. **Hollis.** He's the Act 3 villain with a reason. Without him, Orlo has to ring for Buh himself, which makes Orlo's turn harder to believe.
4. **How dark the Cradle rule is.** Naming a child is the movie's engine, played as something nobody says out loud and never shown cruelly. Tell me if it's too dark for the channel.
