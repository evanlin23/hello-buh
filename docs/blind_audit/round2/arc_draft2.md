# BUILD LIMIT, draft 2: the one-hour arc

Draft 1 (the main tab) is a 20-minute script. This is the shape of the full movie: about 60 minutes, five chapters of about 12 minutes, each released as its own video with a hook at the start and a cliffhanger at the end, then cut together as the movie. Nothing here is dialogue yet. Once this arc survives a second blind audit, the screenplay gets written chapter by chapter.

## What five blind readers found in draft 1

Five readers saw only the screenplay, in four parts, and logged what they thought before reading on: a 13-year-old Minecraft viewer, a plot-hole hunter, a Minecraft veteran, a story editor, and a listener who only heard the dialogue. (A sixth, a mystery solver, was stopped by a safety filter partway through.) Every one of them would finish it (7 to 9 out of 10), and they agreed on the best moments: "Thirty-three", "Mom picked", "Took you long enough, Buh", and the cold-open fall. They also agreed on what breaks it:

## The world, fixed

- **Highfall** is one stone tower, centuries old, with 40 floors. Floor 40 sits at build limit. Your floor is your rank. Floors 25 to 40 are above the clouds and get sun, gold blocks and golden carrots; the Highs live there. Floors 1 to 24 are below the clouds: gray light, rain most days, mushroom farms. The Lows live there.
- **The fog** is a white sea under the tower, 30 blocks thick. Everyone believes it's the void. "The Below keeps what it takes": anything dropped into it never comes back.
- **Nothing past the Edge.** No block may be built out from the tower. The Keepers, iron golems, break anything that is. It's the Founders' law, and nobody remembers why.
- **The Drop.** Once a year every 16-year-old jumps from a floor they choose, off that floor's plank on the Drop Face, aiming for the Well: one block of water set into the 3x3 stone top of the Needle, a pillar standing alone beside the tower's base. Land it, and that floor is yours and your family's, forever. The higher the floor, the harder the jump: from Forty the Well is a dot 150 blocks down, and you hit it at full speed. You get one bucket, from the Highkeeper's own hand; the Keepers take everything else off you at the plank. From Forty a bucket clutch is a one-tick window. Most kids jump low. Forty is "cursed": every year, one jumper from Forty misses.
- **The bell.** A bell on Floor 40 rings once as each jumper steps off. On the one jump a year that misses, people swear it rings twice.
- **The Cradle.** The tower holds a fixed number of beds, the Count. One new baby a year, by the Highkeeper's leave, and the family that gets the Cradle celebrates. The price, which nobody says out loud: that family names one of its children to fall at their Drop. Parents can't name themselves ("a cradle needs its parents"). The named one jumps from Forty, gets an empty bucket, and the bell rings twice. Families never speak of it, because they chose.
- **The rig.** A lever room on Floor 40 has two levers: one rings the bell, and the other runs a redstone line down the tower and the Needle to its top. There, two rings fire it: a dispenser holding an empty bucket scoops the Well, a sticky piston pulls the stone out from under that spot, and a second dispenser pours a new Well one block over. Where the Well was, there's now a hole straight down into the fog.
- **The Below.** Under the fog is a whole green world. Fen, the Wellwright, built the rig thirty years ago on the Highkeeper's orders, and the same year he dug a lake under the Needle where every one of the ones has landed. Thirty people live there now, with kids born below and walls and torches against the mobs at night. Fen's iron trapdoor on the Needle's top hides a ladder down the inside. The ones stay hidden by choice (see the first fix above).

## Chapter 1: You Fall Once (about 12 min)

**Hook.** The cold open: Ash's rigged fall from Forty.

1. **Floor 3, night, three years ago (1 min).** Ash (16) and Buh (12) at a note block. The four-note song: low, low, high, low. Ash: "Play it every night and I'll hear it from the top." Over the door, a sign in Ash's handwriting: 8UH'S ROOM. Buh: "You wrote my name with an eight again." Ash: "Eights are cooler."
2. **The Plank, Floor 40 (1 min).** "How many in your family?" "Four." "Which floor?" "Forty." Ash gets a water bucket from the Highkeeper's hand. "You fall once." "The floor I jump from is the floor I live on." He grins, and steps off.
3. **POV fall (1 min).** DONG. DONG. The Well blinks one block over. The bucket in his hand is empty (one full second on screen). He drops past the Needle into the fog.
4. **Floor 3 (20 s).** Buh at the window. A hand pulls the curtain shut. Mom: "Don't look." TITLE.
5. **Now: Highfall (40 s).** One establishing wide: the tower, the clouds splitting it, the Needle, the Well, the fog sea.
6. **The Floor 6 practice ledge (2 min).** Buh doesn't aim for the Puddle (a 3x3 practice pool); he aims for the stone beside it and places water a tick before he hits. The kids go wild. The rules come out as kids' talk: "From Forty the Well's a dot." Rook: "Practice pools don't move. Six days, Buh." Buh: "Then I'll jump from forty-one." Rook: "There is no forty-one. That's build limit, genius." A kid places a block out past the Edge to show off, and a Keeper smashes it.
7. **The market (2 min).** The Highkeeper comes below the clouds with two Keepers. The laws, chanted once. Rook: "All three of us are moving to Forty." Highkeeper, smiling: "There's always room on Forty." The census: "How many in your family?" "Four." "Four?" "Mom, Pip, me. And Ash." Gently: "Three, son." In the book Buh sees an X beside Ash.
8. **Mom's stall (1 min).** Pip (3) asleep among the mushroom sacks; the curtain on the Drop Face window still shut. Mom: "Floor 10. Floor 10 has real windows." Buh: "Ash jumped from Forty." Mom: "And look where it got him."
9. **The ledge by the fog, night (2 min).** A thud: Tilly lands on a hay bale from the floor above. "Ow. Hay bales are a lie." She says her name. Her mom has the Cradle this year, due on Drop Day. "Grandpa says our family jumps from Forty. Family rule. I'm jumping from Floor 1. Imagine his face." Far off in the fog, a single bell rings, though nobody is jumping.
10. **Buh's room (1 min).** He plays the song, like every night. An allay rises out of the fog, hovers over the note block and drops a lodestone compass. It doesn't point north; it points at the Needle.

**Cliffhanger.** The next night Buh plays the song again, and the allay drops a name tag. Buh reads it aloud: "Don't take his... bucket. With an eight." Only one person ever wrote B's as eights.

## Chapter 2: The Seventeenth Bucket (about 12 min)

**Hook.** POV: Buh steps off Floor 12 in practice, switches to his bucket, and it's empty. He saves himself with a ladder on the tower wall.

1. **Floor 12 (1.5 min).** Buh corners Rook. Rook confesses to the swap: "I wanted you scared. Not dead." It's a red herring, and the tag still means someone else.
2. **The Needle (2.5 min).** Buh and Tilly cross the walkway from Floor 2 to Fen's hut. The Needle's top: the Well set into stone, Fen's hammock, an iron trapdoor. Fen's tally marks: thirty, one a year, never two, never none, and a floor number beside each mark: 40, 40, 40. "They all jumped from Forty." "It rings twice for the one." "Echo," says Fen. He sends them off. Unseen by them, an allay sleeps in his hut.
3. **Training (1 min).** Buh goes higher each night (Floor 15, Floor 20) with Tilly timing him. On the walkway, a Keeper watches.
4. **The ledger heist on Floor 40 (3 min).** A stealth set piece past the Keepers, through the Highs' halls, full of empty rooms ("There's always room on Forty"). The Highkeeper's study and the ledger: years, families, "+1 (Cradle)" and "Named:". Three years ago: "Marla's house. +1 (Pip). Named: Ash." This year's page is torn out, and in the margin of the page before, in another hand: "the Buh boy asks too many questions." The bucket chest: Tilly counts aloud, "Fifteen. Sixteen. Seventeen? There are sixteen jumpers." One bucket is empty. The Highkeeper walks in and sees Tilly. He says nothing and lets her go.
5. **The roof (1 min).** Buh: "Named. It's not a miss. Somebody picks." Then: "Then it's me this year."
6. **Night (1 min).** Buh watches Fen open the trapdoor and climb into the Needle with a sack.

**Cliffhanger.** Buh slips into the hut, climbs down the ladder inside the Needle, and goes through ten seconds of white. Under the fog, at sunrise, is a green world: a lake right under the Needle, campfires, people. A voice: "Took you long enough, Buh." It's Ash (19).

## Chapter 3: The Below (about 12 min)

**Hook.** The reunion plays as a fight. Buh punches him: "Three years!" Then the hug. Ash: "Took me three years to tame an allay."

1. **The camp (2 min).** Thirty ones, plus kids born below. Wren, the first one, Fen's daughter: "They threw us away. I'm not going back up to say thank you." The houses, the walls, the torches.
2. **The rig (2.5 min).** Ash doesn't explain it; he builds it. A working model in cobblestone: a little tower, redstone down its side, a bell, a one-block Well, two dispensers and a piston. One button press: ding. Two: the Well blinks one block over and the floor under it opens. "Two buckets. One empty, one full. That's the whole trick." Then the flashback, slowed: Ash's fall from the cold open, and this time we watch the Well move.
3. **Why they stay (1.5 min).** "We could dig out any day. If one of us goes back, our family names another. You. And if the Highkeeper ever finds the lake, he fills it with gravel, and the next one dies for real." Only all thirty, at once, in front of everyone.
4. **Night (2.5 min).** Mobs come out of the dark. The camp holds the wall, with Buh beside Ash: bows, swords, torches. The Below is beautiful, and it isn't easy.
5. **The fire (2.5 min).** The brothers alone. Ash plays the four notes on the camp's note block. "I said I'd hear it from the top. I heard it every night. From the bottom." Ash's crack shows: he laughs, then he doesn't.

**Cliffhanger.** Buh: "Why you?" Ash: "Pip." Buh: "So he picked you because Mom had a baby." Ash: "He doesn't pick. He makes the family name one." A beat. "Mom named me."

## Chapter 4: One In, One Out (about 12 min)

**Hook.** "She named you. Over me." Ash: "I made her. You were twelve; you'd have waited four years knowing." Buh climbs back up the ladder alone, furious. At the top, Fen is waiting. "You were never here." Fen lets him out.

1. **Fen (1.5 min).** Fen on his own: he built the rig because he was ordered to, and he dug the lake the same year, because his daughter was the first one. He keeps the allay for Ash. "If they find the lake, it ends worse."
2. **Mom (2.5 min).** A real fight. "You named him." Mom: "I said take me. They don't let you choose yourself." Buh: "He's alive." Mom: "Don't." She doesn't believe him. "Go to bed." Later, alone, she sits at the note block and can't play it.
3. **The Highkeeper alone (1 min).** At night with the ledger and the torn page. He takes shears from a drawer (the only clue to who cuts the line).
4. **Tilly learns (2 min).** She overhears her father and grandfather in the study. Hollis: "Name the Buh boy. Nobody misses a Low." Orlo: "That's not how it works." Hollis: "Then I'll make it work." Then the torn page, in her father's room: "Named: Tilly." It's her own family. Now "I'm jumping from Floor 1" is her way to live, because the rig only works from Forty.
5. **The plan (2 min, shown, not told).**
   - Tilly cuts the line in the lever room.
   - Rook, told the truth and sick about his "family moving to Forty", jumps right before Buh and stays on the Needle as a witness.
   - Buh jumps from Forty.
   - When he lands, Fen opens the trapdoor.
   - Ash brings the thirty up the ladder.
6. **All is lost (2 min).** The night before the Drop, Hollis comes down with Keepers. "The Wellwright is unwell." They take Fen up to 40, and a Keeper stands over the trapdoor's lever. At home Mom says: "You jump from Floor 10, or you don't jump."

**Cliffhanger.** Drop Day dawn. Buh climbs the stairs alone, past every floor, to Forty. On Floor 3, Mom kneels at the note block for the first time in three years. The crier: "Drop Day!"

## Chapter 5: Thirty-Three (about 13 min)

**Hook.** The whole city on its ledges. One ring, one splash, over and over, in a montage of jumps. Tilly jumps from Floor 1: the crowd roars, half laughing, half outraged, and her father's face falls.

1. **Rook (1 min).** He jumps from Forty and lands it, screaming. He stays on the Needle, beside the Keeper at the trapdoor.
2. **The lever room (2 min).** Tilly runs up from Floor 1. The line is already cut, cleanly. It wasn't her. Relief. Then her father, kneeling, laying redstone across the cut.
3. **The Plank (2 min).** The Highkeeper holds one full bucket and one empty. "How many in your family?" Buh: "Thirty-three. Mom, Pip, me, and the thirty you sent down." The Highkeeper's hand hovers over the empty bucket, then he gives him the full one. Buh steps off. DONG.
4. **POV fall, in slow motion (2 min).** In the lever room Tilly grabs for her father's arm, too late. DONG. In front of the whole city, the Well blinks one block over. Under Buh there's a hole into the fog. He doesn't chase the Well. He aims at the stone and places his water a tick before he hits: the Floor 6 trick, from Forty. He lands on the Needle. The city saw the Well move. Rook holds up the empty bucket that fell out of the dispenser: "It moved! It's a trick!"
5. **The trapdoor (1.5 min).** Buh is standing next to the lever. The Keeper turns; Buh pulls it anyway. "Everybody! Come see where they go!" Ash climbs out, then Wren, then more and more, until thirty people stand on the Needle waving up at the city. On Floor 3 Mom plays the four notes, and Ash, climbing out, hears them. Mom runs down the stairs and the reunion is on screen.
6. **The Highkeeper (1.5 min).** On the Plank, looking at his son, his granddaughter, and the boy on the Needle: "My grandson was born this morning. One in, one out. For thirty years I made families name one. Not this year." Tilly: "Grandpa, no!" He steps off, past the Well.
7. **Fen's lake (1 min).** The Highkeeper bursts up out of the water, alive. He never knew. Fen, freed, climbs down the last rungs and sits on the shore. "Thirty years, Orlo. I couldn't stop your bell. So I dug a lake." The Highkeeper laughs, or he cries. Both.
8. **Weeks later (1 min).** The Keepers stand aside. A staircase grows down from the Needle, through the fog, to the grass. People from every floor walk down into the sun. Pip's fourth birthday, under a real sky.

**Cliffhanger (the series button).** Buh and Tilly on a hill. Buh: "Told you there was a forty-one." Tilly: "Grandpa says we build out now. Not up." On the horizon, far away, the base of another tower rises into the fog, then another. Faint, across the world: DONG. DONG. Tilly: "The echo." Buh: "That's not an echo." CUT TO BLACK.

## Left open on purpose (each gets exactly one clue)

- **Who cut the line?** The Highkeeper's shears in Chapter 4 point one way. Tilly assumes it was her grandfather and never asks.
- **Would the Highkeeper have handed Buh the empty bucket?** His hand hovers; "Thirty-three" may be what changed it.
- **Do Mom, Fen and the Highkeeper deserve forgiveness?** Fen let mothers grieve for thirty years to keep their children alive. The movie doesn't decide.
- **Did Ash know he was lying when he promised "from the top"?** He'd already made Mom name him.
- **The other towers.** Do they have a Fen?

## Choices for you

1. **Rook for Dace.** "Dace" sounds like "days" in a movie with a days countdown. A rook is the chess piece shaped like a tower.
2. **The climax.** This draft gives Buh the stone MLG from Forty after a second ring he didn't expect. The alternative: Buh takes the empty bucket himself in front of everyone ("They don't let you choose yourself." / "Watch me.") and hits the one-block Well with no backup. It's darker and more about choice, with less Minecraft skill on show.
3. **Hollis.** A father who rings twice to save his daughter is a real villain for Act 3, but the movie gets one character bigger. Without him, the Highkeeper has to be the one who rings, which makes his turn harder to believe.
4. **How dark the Cradle rule is.** Naming a child is the movie's engine. It's played as something nobody says out loud, never shown cruelly. Tell me if it's too dark for the channel.
