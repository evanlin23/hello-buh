# BUILD LIMIT, draft 3: the one-hour arc

Draft 2.1 (`round2/arc_draft21.md`) went to two rounds of blind readers. Draft 3 applies the research report, `reports/Movie and trailer craft for BUILD LIMIT.md`, which compared the arc with real screenplays (The Truman Show, Snowpiercer, The Village, The Hunger Games, The Giver, Knives Out, Glass Onion, Coraline) and with what Minecraft audiences love and nitpick. The owner chose to keep the premise, rework it, and hold it to the bar of the really good movies, not kids' movies.

The movie runs about 62 minutes in five chapters of 12 or 13 minutes. Each chapter is released as its own video, with a hook in its first 30 seconds and a cliffhanger. They're then cut together as the movie. This is a treatment, so the lines are the key ones, not final dialogue.

## The one idea

Build limit is the only rule in this world that nobody made. The Highs dress their own laws in its authority:
- The tower is full, they say, because there is no forty-one.
- So every house may raise two.
- Nobody may build out past the Edge.
- When the Highkeeper grants a third child, someone has to go out.

Half of Forty stands empty. The shortage is real only because the law against building out keeps it real.

Buh beats that lie with a rule the game itself makes: a water bucket placed in the last tick of a fall from the top of the world. The movie ends when the city builds out instead of up.

## The rules

Each public rule is one face of "the tower is full".

| Rule | Face of "the tower is full" | Where it's said or shown |
|---|---|---|
| **The Drop.** At sixteen you fall once, from the floor you dare, into the Well, one block of water on the Needle. Land it, and that floor is your family's forever. One bucket, from the Highkeeper's hand. | Floors are scarce, so you win one with your life. | The cold open (the pat-down, the two buckets); the chant |
| **Two to a house.** Every house may raise two children. A third needs the Cradle: one a year, granted by the Highkeeper and celebrated. | No room for more. | The chant; Mom: "Pip was a Cradle baby. We were lucky."; Tilly: "Mom got the Cradle this year." |
| **Nothing past the Edge.** No block may be built out from the tower. | The law that keeps the tower full. | The chant; Kit's block, broken by a Keeper |
| **There is no forty-one.** Floor 40 is at build limit. | The excuse for all of it, and the only rule that's real. | The chant; Rook's taunt; the game refuses Buh's block on Forty's roof; Orlo's credo |
| **The Below keeps what it takes.** The fog under the Needle is the void. Forty is cursed: one jumper a year never lands, and that one gets two tolls. | Where the extra go. | Juno's block never lands; Rook: "Forty's cursed."; Fen: "It rings twice for the one." |

The secret comes out in three pieces, in this order:
1. **The lake.** A green world lies under the fog, and Fen's lake sits at the Needle's foot. *Ch2 cliffhanger.*
2. **The trick.** The one is handed an empty bucket, and the second toll opens a hole under the Well. *Ch3, from Buh's own model.*
3. **The Cradle's price.** The house that gets the Cradle names one of its own children to jump from Forty, or it names another. Parents can't name themselves. *Ch3, said by Wren during the fight, then made personal: "Mom named me."*

The research put the turns of closed-society films at about these points, and the chapter breaks already land on them:
- the ritual on screen by 12%;
- the hero commits at 15 to 25%;
- the threshold into the hidden place at 40 to 50%;
- the worst loss at 65 to 77%;
- an exit for the ending.

Draft 3 changes what happens at those breaks, not where they fall.

## Chapter 1: You Fall Once (12 min)

**Hook (0:00).** The Plank on Floor 40, at night, three years ago. Keeper Brann pats Ash (16) down and takes his own bucket. On a stand by the Plank are two buckets. The Highkeeper lifts both, and the moon catches the water in one of them.
- "How many in your family?" "Four."
- "Which floor?" "Forty."
- He sets the full bucket down behind him and puts the empty one in Ash's hand. Ash is looking down at the drop and never checks it. The hotbar shows a plain bucket, not a water bucket. We see it; he doesn't.
- "You fall once."
- Ash: "The floor I jump from is the floor I live on." He grins, and steps off.

1. **The fall, intercut (1.5 min).** The first toll: every bell on the tower at once, DONG. Intercut with earlier that night, Ash and Buh (13) at the note block on Floor 3:
   - The four-note song, low, low, high, low.
   - Ash: "Play it every night and I'll hear it from the top."
   - Over the door, 8UH'S ROOM. Buh: "You wrote my name with an eight again." Ash: "Eights are cooler."

   Back to the fall. The Well is a blue dot. A second toll, but this one doesn't ring all at once: it rolls down the tower bell by bell, chasing him and catching up. He switches to his bucket. It's empty, held for a full second. Cut to black before he lands.
2. **Floor 3 (20 s).** Buh at the window. Two floors down and across, the Needle's cap, and its blue square. For one frame the square goes dark. A hand pulls the curtain shut. Mom: "Don't look." TITLE.
3. **Now: Highfall (40 s).** The establishing wide:
   - one stone tower, forty floors, split by the clouds;
   - the Needle beside it, with the 7x7 cap at Floor 1's height and the Well in its centre;
   - the walkway from Floor 1;
   - the fog sea below everything.
4. **The Floor 6 practice ledge (2.5 min).** Buh steps off. In the last tick he places water on the stone beside the Puddle, lands in it and scoops it back up, and the empty bucket sits in his hotbar again. The kids lose it. The rules come out as kids' talk:
   - Juno: "From Forty it's a dot."
   - Rook: "Forty's cursed. One a year. Six days, Buh."
   - Buh: "Then I'll jump from forty-one." Rook: "There is no forty-one. That's build limit, genius."
   - Kit places one block out past the Edge to show off. Keeper Brann breaks it with one swing and takes a stack of Kit's mushrooms as a fine.
   - Juno drops a cobblestone into the fog. They listen. It never lands. Rook: "The Below keeps what it takes."
5. **The market (2 min).** Highkeeper Orlo comes below the clouds, with Hollis at his shoulder, little Teo holding Hollis's hand, and two Keepers. The city chants the laws as he passes:

   > One tower. One jump.
   > One bucket, from his hand.
   > The floor you fall from is yours, forever.
   > Nothing past the Edge.
   > Two to a house.
   > The tower is full. There is no forty-one.

   - Rook: "All three of us are moving to Forty." Orlo, smiling: "There's always room on Forty."
   - The census at Mom's stall. "How many in your family?" Buh: "Four." "Four?" "Mom, Pip, me. And Ash." Gently: "Three, son." He writes.
6. **Mom's stall (1 min).** Pip (3) is asleep among the mushroom sacks, and the curtain is still shut.
   - Mom: "Floor 10. Real windows." Buh: "Ash jumped from Forty." Mom: "And look where it got him."
   - Later, to a customer: "Pip was a Cradle baby. We were lucky."
7. **The ledge by the fog, night (2 min).** A thud: Tilly lands on a hay bale.
   - "Ow. Hay bales are a lie. I'm Tilly. Floor 40. Don't tell."
   - Her mom got the Cradle this year; the baby is due on Drop Day.
   - "Grandpa says our family jumps from Forty. Family rule. I'm jumping from Floor 1. Imagine his face."
   - Far off in the fog, a single bell rings, though nobody is jumping.
8. **Buh's room (1 min).** He plays the song, like every night. An allay rises from the direction of the Needle, hovers over the note block and drops a lodestone compass. It points at the Needle.

**Cliffhanger.** The next night the allay brings a name tag. Buh reads it aloud: "Don't take his bucket." It's signed with an eight. Only one person ever wrote B's as eights.

## Chapter 2: The Seventeenth Bucket (12 min)

**Hook (0:00).** POV: Buh steps off Floor 12 in practice and switches to his bucket. It's empty. He grabs a ladder on the tower wall at the last second, and the fall stops dead. Someone emptied it, and the tag said "Don't take his bucket."

1. **The permit (1 min).** Brann reports the near miss, and Orlo comes down himself. He kneels to Buh's height: "A boy who clutches like that shouldn't practise off a Low ledge." He gives him a paper named PERMIT: FLOORS 1 TO 39, and Brann "to keep you safe."
   - Mom, tight: "He's jumping from Ten." Orlo: "Then let him practise for Ten."
   - Hollis watches the paper change hands.
2. **The compass (1.5 min).** Buh slips Brann, and he and Tilly follow the compass along the walkway from Floor 1 to the Needle's cap:
   - a 7x7 stone platform with the Well in its centre, and a dispenser set into the Well's rim, facing the water;
   - Fen's hut, with redstone dust and torches, a Wellwright's tools;
   - an iron trapdoor in the stone;
   - an allay asleep in the hut, unseen.

   Buh: "Why's there a dispenser pointing at the Well?" Fen: "Keeps it topped up."
3. **Fen (1.5 min).** On the hut wall are tally marks: thirty, one a year, never two, never none, with a floor beside every mark: 40, 40, 40.
   - Buh: "They all jumped from Forty."
   - Fen: "It rings twice for the one." Buh: "Twice?" Fen: "Echo." He sends them off.
4. **Training (1 min).** With the permit: Floor 15, then Floor 20, at night, with Tilly timing him and Brann watching from the stairs. Tilly: "From Forty you get one tick. Sometimes none."
5. **The heist on Forty (3.5 min).** They slip past the Keepers and through the Highs' halls.
   - Tilly: "Half of Forty's empty." Buh: "Then what are they counting?"
   - A grate in a locked door: the lever room. Two levers, a bell rope, and a line of string running into the wall.
   - The Highkeeper's study and the ledger: years, houses, "+1 (Cradle)" and "Named:". Three years ago: "Marla's house. +1 (Pip). Named: Ash." This year's page is torn out. In the margin before it, in a different hand: "the Buh boy asks too many questions."
   - The bucket chest. Tilly counts aloud: "Fifteen. Sixteen. Seventeen? There are sixteen jumpers." One is empty.
   - The door opens. Orlo looks at his granddaughter for a long moment. "Go to bed, Tilly." She runs. Buh goes up.
6. **The roof (1.5 min).** Forty's roof, the top of the world. Buh tries to stack one more block to see over the parapet, and the game refuses. In red above his hotbar: *Height limit for building is 320.* He reads it twice.
   - Tilly finds him. "Nobody gets to forty-one."
   - Buh looks down at Forty's dark windows. "Then who made the rest?"
   - Then the thing he can't say: "Named. It's not a miss. Somebody picks." A beat. "It's me this year."
7. **Night (1 min).** From the walkway, Buh watches Fen open the trapdoor and climb into the Needle with a sack.

**Cliffhanger.** Buh slips into the hut and down the ladder inside the Needle. Beside the ladder, in the dark under the Well, he passes the face of a sticky piston and the stone block it holds. Then ten seconds of white. Under the fog, at sunrise: a green world, a lake at the Needle's foot, campfires, people. A voice: "Took you long enough, Buh." It's Ash (19).

## Chapter 3: The Below (12 min)

**Hook (0:00).** The reunion plays as a fight. Buh punches him: "Three years!" Then the hug. Ash: "Took me three years to get an allay to come when I call."

1. **The camp (1.5 min).** Thirty ones, and kids born below, among them Moss (8). Wren, the first one: "They threw us away. I'm not climbing back up to say thank you." By the fire sits Jory, who doesn't look up.
2. **The model (2.5 min).** On the lakeshore, Buh builds it himself in cobblestone from what he's seen: a tower, a string down its side, two levers, a bucket on a stand, a one-block Well, a dispenser at its rim and a piston under it. Ash watches, and only answers:
   - Buh: "Two levers." Ash: "Two tolls."
   - Buh: "Seventeen buckets." Ash: "One's always empty."
   - Buh: "The dispenser." Ash: "Drinks the Well."
   - Buh: "The piston." Ash: "Opens the floor."
   - Buh: "And the line's slow." Ash: "Jump from anywhere lower and you've landed before it opens. So it's always Forty."
   - Buh: "A bucket and a hole." Ash: "That's the whole trick."

   Then Buh remembers, slowed, from the Floor 3 window three years ago:
   - the second toll rolling down the tower;
   - the dispenser drinking the Well;
   - the stone sliding away into a black square;
   - Ash's shape dropping into it;
   - the floor sliding back and the water pouring in again;
   - and Mom's hand on the curtain.

   This time he remembers past the curtain.
3. **The gravel bank (45 s).** Moss shows off at the camp's gravel bank. He puts a torch under a column, breaks one block, and the whole column rains down onto the torch and pops into items. Buh: "That's cheating." Moss: "It's faster."
4. **Night at the wall (2 min).** Mobs come at the camp's wall, and Buh fights beside Wren. Back to back, between zombies, she says the rule as if he should already know it: "Two is the Count. A third needs the Cradle. The Cradle costs a name. The house names one of its own, and the one jumps from Forty, or the house names another. Parents can't give themselves." A skeleton's arrow; she pulls him down behind the wall. Buh, slowly: "Pip was a Cradle baby."
5. **The grave (1.5 min).** After the fight, Wren stands at a single cobblestone wall with a sign: CASS. She lays her palm flat on the sign and leaves it there.
   - "Year nine, Jory climbed back up. Walked into his mother's kitchen. That spring his house named his brother."
   - "Cass jumped wide."
   - She takes her hand away. "Nobody goes up. And if the Highs ever find this lake, they'll fill the Needle."
6. **The fire (2.5 min).** The brothers alone. Ash plays the four notes on a note block by the fire. "I said I'd hear it from the top. I heard it every night. From the bottom." He laughs, then he doesn't.

**Cliffhanger.** Buh: "Why you?" Ash: "Pip." Buh: "So he picked you." Ash: "He doesn't pick. The house names one." A beat. "Mom named me."

## Chapter 4: One In, One Out (13 min)

**Hook (0:00).** "She named you. Over me." Ash: "I made her. You were thirteen. You'd have spent three years knowing." Buh climbs back up the ladder alone, furious. Fen is waiting at the top. "You were never here."

1. **Mom (3 min).** A real fight.
   - "You named him." Mom: "I said take me."
   - The memory, played gently. The census night three years ago, with newborn Pip in Mom's arms. Orlo kneels by the stall, as kind as he was in Chapter 1. "How many in your family, Marla?" "Four." He waits.
   - Mom: "Take me." Orlo: "Parents can't give themselves." From behind the curtain, Ash (16): "Write me." Orlo looks only at Mom, because the house has to say it. She says: "Ash." He writes.
   - Back to now. Buh: "He's alive." Mom: "Don't." She doesn't believe him. "Go to bed."
   - Alone, she sits at the note block and can't play it.
2. **Rook (1.5 min).** Buh corners him, and Rook confesses: he emptied Buh's practice bucket, "to scare you off Forty, not to kill you." Buh tells him what Forty is paid with. Rook: "My family's moving to Forty." Then: "What do you need?"
3. **Down again: the plan (3 min).** Buh goes back down with Tilly. Up on the walkway, Brann watches them go into Fen's hut and not come out.
   - The brothers make up. Buh: "You don't get to do it alone again." Ash: "Then don't do it alone either."
   - Tilly overheard her father: "Name the Buh boy. Nobody misses a Low." So someone will ring twice for Buh.
   - Buh: "Let him. Let the whole city watch it open." Ash: "If the floor opens, just drop. The lake'll catch you." Buh: "Then nobody sees."
   - The plan:
     - Rook jumps first and stays on the cap as a witness, with the Lows packed on the Floor 1 walkway.
     - Buh jumps from Forty.
     - Fen opens the hatch the second Buh lands.
     - The thirty wait on the ladder all night.
   - Wren votes no. "Jory went up alone." Ash: "Then I go alone." Wren, at last: "Not alone. All of us. Then there's nobody left to name."
4. **The Highkeeper alone (1 min).** Night, with the ledger open at Tilly's name. He takes shears from a drawer. Tripwire string can be cut with shears without setting it off.
5. **All is lost (2.5 min).** The night before the Drop.
   - Hollis comes down with Keepers. "The Wellwright is unwell." They take Fen up to Forty, and the hatch's lever with him.
   - Hollis lifts the iron trapdoor and looks down the shaft. Far below, firelight moves on water. He says nothing.
   - Then the Keepers bring gravel, all night: block after block down the Needle, every tenth layer a layer of stone. From the walkway, Buh watches with Brann's sword between them.
   - Below, the thirty watch gravel rain into the lake. A mound rises under the shaft, the ladder is buried, and the shaft fills. Ash understands what it means for tomorrow: the floor under the Well will open onto gravel.
   - At dawn the Keepers shut the trapdoor, and with no lever it has no power.
   - At home, Mom: "Floor 10, or you don't jump."
   - At his window, Buh plays the song anyway. Ping rises from the Needle and drops a single torch on the note block. Buh: "They're digging."
6. **Tilly (1.5 min).** In her father's room she finds the torn page. "Named: Tilly." It's in her grandfather's hand.

**Cliffhanger.** "Named: Tilly." Her "Floor 1. Imagine his face" was never a joke.

## Chapter 5: Thirty-Three (13 min)

Cut across five places, as the escape in The Truman Show is: the Plank, the lever room, the cap, Floor 3, and the shaft, where the thirty dig upward all through the Drop.

**Hook (0:00).** Drop Day dawn. Mom kneels at the note block for the first time in three years. Buh climbs the stairs past every floor. In the shaft: pickaxe, pickaxe, a torch, a rush of gravel. Sallow the crier: "Drop Day!"

1. **The montage (1.5 min).** One toll, one splash, over and over.
   - Tilly jumps from Floor 1 to a roar, half laughing and half outrage. Her father's face falls: now his house owes another name.
   - Rook jumps from Forty, lands it, and stays on the cap beside Brann.
   - Between jumps, the shaft. Thirty people in a column, passing torches up. Ash at the top, Wren at the bottom. They hit a stone layer, then gravel again. Jory, the first thing he's said: "Pass me a torch."
2. **The lever room (1.5 min).** Tilly runs up the stairs. The string is cut cleanly. Then her father, kneeling, bridging the cut. She could stop him. She remembers Buh's "let him" and says: "Do it, then. Everyone's watching." Down the hall, in a locked room, Fen hears her voice.
3. **The Plank (2 min).** The Highkeeper holds two buckets.
   - The offer first: "Your mother on Forty. Your sister at a real window. Take the bucket, jump, and it's done."
   - "How many in your family?" Buh: "Thirty-three. Mom, Pip, me, and the thirty you sent down."
   - Orlo, who believes the fog kills, hears only grief. Then his one credo, quietly: "The tower is full, son. There is no forty-one. Someone always makes room."
   - Buh, loud enough for the floor below: "Show them it's full."
   - The Highkeeper's hand hovers. He tips it: water. He hands it over.
   - Buh steps off. DONG.
4. **The fall, in slow motion (2 min).** The second toll rolls down the tower bell by bell, chasing him.
   - On Floor 3, Mom stands at the window, then opens the curtain.
   - In the shaft, Ash's pickaxe rings on iron: the underside of the trapdoor.
   - The dispenser drinks the Well, and the floor under it slides open onto gravel. He can't take the hole.
   - He aims at the stone beside it and places water in the last tick: the Floor 6 trick, from Forty, with one tick to do it in. Splash on stone. He lands, scoops the water back, and the empty bucket is in his hotbar.
   - Rook points at the open square and the gravel in it, yelling for the walkway to hear: "It opened! It's a trick!" The floor slides shut, too late. The Lows on the walkway saw it.
5. **The Highkeeper (1.5 min).** On the Plank, with the city roaring, Tilly faces her father: "You rang it. For him."
   - Orlo looks at his son, his granddaughter, and the boy on the cap. "My grandson was born this morning. For thirty years I made houses name one. Not this year."
   - Tilly: "Grandpa, no!"
   - He steps off, wide of the Needle, into the fog.
6. **The hatch (1.5 min).** Knocking under the iron trapdoor. It has no power.
   - Rook shoulders Brann aside.
   - Buh grabs a redstone torch from Fen's hut and sets it beside the trapdoor, which swings open.
   - Ash's face, grey with gravel dust: "Took you long enough, Buh."
   - Buh, to the city: "Everybody! Come see where they go!"
   - The thirty climb out, more and more, and Wren last. Thirty people on the cap and the walkway, waving up at the city.

## Production notes

- **Heights.** Floors are 5 blocks apart. Forty's roof is the top block layer (y 319), so the game refuses any block above it with its own message, *Height limit for building is 320*. Floor 1 is at about y 125. The Needle's cap is level with Floor 1, beside the tower, so the fall from Forty is almost 190 blocks, about five seconds. The fog runs from just under the cap down about 30 blocks; the Below's ground and Fen's lake are under that, wide around the Needle's foot.
- **The Needle.** A 3x3 stone column with a 2x1 shaft down its core. One column of the shaft holds the ladder, under the iron trapdoor. The other is the drop, under the Well. The shaft opens into the lake.
- **The cap.** A 7x7 stone platform. The Well is a water source in a one-deep pit at its centre, with a dispenser set in the pit's rim. Fen's hut and the trapdoor sit beside it, and the walkway runs to Floor 1.
- **The rig, two parts, all vanilla.**
  - *The bucket.* The jumper gets a plain bucket on the Plank. Its hotbar icon is the tell.
  - *The hole.* Lever one rings every bell at once: the toll every jumper gets. Lever two sends a slow line down the tower. On 40 it runs through a tripwire, which is why shears can disarm it. It passes each bell on its way, so the second toll rolls down the tower. Repeaters at full delay make the trip last about four and a half seconds, so from any lower floor the jumper has landed before it arrives. At the cap it does two things:
    - the dispenser, holding an empty bucket, picks up the Well;
    - a sticky piston pulls the floor stone out from under the pit.

    The jumper falls through, down the shaft and into the lake. Then the piston pushes the stone back and the dispenser, now holding a full bucket, pours the Well again in the same place.
  - The reels' sliding-Well shots must be re-recorded as drink, open, close and pour.
- **The gravel.** Keepers pour it through the hatch and the open floor, with a stone layer every ten blocks. Gravel only falls straight down, so once the ladder column is dug clear, the drop column stays full: on Drop Day the floor opens onto gravel. The torch trick works on each gravel stretch, and the stone layers need pickaxes, which makes the dig a night-long clock. **Run the minecraft-audit on the pour and the dig before scripting Ch4 and Ch5.**
- **The clutch.** At about 3.6 blocks a tick, water can only be placed with the stone within 4.5 blocks of your eyes, which is one tick. About one fall in five has no valid tick at all, so "Sometimes none" is literally true. Show it as players do it: placed in the last tick, scooped straight back up, with the empty bucket in the hotbar. Don't fire water at the ground from a distance; players corrected the 2025 movie for that.
- **The trapdoor.** An iron trapdoor opens only with redstone power, so a redstone torch placed beside it opens it.
- **Ladders.** Grabbing a ladder stops a fall (the Ch2 hook).
- **Hay bales.** They cut fall damage by 80%, so Tilly's "Hay bales are a lie" is a joke about the other 20%.
- **The Keepers** are people in iron armour (actor bots). Iron golems can't take items, break blocks or guard a lever.
- **The allay** hovers and delivers to a note block played within about 16 blocks, so Floor 3's window must be within 16 blocks of Fen's hut. It doesn't land or dance. "Tame" isn't a vanilla word: Ash "gets it to come when he calls."
- **The bells** carry about 32 blocks, which is why one hangs every five floors, wired.
- **Voices.** Real, directed performances, with a different voice for each brother. AI voices are for temp tracks only: the genre punishes them.
- **The movie cut.** No chapter recaps. Each cold open must work as a standalone clip. No narrator, ever.

## Decided

The owner, 2026-11-09, on the two choices: "whatever is better". Both go the darker way, because that is the version the research backs.

1. **The grave stays.** Cass is dead: the one time the fog really kept what it took. Without it the Below is the "crummy secret" Roger Ebert punished in The Village, a reveal that makes every past danger fake. With it, the fog is still dangerous and the camp has a reason never to go up.
2. **The gravel goes under the hole.** Hollis means for Buh to die on Drop Day. The worst loss in the films that work, at 65 to 77% of the runtime, is a death or a horror the system commits on purpose: Rue in The Hunger Games, the release footage in The Giver. It also takes away Buh's safety net, so the one-tick clutch is the only way he lives, and his choice is to go to Forty knowing that.
