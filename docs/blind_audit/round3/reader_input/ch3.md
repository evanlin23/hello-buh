BUILD LIMIT is a Minecraft movie for YouTube (machinima, played straight), about 60 minutes in five chapters of about 12 minutes, each released as its own video. This is its treatment: what happens, scene by scene, with some key lines. It is not the final dialogue.

## Chapter 3: The Below (12 min)

**Hook (0:00).** The reunion plays as a fight. Buh punches him: "Three years!" Then the hug. Ash: "Took me three years to get an allay to come when I call."

1. **The camp (1.5 min).** Thirty ones, and kids born below, among them Moss (8). Wren, the first one: "They threw us away. I'm not climbing back up to say thank you." By the fire sits Jory, who doesn't look up.
2. **The model (2.5 min).** On the lakeshore, Buh builds it himself in cobblestone from what he's seen: a tower, a string down its side, two levers, a bucket on a stand, a one-block Well, a dispenser at its rim and a piston under it. Ash watches, and only answers:
   - Buh: "Two levers." Ash: "Two tolls."
   - Buh: "Seventeen buckets." Ash: "One's always empty."
   - Buh: "The dispenser." Ash: "Drinks the Well."
   - Buh: "The piston." Ash: "Opens the floor."
   - Buh: "And the line's slow." Ash: "Jump from anywhere lower and you've landed before it opens. So it's always Forty."
   - Buh: "A bucket and a hole." Ash: "That's the whole trick."

   Then Buh remembers, slowed, from the Floor 3 window three years ago:
   - the second toll rolling down the tower;
   - the dispenser drinking the Well;
   - the stone sliding away into a black square;
   - Ash's shape dropping into it;
   - the floor sliding back and the water pouring in again;
   - and Mom's hand on the curtain.

   This time he remembers past the curtain.
3. **The gravel bank (45 s).** Moss shows off at the camp's gravel bank. He puts a torch under a column, breaks one block, and the whole column rains down onto the torch and pops into items. Buh: "That's cheating." Moss: "It's faster."
4. **Night at the wall (2 min).** Mobs come at the camp's wall, and Buh fights beside Wren. Back to back, between zombies, she says the rule as if he should already know it: "Two is the Count. A third needs the Cradle. The Cradle costs a name. The house names one of its own, and the one jumps from Forty, or the house names another. Parents can't give themselves." A skeleton's arrow; she pulls him down behind the wall. Buh, slowly: "Pip was a Cradle baby."
5. **The grave (1.5 min).** After the fight, Wren stands at a single cobblestone wall with a sign: CASS. She lays her palm flat on the sign and leaves it there.
   - "Year nine, Jory climbed back up. Walked into his mother's kitchen. That spring his house named his brother."
   - "Cass jumped wide."
   - She takes her hand away. "Nobody goes up. And if the Highs ever find this lake, they'll fill the Needle."
6. **The fire (2.5 min).** The brothers alone. Ash plays the four notes on a note block by the fire. "I said I'd hear it from the top. I heard it every night. From the bottom." He laughs, then he doesn't.

**Cliffhanger.** Buh: "Why you?" Ash: "Pip." Buh: "So he picked you." Ash: "He doesn't pick. The house names one." A beat. "Mom named me."
