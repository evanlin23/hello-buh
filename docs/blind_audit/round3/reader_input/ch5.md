BUILD LIMIT is a Minecraft movie for YouTube (machinima, played straight), about 60 minutes in five chapters of about 12 minutes, each released as its own video. This is its treatment: what happens, scene by scene, with some key lines. It is not the final dialogue.

## Chapter 5: Thirty-Three (13 min)

Cut across five places: the Plank, the lever room, the cap, Floor 3, and the shaft, where the thirty dig upward all through the Drop.

**Hook (0:00).** Drop Day dawn. Mom kneels at the note block for the first time in three years. Buh climbs the stairs past every floor. In the shaft: pickaxe, pickaxe, a torch, a rush of gravel. Sallow the crier: "Drop Day!"

1. **The montage (1.5 min).** One toll, one splash, over and over.
   - Tilly jumps from Floor 1 to a roar, half laughing and half outrage. Her father's face falls: now his house owes another name.
   - Rook jumps from Forty, lands it, and stays on the cap beside Brann.
   - Between jumps, the shaft. Thirty people in a column, passing torches up. Ash at the top, Wren at the bottom. They hit a stone layer, then gravel again. Jory, the first thing he's said: "Pass me a torch."
2. **The lever room (1.5 min).** Tilly runs up the stairs. The string is cut cleanly. Then her father, kneeling, bridging the cut. She could stop him. She remembers Buh's "let him" and says: "Do it, then. Everyone's watching." Down the hall, in a locked room, Fen hears her voice.
3. **The Plank (2 min).** The Highkeeper holds two buckets.
   - The offer first: "Your mother on Forty. Your sister at a real window. Take the bucket, jump, and it's done."
   - "How many in your family?" Buh: "Thirty-three. Mom, Pip, me, and the thirty you sent down."
   - Orlo, who believes the fog kills, hears only grief. Then his one credo, quietly: "The tower is full, son. There is no forty-one. Someone always makes room."
   - Buh, loud enough for the floor below: "Show them it's full."
   - The Highkeeper's hand hovers. He tips it: water. He hands it over.
   - Buh steps off. DONG.
4. **The fall, in slow motion (2 min).** The second toll rolls down the tower bell by bell, chasing him.
   - On Floor 3, Mom stands at the window, then opens the curtain.
   - In the shaft, Ash's pickaxe rings on iron: the underside of the trapdoor.
   - The dispenser drinks the Well, and the floor under it slides open onto gravel. He can't take the hole.
   - He aims at the stone beside it and places water in the last tick: the Floor 6 trick, from Forty, with one tick to do it in. Splash on stone. He lands, scoops the water back, and the empty bucket is in his hotbar.
   - Rook points at the open square and the gravel in it, yelling for the walkway to hear: "It opened! It's a trick!" The floor slides shut, too late. The Lows on the walkway saw it.
5. **The Highkeeper (1.5 min).** On the Plank, with the city roaring, Tilly faces her father: "You rang it. For him."
   - Orlo looks at his son, his granddaughter, and the boy on the cap. "My grandson was born this morning. For thirty years I made houses name one. Not this year."
   - Tilly: "Grandpa, no!"
   - He steps off, wide of the Needle, into the fog.
6. **The hatch (1.5 min).** Knocking under the iron trapdoor. It has no power.
   - Rook shoulders Brann aside.
   - Buh grabs a redstone torch from Fen's hut and sets it beside the trapdoor, which swings open.
   - Ash's face, grey with gravel dust: "Took you long enough, Buh."
   - Buh, to the city: "Everybody! Come see where they go!"
   - The thirty climb out, more and more, and Wren last. Thirty people on the cap and the walkway, waving up at the city.
7. **The family (1.5 min).** On Floor 3, Mom plays the four notes, and Ash hears them. Mom runs down the stairs to the walkway. "I named you." Ash: "I made you." She holds him. Buh and Mom look at each other: no Floor 10 now.
8. **Fen's lake (1 min).** In the empty camp, the Highkeeper surfaces in the lake, alive and alone. Fen, freed by Tilly in the chaos on Forty, climbs down with Wren. "Thirty years, Orlo. I couldn't stop your bell. So I dug a lake." Orlo laughs, or cries. Both.
9. **Weeks later (1 min).** The Keepers stand aside. A staircase grows down from the Needle, through the fog, to the grass, and every floor walks down into the sun. At Pip's fourth birthday, Ping brings the cake. Last of all, Hollis stands alone on Forty, looking down.

**Cliffhanger (the series button).** The first house on the grass. Buh hangs a sign on its door, 41, then sits on the hill with Tilly.
- Buh: "Told you there was a forty-one."
- Tilly: "We build out now. Not up."
- Far away, the base of another tower rises into the fog, then another.
- Faint, across the world: DONG. DONG.
- Tilly: "The echo." Buh: "That's not an echo."

CUT TO BLACK.
