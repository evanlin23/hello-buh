# BUILD LIMIT, draft 3.1: the one-hour arc

Draft 3.1 is draft 3 after two things. First, the owner's questions on 2026-11-09: "i dont understand the premise, like needing to land a clutch from a certain height?" and "why would you jump if you were given an empty bucket?" Second, five fresh blind readers on draft 3 (`round3/`: a 13-year-old viewer, a plot-hole hunter, a Minecraft veteran, a story editor and a prestige-film viewer). It's still a treatment: about 62 minutes in five chapters, with the key lines, not final dialogue.

## The premise, in three sentences

At sixteen you jump once, from any floor you dare, and clutch onto the Mark, one gold block on the stone far below, with a single water bucket. Land it, and that floor is your family's home forever. Every year one kid is chosen to pay for a family's third child, and is handed an empty bucket: they know, and they jump anyway, so a brother or sister doesn't have to.

## The one idea

Build limit is the only rule in this world that nobody made. The Highs dress their own laws in its authority:

- "The tower is full. There is no forty-one."
- So every house may raise two children.
- So nobody may build out past the Edge.
- So when the Highkeeper grants a third child, one has to go.

Half of Forty stands empty. Buh beats the lie with the one rule that is real: a water bucket placed in the last tick of a fall from the top of the world. The movie ends when the city builds out instead of up.

## The rules

Each rule is one face of "the tower is full".

| Rule | Where it's said or shown |
|---|---|
| **The Drop.** At sixteen you fall once, from the floor you dare, onto the Mark, with one bucket from the Highkeeper's hand. Clutch it, and that floor is your family's forever. Higher is harder: from Forty you fall four and a half seconds and get one tick to place the water. Sometimes none. | The cold open; the chant; the Floor 6 ledge |
| **Two children to a house.** A third needs the Cradle: one a year, granted by the Highkeeper and celebrated. | The chant; Mom: "Pip was a Cradle baby. We were lucky." |
| **The Cradle's price** (known to parents who have paid it, never said aloud). The house names one of its own children. The one jumps from Forty with an empty bucket. If they won't, the house names its next child. | The cold open (Ash takes the empty bucket); Wren, Ch3; Ash, Ch3 |
| **Nothing past the Edge.** No block may be built out from the tower. | The chant; Kit's block, broken by a Keeper |
| **There is no forty-one.** Floor 40 is at build limit. It's the excuse for all of it, and the only rule that's real. | The chant; Rook; the game's own message on Forty's roof; Orlo's credo |
| **The Below keeps what it takes.** The fog under the Needle is believed to be the void. "Forty is cursed": one a year never lands, and that one gets two tolls. | Juno's block; Rook; Fen: "It rings twice for the one." |

The secret comes out in three pieces, in this order:
1. **The Below.** There's a green world under the fog and a lake at the Needle's foot (Ch2 cliffhanger).
2. **The hole.** The second toll pulls the Mark, and the one falls into the Needle and the lake (Ch3, from Buh's own model).
3. **Who names the one.** "Mom named me." (Ch3)

## Chapter 1: You Fall Once (12 min)

**Hook (0:00).** The Plank on Floor 40, at night, three years ago. Far below, on the Needle's stone cap, the Mark, one gold block, catches the moon. A Keeper pats Ash (16) down and takes his own bucket. The Highkeeper has the census ledger open.

- "How many in your family?" "Four." He writes a 4, the way you'd count heads.
- "Which floor?" "Forty."
- On the stand are two buckets, one full and one empty. He holds out the empty one.
- Ash looks at it, then at the Highkeeper. The Highkeeper doesn't look away: "You fall once."
- Ash takes it. His hotbar shows a plain bucket.
- He turns to the city below and shouts it like a joke: "The floor I jump from is the floor I live on!" The floors roar. He steps off.

1. **The fall, intercut (1.5 min).**
   - The first toll: every bell on the tower at once, DONG.
   - Intercut with earlier that night, Ash and Buh (13) at the note block on Floor 3:
     - The four-note song, low, low, high, low.
     - Ash: "Play it every night and I'll hear it from the top." He already knows. We know he knows.
     - Over the door, 8UH'S ROOM. Buh: "You wrote my name with an eight again." Ash: "Eights are cooler."
   - Back to the fall. The Mark is a gold dot. A second toll, but this one doesn't ring all at once: it rolls down the tower bell by bell, chasing him and catching up. He doesn't reach for the bucket. Cut to black before he lands.
2. **Floor 3 (20 s).** Buh at the window. Two floors down and across is the Needle's cap and its gold glint. For a breath, the glint is gone. A hand pulls the curtain shut. Mom: "Don't look." TITLE.
3. **Now. Three years later (40 s).** The establishing wide:
   - one stone tower, forty floors, split by the clouds;
   - the Needle beside it, with its 7x7 cap at Floor 1's height and the Mark in the centre;
   - the walkway from Floor 1;
   - the fog sea below everything.
4. **The Floor 6 practice ledge (2.5 min).** Kids practise clutches onto a practice mark on the ledge below, with the Puddle, a pool, beside it as a safety net. Buh steps off and places water on the practice mark in the last tick. The bucket goes empty in his hotbar; he lands in the splash and scoops it back up. The kids lose it.
   - Juno: "From Forty the Mark's a dot."
   - Rook: "Forty's cursed. One a year. Six days, Buh. Sixteen means you jump."
   - Buh: "I'm jumping from Forty." Rook: "Then you're jumping from forty-one, genius. There is no forty-one."
   - Kit places one block out past the Edge to show off. Keeper Brann breaks it with one swing and takes a stack of Kit's mushrooms as a fine.
   - Juno drops a cobblestone into the fog. It never lands. Rook: "The Below keeps what it takes."
5. **The market (2 min).** Highkeeper Orlo comes below the clouds. With him are his son Hollis, little Teo holding Hollis's hand, and two Keepers. The city chants the laws as he passes:

   > At sixteen you fall once.
   > One bucket, from his hand.
   > The floor you fall from is yours, forever.
   > Two children to a house.
   > Nothing past the Edge.
   > The tower is full. There is no forty-one.

   - Rook: "All three of us are moving to Forty." Orlo, smiling: "There's always room on Forty."
   - The census at Mom's stall. "How many in your family?" Buh: "Four." "Four?" "Mom, Pip, me. And Ash." Gently: "Three, son." He writes a 3 over the 4.
6. **Mom's stall (1 min).** Pip (3) is asleep among the mushroom sacks, and the curtain is still shut. Mom: "Floor 10. Real windows." Buh: "Ash jumped from Forty." Mom: "And look where it got him." Later, to a customer, too brightly: "Pip was a Cradle baby. We were lucky."
7. **The ledge by the fog, night (2 min).** A thud: Tilly lands on a hay bale.
   - "Ow. Hay bales are a lie. I'm Tilly. Floor 40. Don't tell. My dad's Hollis. Grandpa's the Highkeeper."
   - Her mom got the Cradle this year, and the baby is due on Drop Day.

## Chapter 2: The Seventeenth Bucket (12 min)

He gives Buh a paper named PERMIT: FLOORS 1 TO 39, and Brann "to keep you safe." Mom, tight: "He's jumping from Ten." Orlo: "Then let him practise for Ten." Hollis watches the paper change hands.
2. **The compass (1.5 min).** Buh slips Brann. He and Tilly follow the compass along the walkway to the Needle's cap: a 7x7 stone platform with the gold Mark in its centre. There's Fen's hut, with redstone dust and torches, a Wellwright's tools, and a wooden trapdoor in its floor. An allay idles in the hut.
3. **Fen (1.5 min).** On the hut wall are tally marks: thirty of them, one a year, never two, never none, and a floor beside every mark: 40, 40, 40. One mark is crossed out.
   - Buh: "They all jumped from Forty."

   - Buh touches the crossed mark. "And this one?" Fen: "That one landed." He sends them off.
4. **Training (1 min).** With the permit: Floor 15, then 20, at night, with Tilly timing him and Brann watching from the stairs. Tilly: "From Forty you get one tick. Sometimes none."
5. **The heist on Forty (3.5 min).** They slip past the Keepers and through the Highs' halls.
   - Room after room above the clouds stands empty: made beds, sunlight, dust. Tilly: "Half of Forty's empty." Buh: "Then the tower isn't full."
   - Through a grate in a locked door, the lever room: two levers, and a line of redstone running into the wall.
   - The Highkeeper's study and the ledger: years, houses, "+1 (Cradle)" and "Named:". Three years ago: "Marla's house. +1 (Pip). Named: Ash." This year's page is torn out. In the margin before it, in a different hand: "the Buh boy asks too many questions."

6. **The roof (1.5 min).** Forty's roof, the top of the world. Buh tries to stack one more block, and the game refuses. In red above his hotbar is the game's own height-limit message. He reads it twice.
   - Tilly finds him. "Nobody gets to forty-one."
   - Buh: "The world made this one." He looks down at Forty's empty windows. "Who made the others?"
   - Then the thing he can't say: "Named. It's not a miss. Somebody picks." A beat. "And he knew. Ash took it."
7. **Night (1 min).** From the walkway, Buh watches Fen lift the trapdoor in his hut and climb into the Needle with a sack.

**Cliffhanger.** Buh climbs down the ladder inside the Needle. In the dark beside the ladder he passes the face of a sticky piston, holding a block of gold from below. Then ten seconds of white. Under the fog, at sunrise: a green world, a lake at the Needle's foot, campfires, and low houses built out across the grass. A voice: "Took you long enough, Buh." It's Ash (19).

## Chapter 3: The Below (12 min)

**Hook (0:00).** The reunion plays as a fight. Buh punches him: "Three years!" Then the hug. Ash: "Took me three years to find an allay."

1. **The camp (1.5 min).** Thirty ones, and kids born below. Houses spread wide and low across the grass. Buh: "You built out." Ash: "Nobody down here to stop us." Wren, the first one: "They threw us away. I'm not climbing back up to say thank you."
2. **The model (2.5 min).** On the lakeshore, Buh builds it himself in cobblestone, from what he's seen. A tower, a lever room, a Plank, a bucket on a stand, the cap, the Mark, a piston under it. Ash watches and only answers:
   - Buh: "Seventeen buckets." Ash: "One's always empty."
   - Buh: "Two levers." Ash: "One rings for everybody. The other arms a tripwire on Forty's Plank. Only Forty's."
   - Buh: "So it's always Forty." Ash: "And the second toll runs down with you."
   - Buh: "The piston." Ash: "Pulls the Mark."
   - Buh: "And you go through." Ash: "Into the Needle. Into the lake."
   - Buh: "Why the empty bucket, then?" Ash: "So you can't clutch beside the hole."
   - Buh: "A bucket and a hole." Ash: "That's the whole trick."

   Then Buh remembers, slowed, from the Floor 3 window three years ago: the second toll rolling down the tower, the gold glint sliding away into a black square, Ash's shape dropping into it, the Mark sliding back, and Mom's hand on the curtain. This time he remembers past the curtain.
3. **Who dug it (1 min).** Buh: "Who dug the lake?" Wren: "My father. He built their bell for the Highkeeper. The year they named me, he dug this."
4. **Night at the wall (2 min).** Mobs come at the camp's wall, and Buh fights beside Wren. Back to back, between zombies: "A third child costs the house one of its own." A skeleton's arrow; she pulls him down behind the wall. Buh, slowly: "Pip was a Cradle baby."
5. **The grave (1.5 min).** After the fight, Wren stands at the camp's one grave, a cobblestone wall with a sign: CASS. She lays her palm flat on the sign and leaves it there.
   - "Year nine. The line ran late. He was on the stone before it opened."
   - "My father times it every Drop since."
   - She takes her hand away. "That's why we never go up. Go back, and your house is over the Count, and they name the next one."
6. **The fire (2.5 min).** The brothers alone. Ash plays the four notes on a note block by the fire. "I said I'd hear it from the top. I heard it every night. From the bottom." He laughs, then he doesn't.

**Cliffhanger.** Buh: "You took it. You looked right at it and took it." Ash: "If I didn't, they'd name the next one." Buh: "Me." Ash says nothing. Buh: "Who named you?" A beat. "Mom named me."

## Production notes

- **Heights.** Floors are 5 blocks apart, and Forty's roof is the top block layer. The game refuses any block above it with its own red height-limit message: check the exact wording in-game (it may say 319 or 320), and build the roof without a parapet. Floor 1 is at about y 125, and the cap is level with Floor 1, beside the tower. The fall from Forty is almost 190 blocks.
- **The clutch.** Simulated: the fall from Forty takes about 4.55 s and lands at about 3.28 blocks a tick. Water can only be placed with the block within reach, which leaves one valid tick in about 88% of falls and none in about 12%, so "Sometimes none" is true. Show it as players do it: aim at the block from the start, place the water in the last tick (the bucket goes empty in the hotbar), land, and scoop it back (a water bucket again). Never fire water at the ground from a distance.
- **The Needle.** A 5x5 stone column with a 2x1 shaft down its core. One column holds Fen's ladder, under a wooden trapdoor in his hut. The other is the drop under the Mark. The shaft opens into Fen's lake. The cap on top is 7x7, with the Mark, a gold block, in the centre.
- **The rig: one hole, all vanilla.**
  - Lever one rings every bell at once: the toll each jumper gets. A Keeper pulls it as they step off.
  - Lever two arms a tripwire strung across Forty's Plank, and only Forty's.
  - The named one's step trips it. The line holds about 2.9 s on Forty, then runs down fast, so the second toll rolls down the tower and catches the jumper. There's a bell every five floors, wired, because a bell carries only about 32 blocks.
  - At the cap, a sticky piston pulls the Mark sideways. The one falls down the drop column into the lake, and after a second the piston pushes the Mark back.
  - Shears cut tripwire string without setting it off. Tying a cut tripwire back means placing new string between the hooks.
  - The reels' sliding-Well shots must be re-recorded as the Mark sliding out and back.
- **The gravel.** Keepers climb down the ladder to the shaft's foot and pillar back up. Each places gravel under himself, with a stone layer every ten blocks, and breaks the ladder above as he climbs. A pillar, not a mound. Afterwards the drop column under the Mark is solid, so the Mark opens onto gravel.
- **The pillars.** Thirty players jump-and-place dirt up through the fog beside the Needle. The fog is the renderer's visual layer only; vanilla has none. **Run the minecraft-audit on the rig's timing, the gravel pillar and the pillar climb before scripting Ch4 and Ch5.**
- **The allay.** Ping keeps the item it was given in its hand, and carries matching copies it picks up to a note block played within about 16 blocks. Ash brings it close: Fen's hut in Ch1, and in Ch5 a pillar under the fog below Floor 3. Show Ping holding the template item. It isn't trained and doesn't sleep.
- **The name tag** is typed text, so the tell is inside it: "8UH."
- **Ladders and hay bales.** Ladders stop a fall. Hay bales cut fall damage by 80%, so Tilly's "Hay bales are a lie" is about the other 20%.
- **The Keepers** are people in iron armour (actor bots). Iron golems can't take items, break blocks or guard a lever.
- **Voices.** Real, directed performances, with a different voice for each brother. AI voices are for temp tracks only.
- **The movie cut.** No chapter recaps. Each cold open works as a standalone clip. No narrator, ever.
