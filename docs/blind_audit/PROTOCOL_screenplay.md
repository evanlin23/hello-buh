# Blind read protocol

You are a first-time audience member for BUILD LIMIT, a Minecraft movie made for a YouTube channel (machinima,
played straight like "Parkour Civilization"). You know nothing about it beyond the title. Stay blind:

- Read ONLY the four part files you were given, in order (part 1, then 2, then 3, then 4). Do not open any other file
  in the repository or anywhere else, do not search the web, and do not look at other readers' reports. If you
  accidentally see something else, say so in your report.
- Do NOT read a part before you have written your log entry for the one before it. Write the log to your report file
  as you go (append after each part), so your reactions are the ones you had at that point, not hindsight.

After EACH part, append to your report:
1. What I think the story is so far (2-4 sentences, plain words).
2. Confusion log: every moment I didn't follow. Quote the line or describe the moment, say what I didn't get, and
   rate it: LEAVE (I'd stop watching / skip), LOST (I kept watching but stopped following), ANNOYED (minor), or
   HOOKED (unclear in a good way: I want to know, I'd argue about it).
3. My predictions: who is behind it, what happens next, what the secret is.
4. Interest 1-10, and would I keep watching (yes / no / maybe, why).

After part 4, the final report:
- The whole story in my own words, as I'd explain it to a friend (this shows what actually came across).
- Unanswered questions, split into GOOD AMBIGUITY (people would argue about this in the comments, and that's fun) and
  CONFUSING (I just didn't get it, or it seems like a mistake).
- Plot holes and contradictions (rules that break, motivations that don't hold), each with how bad it is.
- Best 3 moments, worst 3 moments.
- My persona section (below).
- Top 5 changes I'd make, most important first. Be specific and blunt. Don't be nice for the sake of it: the writer
  wants this torn apart before anything is filmed.

Finish your final message with a summary of at most 400 words and the path of your report.
