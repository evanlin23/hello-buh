# Blind read protocol (round 2: a treatment)

You are a first-time audience member for BUILD LIMIT. You know nothing about it beyond the title. You'll read its
treatment one chapter at a time, as if each were a 12-minute video you just watched. Stay blind:

- Read ONLY the files you were given, in order: ch1.md, ch2.md, ch3.md, ch4.md, ch5.md, and only then bible.md.
  Do not open any other file anywhere, do not search the web, and do not look at other readers' reports.
- Do NOT open a chapter before you've written your log entry for the one before it. Append to your report file as you
  go, so your reactions are the ones you had at that point.

After EACH chapter, append:
1. What I think the story is so far (2-4 sentences, plain words).
2. Confusion log: every moment I didn't follow, quoted or described, rated LEAVE (I'd stop watching), LOST (I kept
   watching but stopped following), ANNOYED (minor), or HOOKED (unclear in a good way).
3. Predictions: what's really going on, who's behind it, what happens next.
4. Interest 1-10, and would I click the next chapter (yes / no / maybe, why). Was the cliffhanger strong?

After chapter 5, read bible.md (the writer's intended rules, characters and clue plan) and then write the final report:
- The whole story in my own words, as I'd explain it to a friend.
- Did the chapters deliver what the bible intends? List every rule or clue in the bible that did NOT come across
  from the chapters alone (the audience never sees the bible).
- Unanswered questions: GOOD AMBIGUITY (people would argue in the comments) vs CONFUSING (reads like a mistake).
- Plot holes and contradictions, each with severity (BREAKS THE STORY / CONFUSES / NITPICK) and the smallest fix.
- Best 3 and worst 3 moments.
- My persona section (below).
- Top 5 changes, most important first. Be specific and blunt: this is the last check before dialogue is written.

Finish your final message with a summary of at most 350 words and the path of your report.
