# Final report

## The whole story, how I'd explain it to my friend
"OK so there's this giant tower that's at build limit and everyone lives on different floors. The higher you live, the better. When you turn 16 you jump off whatever floor you want into ONE block of water on a pillar, and if you make it your family lives on that floor. Every year exactly one kid doesn't make it, because the tower's full: when somebody has a baby, that family has to pick someone to go. The old guy in charge, the Highkeeper, gives them an empty bucket and rings a bell twice, which fires a piston and moves the water one block, so they fall into the fog.

Buh's brother Ash was the one three years ago, and the crazy part is their MOM picked him, because Ash made her. But an allay brings Buh a compass and a name tag from the fog, and Buh climbs down a secret ladder and finds out there's a whole green world under the fog. The old guy who runs the Well dug a lake, so all 30 people who 'fell' are alive down there, including Ash.

On Drop Day, Buh walks up to the Highkeeper and when he asks 'how many in your family' Buh goes 'THIRTY-THREE,' meaning everyone he sent down. The Highkeeper gives him the real bucket, and then HE jumps instead, because his own grandson was born that morning and he's done making families pick. Buh opens the trapdoor, all 30 people climb out in front of the whole city, Mom sees Ash, everyone moves down to the green world, and the Highkeeper survives in the lake too. Then at the end you see OTHER towers far away, and their bells are ringing twice. So it's happening everywhere."

(Note: I'd explain the bell/piston and the "family picks" rule kind of wrong if I had to do it fast. Those are the parts that didn't fully stick.)

## Unanswered questions

But it's right on the edge: if there's not even ONE tiny clue, half the comments will say it's a plot hole instead of a mystery. Give us one clue to fight over.
- **Was the Highkeeper going to give Buh the empty bucket?** His hand hovers over it. Did "Thirty-three" change his mind, or was he always going to jump?
- **The other towers.** Do they have a lake? Is "That's not an echo" someone dying for real? Perfect sequel hook.
- **"He laughs, or he cries. Both."** Is he relieved or broken? Does he deserve to be OK after 30 years of this?
- **Does Buh forgive Mom?** They never actually fix it on screen. She just rips the curtain open and says "Ash." I kind of like that it's not talked out.

### CONFUSING (I didn't get it, or it seems like a mistake)

## Plot holes and contradictions (with how bad)
1. **"Just pillar up / just build sideways." HIGH (for this audience).** The whole movie is about "build limit, no room." Every Minecraft kid will say: build a second tower, extend a floor sideways, or pillar up from the Below. The ending's "build out, not up" sounds like a brand-new idea when it's the first thing any player would try. The movie needs to say early on WHY nobody builds out (everyone thinks the fog is the void, nothing to build on, a block you drop just vanishes) and WHY the Below people stayed (they'd just get sent down again, so they need everyone watching at once). Ash almost says this, but the trapdoor line muddies it.
2. **Climax setups don't pay off. MEDIUM-HIGH.** "DON'T TAKE HIS 8UCKET" plus Buh's stone MLG on Floor 6 are clearly set up for the final jump, and then he just gets handed the full bucket and lands in the water. Not a logic hole, but it feels like a missing scene.
3. **The Highkeeper's motive is muddy. MEDIUM.** By his own rule, the baby's family picks. This year that's HIS family. So why is he writing notes about Buh, tearing out the page, and hovering over the empty bucket in front of Buh? Was he going to break his own rule and kill a nosy kid? If so, that's a lot darker than "sad old man following the rules," and he's forgiven very fast.
4. **No stakes in the final jump. MEDIUM.** Buh has a full bucket AND we know there's a lake. The steer-left fake-out is clever, but I was never scared for him.
5. **Bell wire vs two levers. LOW-MEDIUM.** Ash's explanation and the lever room don't match.
6. **Trapdoor "only opens from the top." LOW-MEDIUM.** Wood trapdoors open from both sides. Make it an iron trapdoor with a lever on top, or drop the line.
7. **Pistons and water. LOW-MEDIUM (a redstone nerd WILL comment).** I'm pretty sure pistons can't push a water source block in vanilla. A sticky piston "sliding" the Well might get called out. Moving a waterlogged block, or using dispensers (one picks the water up, one places it a block over), might be safer. Someone in the comments will know for sure.
8. **The allay timing / Ash never reaching out for 3 years. MEDIUM.** One line could fix it ("Took me three years to tame an allay").
9. **The Highkeeper never noticed Fen's ladder runs in 30 years. LOW.**
10. **Dace's plan role does nothing. LOW.**
11. **Ages and who's actually jumping. LOW.**
12. **"Twenty-nine" vs "thirty." LOW.** I think it's 29 plus Ash, but say thirty.
