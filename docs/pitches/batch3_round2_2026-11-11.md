# Batch 3, round 2 premises (2026-11-11): diverge first, check later

Owner, 2026-11-11, on round 1 (docs/pitches/batch3_premises_2026-11-11.md on claude/batch3-premises): "theyre all ok,
but we should review further... i want to be more creative". This page is **premises only**, for the owner to pick
from.

## Why round 1 came out samey, and what changed
- **Round 1:** one writer pitched under every rule at once (naive narrator, vanilla twist, place link, theme,
  buildable), so almost everything became "a strange village custom hiding a mob". The cold reader guessed 9 of the
  top 10 at 70-95 % by the midpoint.
- **Round 2:** the rules came **after** the ideas, not before.

## The process
1. **Diverge.** Four writers worked in parallel, each with **one lens and no other rules**: first person, Minecraft,
   and a twist that lands in one picture. Each wrote 12 raw ideas.
   - **Lens A, strange narrators:** an allay, cat, parrot, fox, iron golem, a trader's llama, a piglin, the warden, a
     sniffer, a bee, an axolotl, a horse.
   - **Lens B, player habits seen from the other side:** trading halls, AFK farms, cure discounts, name tags, map
     art, nether-roof travel, the dragon, sorters, mob farms, strip mining, statues.
   - **Lens C, game systems as mysteries:** chunk loading, spawn points, parrots, despawning, the day counter, death
     messages, hardcore, the world border, other players, lag.
   - **Lens D, moods we haven't done:** laugh-out-loud funny, wonder, eerie but not deadly, sweet with no tragedy.
   - **Seeds:** the lead added 5 ideas (S1-S5), judged like the rest.
2. **Cold-read gate (53 ideas).** Two fresh readers, each a long-time Java player, split the ideas at random. Each
   reader heard only the narrator, line 1 and three midpoint lines, then guessed the twist and said how sure it was.
   It was then shown the real ending and said whether it was right, surprised and satisfied, gave a share score, and
   wrote a one-sentence read.
   - **Kept:** ideas guessed at about 50 % or less, or where the reader was sure but wrong.
   - **Result:** 11 of 53 passed.
3. **Then the checks, on the survivors only:**
   - vanilla, on minecraft.wiki (Java 1.21);
   - the house rules (STANDARDS 0, 0a, 1, 4b, 5a), with naivety allowed from an animal narrator;
   - a place in docs/world/canon.md and map.json (latest main, with the batch-2 lore pass);
   - the theme, or another mood that fits the world;
   - engine needs, against renderer/ and docs/engine_features.md (`node scripts/mob_audit.js`);
   - the story-critic in pitch mode, against light_sleeper and runaway. It also scored two ideas the gate killed
     (C2, D3), as wild-card candidates.
4. **Not repeated:** batch 2's shapes (the claude/script-* branches) and round 1's eight.

## The honest headline
- **The divergence worked on variety.** The 53 ideas include no "village custom hiding a mob". The survivors are told
  by an allay, a baby piglin, a sniffer, a bee, a turtle, a sheep and a cat. Four are funny or light.
- **The gate was hard.** Minecraft players guess mechanic twists fast: 42 of 53 were guessed at 55-95 %. Many of the
  readers' favorite endings were guessed and still loved, because the reveal added a second layer the reader didn't
  see: the arrow from the day he left (C2, 10/10), the 41 hidden creepers (C1), and the map art that is a proposal
  (B6). The gate killed them all the same.
- **None of the 13 the critic scored is at the runaway / Light Sleeper bar.** A7 develops as written, and A1 develops
  after a one-line fix. The critic would park ranks 3-6 and kill ranks 7-8. Ranks 7 and 8 are on the list for
  variety (funny and sweet), not because they're ready.
- **A new sameness to watch:** D4, D6, D10 and D12 all turn on "the giant was secretly kind". Take one forward, not
  four.
- **The chunk-freeze idea (S3, C2) clashes with the canon.** If chunks froze whenever no player was near, Hushwell's
  villagers couldn't live 900 days without one (Dog on the Hill). It is also the never-made Same Spot pilot's rule,
  which canon conflict 17 says is not canon. Both are wild cards: they need a canon call from the owner.
