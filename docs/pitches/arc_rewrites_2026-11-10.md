# Arc rewrites (2026-11-10)

Five of our best stories reshaped to the templates in `docs/story_arcs.md`, written to ~5 words/s. The pace comes from short sentences and refrains, not from speeding up the take. The mystery test stays: a question by 3 s, a guess at the midpoint, the game reveals, cut within ~1 s.

These are **line drafts**. The approved story.json files keep their current lines until the owner picks; the shots then follow the lines. Nothing is voiced. `[brackets]` are the picture.

## Notes for the critic
- **Light Sleeper L05** (Mom) is 7 words: "Walk SOFT, sweetie. The ground's a light SLEEPER." It's the owner's favourite plant, so
  it's kept as an exception for the critic to rule on. The 6-word version is "Walk SOFT. The ground's ASLEEP." (it
  loses "light sleeper").
- **Word counts are 114-150, under the model's 196.** We don't pad (the mystery test says "nothing that doesn't move
  the question"). The refrains add rhythm where a beat needed it.
