## Draft lines
The text below is exactly what the cold reader read. The `---` marks the midpoint, where they stopped to guess.

## A
N: Every SHEEP in our village is BLUE. All SIXTY of them.
N: Grandpa dyes them every MORNING. He buys every blue dye the traders have.
Grandpa: They're our EYES, kid.
N: Last spring, ONE came back from the woods RED.
N: Grandpa sat at the GATE with his crossbow for a WEEK.
N: That night I heard something SNAP in the trees. Like TEETH.

## B
N: My grandma keeps FORTY-ONE cats on the WALL. All facing OUT.
N: Not ONE is allowed INSIDE. Not even when it SNOWS.
Grandma: They keep the QUIET ones out.
N: She got the first one the week I CAME here. Now there's a cat every three BLOCKS.
N: Every night she COUNTS them. If one's missing, nobody SLEEPS.
N: Once I heard a HISS right under my window. Then a cat turned its HEAD, and it STOPPED.

## C
N: Every time I open my WINDOW, the bell outside RINGS.
N: THREE HUNDRED nights. Nobody's ever THERE.
N: When it rings, everyone in the village RUNS inside.
Mom: Shutters SHUT, honey.
N: Every morning there's an ARROW in the bell post. Mom pulls it OUT. She thinks I don't SEE.
N: Up on the hill there's an old OAK. Something WHITE stands under it all day.

## D
N: My uncle buys FOUR HUNDRED BEDS a year. He's never SLEPT in one.
Uncle: Best sleep of your LIFE, kid.
N: Every morning he carries ONE down to the CELLAR.
N: Every night he comes back up BLACK with soot. And half DEAF.
N: The cellar door GLOWS purple at night.

## E
N: Every MORNING, the tower on our hill is one floor TALLER.
N: Two hundred FLOORS. I've never seen who BUILDS it.
Mom: He comes while you SLEEP.
N: At the top there's a chest. Every morning, a COOKIE, and a sign: FOR THE TOP.

---
## A (continued)
N: Today my LAMB got out. Grandpa was ASLEEP.
N: I followed her into the WOODS. It was getting DARK.
N: She stopped. She turned around.
[The blue lamb turns red in a puff of swirling particles. Behind it, between the trees, a grey robed villager-like figure (an evoker) lowers its raised arms. A line of steel jaws snaps up out of the ground toward the camera. Cut.]

## B (continued)
N: Tonight it was SO cold. So I took the cat by my window up to my BED.
N: She purred. I fell ASLEEP.
N: Then I heard it AGAIN.
[Outside: the gap in the wall where that cat used to sit. A creeper walks through the gap, up to the window, and starts to swell and flash, hissing. The cat sleeps on the bed. Cut.]

## C (continued)
N: Tonight I opened the shutters and LEANED out, to catch whoever it IS.
[From behind the old oak on the hill: a skeleton draws its bow at the lit window. The arrow flies, and hits the bell hanging between the skeleton and the window: DING. Doors slam all over the village. In the window, the kid. The skeleton nocks another arrow at him. Cut.]

## D (continued)
N: Tonight I followed him DOWN. Through the purple. Into the HEAT.
[The Nether: red rock, lava. Uncle sets a bed against the rock wall, steps behind a block, and lies on it: the bed EXPLODES. In the crater, a dark swirled block (ancient debris). Uncle turns, sooty, sees the kid, and grins. Cut.]

## E (continued)
N: My dad works NIGHTS. I haven't seen him since my BIRTHDAY.
N: Tonight I stayed UP. All night.
[Chat: "Dad joined the game". On top of the tower a figure places one more block, then looks down at the kid and waves. Chat: "Dad: you're up LATE, kiddo". Cut.]
