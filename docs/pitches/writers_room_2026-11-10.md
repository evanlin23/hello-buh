## Twist fixes
1. **Broken Rail:** show the pig stuck in its cart by 8 s, misread ("he loves that cart"). Show why nobody walked the line (Grandpa believes she left). End with the kid laying rail over the crater and her cart rolling into the buffer stop: a wordless last frame.
2. **Names in the Woods:** plant the cure. Mom takes golden apples and a splash of weakness "to the neighbours in the city". ROSA walks out holding the apple, and the kid picks up the potion. Keep the first zombies far and backlit.
3. **Goat Horn:** plant why Dad must be callable (a pack and a map: he leaves for the mines). The kid dodges, the goat hits the oak, and "Goat Horn" drops in a big caption. Last beat: he blows it and Dad turns on the far cliff.
4. **Slow-Falling Grandma:** the only way up is a soul-sand bubble column, shown in shot 1 as "Grandpa's fountain". Turn: Grandpa knows and brews her slow falling at night. Reveal: caught at the brewing stand.
5. **Scarecrow:** snowball splats on my window each morning, misread as someone throwing at me. Reveal, a wide night shot: the golem pelting zombies off my wall. Stalker becomes guard.
6. **Gold for the Portal:** a piglin comes through wearing Grandpa's gold helmet (piglins wear gold). Needs the armour layer.
7. **Nine Thousand Torches:** drop the trading hall for an answer that isn't a cage, or give the image to wall_grows.
8. **Lantern Bay:** rebuild on the true rule. Drowned spawn only in dark water, so the lit bay is a safe path. The reveal is what Grandma swims to.

## Top 3 against runaway
- **Broken Rail:** the cart rolling home as one wordless picture, with the kid's act causing it.
- **Names in the Woods:** hide the turn until ROSA, so no player calls it at frame 1.
- **Goat Horn:** a turn, not a reason: the horn must pay off in the last frame.

Only three develop. Ranks 4-8 need their fixes before scripting.
