# Mystery pitches, 2026-11-10 (writers' room, `claude/writers-mystery`)

The brief is the owner's mystery test (STANDARDS 1.0; the reasons are in `docs/story_research_mystery.md`). It
needs four things. First, a question to the viewer in the first 3 s. Second, at the midpoint a guess but no
certainty. Third, the reveal shown by the game. Fourth, a cut within ~1 s of it. The bars are light_sleeper and
runaway.

Every pitch is in the shared world (`docs/world/canon.md`) and stands alone. It has a first-person kid narrator,
and other characters get one line of six words or fewer. Buh is never named aloud; his name appears only in
in-game text.

Each pitch below gives:
- **Line 1**, said in the first second, and **Q**, the viewer's question at 3 s;
- the **plant**: one character line that is literally true and heard as something else;
- the **clues** a rewatch catches (P = picture, L = line);
- the **bomb**: the second a player can guess and a non-player knows something's there;
- the **want**: what the narrator chooses to do;
- the **reveal**: what the game itself shows;
- the **cut**: where the Short ends;
- **one sentence**: what a viewer should be able to say after the cut;
- **rule**: the vanilla rule the twist uses, and **risk**.

Buildability was checked against `renderer/entity_models.json` and `docs/engine_features.md`. Goat, camel,
axolotl, breeze and creaking are not drawn; the warden and evoker fangs are. Places and days were checked
against `node scripts/world.js list` and `docs/world/survey.json`. That survey has no woodland mansion in range
and no dark forest; it does have 6 igloos, 2 monuments, 7 ruined portals and 12 trial chambers.

## The twenty

### 1. Red Sheep
- **Place, day:** the woods east of Ravenford, day 2100.
- **Narrator:** kid Buh, about 12, living with Grandpa (Ravenford).
- **Line 1:** "Every SHEEP in our village is BLUE. All SIXTY of them."
- **Q:** why tf are all the sheep blue?
- **Plant:** Grandpa: "Sheep see things we DON'T, kid."
- **Clues:**
  - Grandpa dyes them every morning and buys blue dye from every trader (P);
  - a heap of red wool behind the barn that grows (P);
  - "Last spring ONE came back RED. Grandpa barred the doors for a WEEK." (L);
  - a snapping sound in the trees that night (sound).
- **Bomb (~50 %):** the red sheep. Players think of the evoker's "wololo"; non-players know something red is in
  the woods.
- **Want:** his favourite blue lamb wanders into the woods, and he goes after it at dusk.
- **Reveal:** in the trees, the lamb turns from blue to red in a puff of spell particles. Behind it an evoker
  lowers its arms, and fangs snap out of the ground in a line toward him.
- **Cut:** on the fangs, the evoker's face behind them.
- **One sentence:** an evoker turns blue sheep red, so Grandpa's blue sheep are an alarm.
- **Rule:** evokers turn blue sheep red (vanilla).
- **Risk:**
  - Evokers only come from mansions or raids. Canon fits: one raider left over from Ravenford's raids (day 1900),
    never explained.
  - Spell-particle colour needs an engine check.

### 2. The Shortcut
- **Place, day:** a new homestead on the Green's west plain; Dad's mine is in the White. Day 2000.
- **Narrator:** a kid (a player), not Buh.
- **Line 1:** "My dad has DIED two thousand times. Always at SUNSET."
- **Q:** why tf does his dad die every single sunset?
- **Plant:** Dad: "Took the SHORTCUT, kiddo." (every night at dinner)
- **Clues:**
  - the chat at sunset: "Dad fell from a high place" (P);
  - his mine is "a THREE-DAY walk" away, yet he's home for dinner every night (L);
  - he never brings his pickaxe home (P: the empty hands);
  - "Don't TOUCH my bed." (L).
- **Bomb (~55 %):** "Mom moved his bed ONCE. He didn't come home for three DAYS." Players: the spawn point.
- **Want:** he hides in Dad's room at sunset to find out.
- **Reveal:** the chat line appears, then Dad pops into his bed out of thin air, sits up and sniffs dinner.
- **Cut:** on Dad sitting up in the bed, one beat.
- **One sentence:** Dad dies at the mine on purpose so he respawns at home in time for dinner.
- **Rule:** you respawn at your bed; death as fast travel (players really do it).
- **Risk:** "dying on purpose" out of context. Keep it bright and goofy; the death is off screen, and only the
  chat line shows it.

### 3. Struck
- **Place, day:** a new cove hamlet east of Hushwell (the coves are empty land), day 2200, a thunderstorm.
- **Narrator:** a villager family's kid (not Buh).
- **Line 1:** "A WITCH has stood at my grandma's DOOR for THREE HUNDRED nights."
- **Q:** why tf does a witch wait at grandma's door?
- **Plant:** Grandpa: "She knows the WAY home."
- **Clues:**
  - "Grandma went out in the big STORM for the sheep." (L);
  - the witch came the next morning (L);
  - the witch never throws a potion at Grandpa (P; vanilla: witches only attack players);
  - Grandpa leaves Grandma's door open (P);
  - Mom: "Never stand under the OAK in a storm." (L, cut if over budget).
- **Bomb (~50 %):** the storm and the witch on the same night. Was Grandma taken, or is the witch Grandma? Players
  know the rule; non-players are split.
- **Want:** the next storm, he takes Grandpa's sword to chase the witch away.
- **Reveal:** Grandpa walks past him in the rain, out to the witch under the oak, and stands beside her. Lightning
  hits Grandpa, and in a flash he's a witch.
- **Cut:** on two witches side by side at the door in the rain.
- **One sentence:** lightning turns villagers into witches; the witch was Grandma, and Grandpa went to her.
- **Rule:** a villager struck by lightning becomes a witch.
- **Risk:**
  - It's sad; a lightning bolt render is needed (the eerie pitches say lightning renders).
  - Grandpa's choice is a character's, not the narrator's.

### 4. Forty-One Cats
- **Place, day:** a snowy village in the White at (-1585, -692) (unclaimed), day 2400.
- **Narrator:** a kid staying with Grandma.
- **Line 1:** "My grandma has FORTY-ONE cats. Not ONE is allowed INSIDE."
- **Q:** why tf can't any of the 41 cats come in?
- **Plant:** Grandma: "They keep the QUIET ones out."
- **Clues:**
  - every cat sits on the wall, evenly spaced, facing OUT (P);
  - craters all around outside the wall, none inside (P);
  - Grandma counts the cats every evening (P/L);
  - at night a hiss, then nothing, as a cat turns its head (sound).
- **Bomb (~55 %):** the hiss under the wall. Players say creepers; non-players say "something's outside".
- **Want:** it's freezing, and he wants a cat on his bed (cats sleep on beds), so he lures the cat by his window
  inside with fish.
- **Reveal:** that night a creeper walks through the gap in the cats toward his window. The cat on his bed jumps to
  the sill, and the creeper turns and RUNS.
- **Cut:** on the creeper fleeing from one cat.
- **One sentence:** creepers are scared of cats, so the cats are the wall.
- **Rule:** creepers flee cats and ocelots.
- **Risk:** the shape is close to Light Sleeper (a custom, the kid breaks it, a mob). The mechanic is different.
  The ending is relief, not a death.

### 5. The Cabbage Patch
- **Place, day:** a new place at world spawn (128, 70, 16), outside the surveyed land. Day 3100.
- **Narrator:** a kid (a player) raised by Grandma (a villager).
- **Line 1:** "My grandma has found NINE kids in her CABBAGE patch."
- **Q:** where tf do the kids in grandma's cabbages come from?
- **Plant:** Grandma: "Everyone starts SOMEWHERE, sweetie."
- **Clues:**
  - every kid shows up with nothing, starving (L);
  - all of them look alike: the default skins (P);
  - "She says she found ME there too." (L);
  - every compass the trader sells points at the cabbages (P: a compass needle).
- **Bomb (~50 %):** the compass. Players: world spawn. Non-players: the garden is special.
- **Want:** he camps in the cabbages to see a kid arrive.
- **Reveal:** the chat reads "Nina joined the game", and a small kid blinks into being among the cabbages, looking
  around. Grandma walks out with bread.
- **Cut:** on Grandma holding out the bread.
- **One sentence:** Grandma lives on the world spawn and raises every new player who appears, him included.
- **Rule:** new players spawn at world spawn; compasses point to it.
- **Risk:**
  - Players may get it from line 3.
  - It must not touch Buh's origin, which canon keeps as an open question.

### 6. The Neighbor
- **Place, day:** a stilt house over the ocean monument at (95, -465), day 2700.
- **Narrator:** a kid at Grandpa's.
- **Line 1:** "Every MINUTE, a giant GHOST FISH screams in my FACE."
- **Q:** what tf is the ghost fish?
- **Plant:** Grandpa: "Ignore the NEIGHBOR."
- **Clues:**
  - the house stands on stilts over dark water (P);
  - Grandpa takes all day to break one block, "old ARMS" (P/L);
  - prismarine in the walls (P);
  - the water glows under the floor at night (P).
- **Bomb:** the slow mining. Players: mining fatigue, so an elder guardian.
- **Want:** he dives to meet the neighbor.
- **Reveal:** the monument below, and an elder guardian turns its eye up at him.
- **Cut:** on the eye.
- **One sentence:** the house sits on an ocean monument, and the elder guardian curses everyone above it.
- **Rule:** an elder guardian's curse (mining fatigue and the ghost face).
- **Risk:**
  - It answers *what*, not *why*: why does Grandpa live there? That's unanswered.
  - The ghost-face overlay may need an engine feature.

### 7. Four Hundred Beds
- **Place, day:** Uncle's cabin by the ruined portal at (-1070, -457) in the White, day 2350.
- **Narrator:** kid Buh, about 12, visiting.
- **Line 1:** "My uncle buys FOUR HUNDRED BEDS a year."
- **Q:** why tf would anyone need 400 beds?
- **Plant:** Uncle: "Best sleep of your LIFE."
- **Clues:**
  - he comes home black with soot, carrying dark scraps (P: netherite scrap);
  - he never sleeps in a bed himself (L);
  - his ears ring: "Speak UP." (L);
  - the cellar door glows purple (P).
- **Bomb (~50 %):** he carries a bed into the purple glow. Players: beds explode in the Nether.
- **Want:** "Best sleep of your life", so he sneaks down with a bed for a nap.
- **Reveal:** the Nether; he places the bed and lies down. BOOM. The death screen reads "Buh was killed by
  [Intentional Game Design]".
- **Cut:** on the death screen (put in the caption track too).
- **One sentence:** beds blow up in the Nether, so his uncle's beds are bombs for mining.
- **Rule:** beds explode in the Nether and the End; players mine ancient debris with them.
- **Risk:**
  - Comedy, not dread.
  - Buh gets an uncle (a new canon fact).
  - The death screen's small text must be readable at 300 px.

### 8. The Midnight Bell
- **Place, day:** Saltmouth, day 2000.
- **Narrator:** a Saltmouth kid.
- **Line 1:** "Our BELL rings ONCE every night at MIDNIGHT. Nobody's ever THERE."
- **Q:** who tf rings the bell?
- **Plant:** Mom: "Your sister's BUSY, honey."
- **Clues:**
  - an arrow in the bell post every morning, which Mom pulls out (P);
  - one light in the old tower on the hill (P);
  - the sister left after a fight: "she was the best SHOT in the village" (L);
  - everyone runs inside when it rings (P).
- **Bomb:** the arrow in the post.
- **Want:** he stays up to catch the ringer.
- **Reveal:** zombies come out of the fog. An arrow flies out of the dark, DING, the villagers dash inside, and the
  doors shut in the zombies' faces. The camera runs the arrow's line back to the tower: his sister, bow up.
- **Cut:** on the sister.
- **One sentence:** his sister rings the bell with an arrow every night to send the village inside before the
  zombies come.
- **Rule:** an arrow rings a bell, and a ringing bell sends villagers indoors.
- **Risk:** the hidden-guard shape again (scarecrow). The sister's motive for hiding is thin.

### 9. Three Thousand Lecterns
- **Place, day:** a West March shore village, day 2300.
- **Narrator:** a kid at Grandpa's.
- **Line 1:** "My grandpa has broken the same LECTERN three thousand times."
- **Q:** why tf does grandpa keep breaking the lectern?
- **Plant:** Grandpa, reading each book: "Not THAT one."
- **Clues:**
  - he stares at an island every evening (P);
  - "He won't get in a BOAT. Not since the storm." (L);
  - leather boots by the door (P);
  - the librarian's trades flash past (P).
- **Bomb:** he wants one book. Players: he's rerolling for an enchantment.
- **Reveal:** the trade screen shows Frost Walker. He walks onto the sea, and ice forms under every step toward the
  island.
- **Cut:** on the ice path across the sea.
- **One sentence:** he rerolled the librarian for Frost Walker so he could walk to Grandma's island.
- **Rule:** librarian rerolls; Frost Walker.
- **Risk:**
  - "Why not build a bridge?" is unanswered.
  - Boots aren't drawn, but the ice is.
