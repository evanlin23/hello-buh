## Why the rest die, in one line each
- **Struck:** it's the third lightning-witch pitch (Lightning Rods in the absurd and heart rooms, Monsters Nearby in
  eerie). In vanilla, lightning doesn't seek oaks, so Grandpa can't choose to be struck. And "an old man walks into
  the lightning to join his dead wife" reads as suicide out of context.
- **Shortcut:** the eerie critic killed its twin (Loot at the Cliff) because it reads as self-harm. "My dad has
  DIED two thousand times" plus "fell from a high place", on purpose, is that read again. Bed in the Square already
  uses respawn as the way home.
- **Cabbage Patch:** it's Spawn Room (absurd, pick 2) almost line for line ("Everybody starts SOMEWHERE"). It also
  answers Buh's origin, which canon keeps open.
- **Four Hundred Beds:** it's Bed Anywhere (killed), and it ends on a meme death screen. The why-test kills it too:
  why does a kid carry a bed through a portal to take a nap, with 400 beds upstairs?
- **Neighbor / Stone / Glass / Upside-Down:** The Face (killed), Light Sleeper twice, and the absurd room's #15.
- **Tail 12-20:** as the writer said.

## Top 6: the fix, and what "why?" catches
1. **Red Sheep.**
   - **Fix:** make the plant literally true. "Sheep see things we DON'T" is mysticism, not a clue. Use Grandpa:
     "They're our EYES, kid.", heard as sentiment. Then cut on the fangs bursting out of the ground toward the
     camera, with the red lamb in frame.
   - **Why?** First, a heap of red wool that keeps growing contradicts "Last spring ONE came back RED": pick one, the
     single red sheep. Second, canon's Grandpa (Thirty-One Raids, and fighting beside Buh since raid 32) wouldn't
     "bar the doors for a week" because of one evoker. Write "Grandpa sat at the gate with his crossbow for a WEEK."
     Third, why does Buh go into the woods alone at dusk? Show Grandpa asleep, or have him say no.
   - **Engine:** a sheep changing colour on screen, and the spell particles, both need checking before anything
     else.
2. **Forty-One Cats.**
   - **Fix:** cut the craters, so the hiss at ~50% is the only bomb.
   - **Why?** Creepers only hunt players. If the narrator is a player and Grandma is a villager, the whole cat wall
     exists for HIM. That's the runaway move (the answer is about the narrator), so make it the turn. Line 1: "My
     grandma keeps FORTY-ONE cats on the WALL. All facing OUT."
   - **Why?** The ending undercuts the rule. If one cat on the sill saves him, why can't cats come inside? End
     instead on the creeper coming through the gap he made, while his cat sleeps on the bed. Cut on the hiss.
3. **Three Thousand Lecterns.**
   - **Fix:** put the island's stakes on screen in shot 1 (a lit window on it, and Grandma there), and make the
     reveal the ice path forming. The trade screen's small text can't carry it.
   - **Why?** "Why not build a bridge?" is unanswered, and in Minecraft a bridge takes a minute. Without an answer
     on screen, it dies.
   - **Engine:** boots on players aren't drawn, so Frost Walker has no visible cause.
4. **Four Hundred Beds.** Only revivable as Uncle's reveal rather than Buh's nap. Drop "[Intentional Game Design]".
   It's still comedy, under the owner's "serious".
5. **Shortcut.** Not fixable without dropping the deaths on purpose, and those are the twist.
6. **Midnight Bell.**
   - **Fix:** the arrow-rings-the-bell, bell-sends-villagers-in mechanic is real and good. The sister is the
     problem: "Your sister's BUSY, honey" names the answer at ~20%.
   - **Why?** Why does she hide in the tower instead of coming home? Nothing on screen says. It's also the
     hidden-guard shape a fourth time (Scarecrow, Phantom Roof, Monsters Nearby).
