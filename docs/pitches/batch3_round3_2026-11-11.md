# Batch 3, round 3 premises (2026-11-11): moods only

Owner, 2026-11-11, on the batch-3 premises: "do whatever you think is the best". The lead asked for a round 3 from the
**moods lens only** (lens D), the lens readers couldn't predict in round 2
(docs/pitches/batch3_round2_2026-11-11.md on claude/batch3-round2). This page is **premises only**, for the owner to
pick from. Process rule: r:rbf6467 (diverge first, then the cold-read gate, then the checks and the critic).

## The process
1. **Diverge.** Four writers worked in parallel, all on lens D, each with one sub-mood and no other rules than
   "first-person Minecraft, a twist that lands in one picture". Each wrote 12 raw ideas.
   - **F, laugh-out-loud funny.**
   - **W, wonder and awe.**
   - **E, eerie, but nobody dies.**
   - **S, sweet, with no sadness.**
   - Each writer saw only a list of what's taken: published and planned stories, batch 2's scripts, batch 3's four
     picks, and round 2's 53 ideas. Two answer shapes were banned for this round, from the critic's round-2 notes:
     - "the player left";
     - "the giant was secretly kind".
2. **Cold-read gate (48 ideas), as in round 2.**
   - Four fresh readers, each a long-time Java player, got 12 ideas each, shuffled across the sub-moods under
     neutral ids.
   - Each reader heard only the narrator, line 1 and three midpoint lines, then wrote a guess at the twist and how
     sure it was.
   - The guesses were locked, and only then was each reader sent the real endings. It judged its own guess (right,
     partly or wrong), and said whether it was surprised and satisfied, gave a share score out of 10, and wrote a
     one-sentence read.
   - **Kept:** ideas guessed at about 50 % or less, or guessed sure and wrong. The line is round 2's: 55 % "partly"
     passed, 55 % "right" was killed.
   - **Result:** 8 of 48 passed.
3. **Then the checks, on the survivors and on 5 gate-killed wild-card candidates:**
   - vanilla, on minecraft.wiki (Java 1.21), fetched 2026-11-11;
   - the house rules (STANDARDS 0, 0a, 1, 4b, 5a, 6, 7a);
   - a place in docs/world/canon.md and map.json;
   - engine needs, against renderer/, docs/engine_features.md and `node scripts/mob_audit.js`.
   The story-critic, in pitch mode, then scored all 13 **as fixed** by the checks.

## The honest headline
- **The moods lens did much worse this time: 8 of 48 passed (17 %), against round 2's 4 of 12.** Once every idea had
  a mood, readers knew which way the ending would turn, and Minecraft players still guess mechanic twists fast. 40 of
  48 were guessed at 55-95 %.
- **Not one funny idea passed.** Every F idea was guessed at 60-95 %: a comic setup has to name the mechanic to be
  funny, so it gives the twist away. The three best become wild cards, as comedy, not mystery.
- **Only 2 of the 13 the critic scored develop:** E3 Seventy Below (eerie) and W5 The Only Light (wonder). The
  critic: "Fewer than 6 deserve to go forward". Ranks 3-6 below are there to fill the list, and each is marked with
  the critic's park or kill verdict.
- **Sweet failed hardest.** With "secretly kind" banned, every sweet idea became a mechanic fact the reader already
  knew (dark oaks need four saplings, red + yellow make orange). The two that passed (S8, S10) both died on the
  checks.
- **The banned shapes stayed out of the survivors.** E7 (disc 11 on a loop) slid into "the player left" and was
  killed; E12 and S8 sit next to "secretly kind".
- **A new sameness to watch:** E2 and F3 are the same trick: a mob head on a note block, wired to a door plate. Take
  one at most.

### 1. Seventy Below

  Stillwater's farmer.
- **Midpoint misreading (fixed):**

    ([Mob spawning#Despawning](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob_spawning#Despawning)).
