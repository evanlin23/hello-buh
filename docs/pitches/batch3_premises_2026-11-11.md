# Batch 3 premises (2026-11-11): the shortlist for the owner

Owner, 2026-11-11: "Continue with more stories and lore, we should have a back log of good stories." This page is
**premises only**, for the owner to pick from. The process (r:ra5bc8f) is: premise shortlist, then the owner picks,
then one session per script, then the owner audits, then voice and build.

**How it was made:**
- **20 premises** were pitched against docs/stories.md "House rules" and STANDARDS 0, 0a, 1, 4/4b and 5/5a.
- **Each mechanic** was checked on minecraft.wiki; the citations are below.
- **The story-critic** scored all 20 in pitch mode against Light Sleeper, runaway, Dog on the Hill and Gold.
- **A cold reader** saw the top 10. It got line 1 and the clues up to the midpoint, gave its guess and how sure it
  was, then got the ending and said whether it was surprised.

The working files (the draft table, the critic's full notes, the wiki report) were in the session scratchpad. What
matters from them is on this page.

## The honest headline
- **None of the 20 is at the runaway / Light Sleeper bar.** The critic says so, and the cold read agrees.
- **The cold reader, a casual player, was 70-95 % sure at the midpoint on 9 of the 10.** These premises run on
  dramatic irony: the player in the audience sees the danger before the naive kid does. That is the 0a gap working,
  but it is not the mystery test's "a guess, not sure".
  - **Only Pulled Away** fooled the reader. It was 75 % sure of the wrong answer (a trading hall) and called the
    ending "the best reversal of the ten".
- **The owner has accepted a known twist before.** On Six Thousand Torches (about 65 % guess it): "ok for the
  goodbye thats fine". The rewatch and share scores (6-8 out of 10) say the payoffs still land.
- **Two batch-2 runners-up can't be set in this world:**
  - **Goat Horn:** goats spawn only in snowy slopes and jagged or frozen peaks, and the survey has none of them
    ([Goat#Spawning](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Goat#Spawning)). It needs land beyond the survey, or kill it.
  - **The Pale Tree:** the survey has no pale garden, so no natural creaking heart.

### 1. Pulled Away
- **Line 1:** "A man pulled me out of my village in a BOAT. Across the GRASS. For three DAYS."
- **Twist:** every player in the audience is sure he's taking her to a trading hall. He's filling an empty village
  one villager at a time, and her Mom and uncle are already there.
- **Misreadings:**
  - "It's the best TRIP ever."
  - "He took Mom last week. And my uncle before that. None of them came BACK. They must LOVE it there."
  - "He calls me number FORTY. He's GOOD at counting."
  - "We crossed a river. The boat finally got to SWIM."
- **Reveal:** at dusk the boat slides into the old empty village. Every house is lit with a bed in every room, Mom
  and her uncle are in a doorway, the old librarian waves from hers, and the iron golem stands by the well. The bell
  rings. Cut.
- **Place, day, link:** from Oakend (empty land west of the North Inlet) to **Fernbank**, day 6000.
  - **Leaves canon:** Fernbank, empty but for the librarian and the golem since day 520, is a village again.
  - **What fans spot:** the old golem, the empty boat still at the end of the dock, and the HELLOBUH graves.
- **Vanilla:** villagers ride boats and can't leave them on their own, and boats can be leashed and towed
  ([Boat#Lead](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Boat#Lead), [Lead#History](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lead#History): in
  1.21; a lead does nothing while a player sits in the boat). Moving villagers by boat is a habit every player knows.
- **Must change:**
  1. Answer "why fill an empty village?" on screen. Plant the lonely librarian **at Fernbank itself**, as the man's
     one early line: "Someone's been ALONE a long time." The cold reader was confused when the old lady was in "a
     village they passed".
  2. Keep "He's GOOD at counting" on a line (STANDARDS 0a).
  3. Check that a lead on a boat records and draws.

### 2. The Hungry Field
- **Line 1:** "The snow round our village has EATEN thirty-one monsters. It never eats US."
- **Twist:** it isn't magic snow. It's powder snow, which only holds up leather boots. The family's "ugly brown
  BOOTS" were the rule all along, and she has just swapped hers for shiny iron ones.
- **Misreadings:**
  - "A fox walked in and SANK. Two zombies. A llama."
  - "Mom says the snow LIKES us."
  - "Everyone wears the same ugly brown BOOTS. Even the baby."
  - "I SAVED up and bought SHINY iron boots from the ARMORER. I didn't tell MOM. It's a SURPRISE."
- **Reveal:** she runs out onto the field to show them off and sinks, and frost creeps in from the edges of the
  screen. Cut on the frost.
- **Place, day, link:** **Frostgate**, day 3100.
  - **Leaves canon:** a player-made powder-snow ring round Frostgate, from about day 3000 (after More Golems). Stamp it.
- **Vanilla:** [Powder_Snow](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Powder_Snow) (leather boots walk on it; placeable from a
  bucket; it only generates in groves and snowy slopes, so the ring is player-made) and
  [Damage#Freezing](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Damage#Freezing).
- **Must change:**
  1. Make the boots a picture at phone size. In one wide the family walks the white ring in brown boots while a
     zombie chasing them sinks behind them; repeat it on the baby.
  2. "SAVED up / ARMORER" (her own act, not a gift).
  3. No line after the frost.
  4. Optional: plant "Dad brings home BUCKETS of snow."
- **Note:** against runaway it's the closest, but only if the brown boots on the white snow are as strong a picture as
  the carpets, in the first 5 s.

- **Reveal:** a big sign on the pink dirt house: "MIAS HOUSE / MY BRUTHERS WORLD", also in the caption track. Cut on

- **Place, day, link:** **Larkfield** (claimed on main: the High Meadow's north-east top, in sight of Dog Hill), day 5000.

- **Must change:**
  1. The count in line 1 (done above).
  2. The kid tries to teach him (her own act).
  3. The sign is readable at 300 px. Place it in one go, not letter by letter (the reader: "risks feeling slow").

### 4. The White Horse (batch 2's NEW pitch, now placed)
- **Line 1:** "Every THUNDERSTORM, a white horse shows up outside my WINDOW. Thirty storms. Thirty horses."
- **Twist:** the lonely horse that only comes for him is bait. It's a skeleton trap, and it only springs for a player.
- **Misreadings:**
  - "The villagers walk right past it. It only ever looks at ME."
  - "It goes HOME by morning."
  - "You can see its RIBS. It needs APPLES."
  - Mom: "A horse in a storm isn't LONELY." She shutters his window.
- **Reveal:** ten blocks out, apple held out, lightning hits the horse and four riders in iron helmets rise on bone

- **Place, day, link:** **the Cliff House**, day 2300, in a storm. It's Respawn Voice's house; "this house loses kids"
  stays a fan theory.
- **Vanilla:** [Skeleton_Horse#Skeleton_trap](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton_Horse#Skeleton_trap) (a player
  within 10 blocks; four horsemen; despawns after 15 minutes if not triggered).
- **Must change:**
  1. Keep the "only ME" line.
  2. Mom on screen for her line.
  3. Engine before the script: riders, helmets and the bolt.
  4. Canon: if The Night Never Comes keeps an era near day 2300, pick one.

### 5. Thirteen Songs
- **Line 1:** "I've found TWELVE music discs in the grass under the hill. Twelve SONGS. I can't even PLAY them."
- **Twist:** every song was a near miss. A creeper crept up behind him, and the skeleton aiming at him shot it instead.
- **Misreadings:**
  - "They grow under the hill, like MUSHROOMS."
  - "Always after a little THUNK and a puff of SMOKE."
  - "A sleepy white man under the oak WAVES his stick at me."
  - "Mom says the hill is LUCKY."
- **Reveal:** one wide at dusk. A creeper swells behind him, an arrow from the oak (aimed at him) pops it, and a disc
  spins at his feet. He grabs it: "THIRTEEN!" Cut.
- **Place, day, link:** keep it off Saltmouth. Main's lore pass puts The Bell's skeleton under the old oak on Buh's
  yard bank from about day 3550, and The Bell's kid is Saltmouth's only player.
  - **Options:** the Digger's House under Torch Hill, or below Tidewater's lighthouse rise.
  - **A fan option:** Hushwell's farm about day 1450, as the source of Light Sleeper's "music DISC by the FARM". The
    narrator would then be kid Buh, never named, and it would count as one of the two Buh stories.
- **Vanilla:** [Creeper#Drops](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#Drops) (a disc when killed by a skeleton, stray or
  bogged; no explosion on death) and [Skeleton#Behavior](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton#Behavior) (targets
  players, ignores villagers).
- **Must change:**
  1. Cut every "HISS"; it names the creeper.
  2. Drop "nobody has a jukebox": it's Light Sleeper's beat.
  3. The arrow must read as aimed at him (the cold reader's note).
  4. Space it from The Bell.

### 6. The Pumpkin Hat (the critic's reserve; the cold reader's favourite payoff)
- **Line 1:** "Mom makes me wear a PUMPKIN on my head. Every night. For three HUNDRED nights."
- **Twist:** the hat isn't cute. It's the only reason the tall, shy neighbour who visits at night is friendly to her.
- **Misreadings:**
  - "Mom says it's CUTE."
  - "A tall, shy neighbour gardens at night. He lets ME look at him."
  - "He likes my HAT."
  - Mom: "Never take your hat off at NIGHT, pumpkin."
- **Reveal:** she lifts the pumpkin off to show him her real face. The orange blur clears; he's a foot away with a
  grass block in his arms; his jaw drops. Cut on the scream.
- **Place, day, link:** the pumpkin farm on **Hushwell's north road under Dog Hill**, day 4400, at night. On the
  skyline: the dog and Pip sitting on the hilltop (`pip_and_dog`).
- **Vanilla:** [Carved_Pumpkin#Usage](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Carved_Pumpkin#Usage) and
  [Enderman#Provoking](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman#Provoking),
  [#Moving_blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman#Moving_blocks) (it can't pick up blocks from a flat floor:
  stage an edge).
- **Must change:**
  1. Cut the wet-hair and block-carrying clues before the midpoint.
  2. The pumpkin overlay only in her POV shots.
- **Risk:** the third "a custom guards a danger" story (Light Sleeper, Red Sheep). Don't release it near them.

### 7. The Snowman (batch 2's Scarecrow, no grandparent)
- **Line 1:** "SIXTY-THREE mornings in a row, a trail of SNOW runs from our snowman to my WINDOW."
- **Twist:** the snowman isn't creeping on her. Her brother built a snow golem to guard her.
- **Misreadings:**
  - "My big brother built him before he went to the MINES. He said he'd keep an EYE on me."
  - "It never SNOWS up here."
  - "Every night he SINGS under my window. Kind of BADLY."
  - "He leaves PRESENTS on the grass. Rotten FLESH."
  - "He hides when it RAINS."
- **Reveal:** the window flies open and the garden spot is empty. The camera tilts down: the golem on her porch
  knocking zombies off her wall, then looking up at her. Cut.
- **Place, day:** **the High Meadow top**, west of Larkfield (empty land), day 2500. Larkfield is unbuilt that day.
- **Vanilla:** [Snow_Golem#Behavior](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Snow_Golem#Behavior).
  - Since 1.18 it trails snow in every biome, so the critic's meadow-biome fix isn't needed.
  - Rain and water hurt it.
  - Snowballs only knock zombies back, so the rotten flesh is from zombies burning at sunrise.
- **Must change:** apply the critic's six fixes (all in the misreadings above), and no line after the tilt.
- **Weak:** the cold reader said the ending only confirms its guess. It half expected a darker turn (the brother as one
  of the zombies).

### 8. The Night Never Comes
- **Line 1:** "In my village, the night lasts ONE second. I've counted. Two HUNDRED nights."
- **Twist:** the man in the lighthouse isn't scared of the dark. He is the reason there is no dark: the night only
  skips when the player sleeps. Today he doesn't come home.
- **Misreadings:**
  - "The sky goes orange, then it BLINKS."
  - "He runs home every sunset and jumps into BED. He's SCARED of the dark."
  - "Once he was late. I saw a STAR."
  - "Today he didn't come HOME by sunset."
- **Reveal:** orange, then dark blue, then more stars than she's ever seen. The lighthouse lamp switches itself on for
  the first time, and the moon rises over his empty bed. Cut.
- **Place, day:** **Tidewater** (who built the lighthouse becomes canon).
- **Vanilla:** [Bed#Sleeping](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bed#Sleeping) (players_sleeping_percentage 100 by default;
  in 1.21.11 a player can sleep from tick 12523).
- **Canon blocker:** time is global, so 200 one-second nights is a world-wide era like the Long Day. No night shot in
  any story may fall in it, and no other player may be awake then.
  - **Re-date it** to an empty window after day 4300. The candidate was 5100-5600, but that collides with Pip's
    nightly sits from 4300, so the lead must pick the window.
- **Weak:** the cold reader found the ending moving but unclear ("did he quit or die?"). It's the fourth player
  goodbye; never release it next to Dog on the Hill or Six Thousand Torches.

## For the next premise round
The cold reader guessed most twists from the clues alone, so the clues gave too much away. Two changes:
- **Don't end the setup with the kid's plan** ("tonight she's going to...").
- **Hold each mechanic's tell-tale word** (hiss, pumpkin, boots) until after the midpoint.

Pulled Away worked because the viewer's own player knowledge was the misdirect. More premises should turn a habit
players know against them.
