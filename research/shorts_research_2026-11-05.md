# Research: Minecraft stunt and gag Shorts for @HelloBuh

Collected 5 Nov 2026, signed out and read-only (no logins, likes, comments, subscriptions or downloads).

**Method.** I ran about 40 YouTube searches using your search ideas and close variants, which turned up about 1,000 Shorts. I filtered those down to Minecraft gameplay channels with 1K–500K subscribers. For each channel I pulled the Shorts tab sorted by **Popular** and the latest uploads. I opened each Short in Table B and checked 2–3 frames (about 0.3–0.5 s, the middle and near the end) to read the caption, bands, hook and sound label. I clicked the sound label on the Short to get its use count.

**How the numbers were recorded**
- **Views and dates** come from the Short's own page data on 5 Nov 2026, so view counts are exact rather than rounded.
- **Subscriber counts** and **"best Short" views** are shown exactly as YouTube displays them.
- **Typical Short views** is the median of each channel's ~48 most recent Shorts. I calculated it from the view counts YouTube shows.
- **Edit tricks:** I only spot-checked frames, so I list a trick only when I saw it in a frame. Anything I couldn't see is marked "n/a" rather than guessed.

**Excluded:**
- **Animation channels:** Blocklyte (looked like gameplay in search, but it's animated), JustAJoe/joenhh and Kamui.
- **Dream/manhunt clip channels:** PIoomsy, DrDonut Clips.
- **Compilation/ranking channels:** BlockRanked, TheoClippedIt.
- **Advertisers that showed up in searches:** Liberty Mutual, AT&T, PetSmart and others.

## List D: Five formats a scripted bot can stage on a private server

Each of these needs only a single player POV, `/fill` and `/setblock` builds, `/summon`, and `/item`/`/give`. A caption and a timer or countdown can be added in editing.

1. **"POV: You Throw an Ender Pearl But…" countdown clutch**
   - **Caption:** white bold "POV: You Throw an Ender Pearl But…" in a top black band, one red keyword.
   - **Stunt:** big centered overlay "Elytra About To Break: 2.14" counting down over a lava lake. The elytra breaks, then a last-instant pearl lands you on a ledge.
   - **Sound:** misery. (pupsies).
   - **Staging:** `/item replace … elytra` with damage set near max, `/fill` lava, place the landing ledge. Fully scriptable.
   - **Examples:** https://www.youtube.com/shorts/MrKNIRuMvoc, https://www.youtube.com/shorts/8buPNR7RnH0

2. **12-second skyblock "one job" timer stunt**
   - **Caption:** a single short line in a thin white font with bands top and bottom ("She had one job…", "Just in time. 😲").
   - **Stunt:** a tiny sky island in the void, an on-screen countdown, and a task (bridge, catch falling blocks) that fails or saves at 0.
   - **Sound:** Original Sound.
   - **Staging:** void superflat world, `/fill` the island, `/summon falling_block`.
   - **Examples:** https://www.youtube.com/shorts/c6BSiczC9rs, https://www.youtube.com/shorts/GNvddxOAkj0

3. **Villager "job" gag**
   - **Caption:** "POV: THE VILLAGER IS UNEMPLOYED 💀" in caps with colored words, or "Pov :- Villager".
   - **Stunt:** a villager missing or abusing its workstation. The joke lands within 1 s.
