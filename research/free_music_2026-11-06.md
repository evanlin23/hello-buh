# Free music for the Shorts (2026-11-06)

A research agent compiled this. The tracks are in `songs/free/` (gitignored; download them with
the URLs below). Demos: the same Short with each track, made with
`python3 scripts/add_song.py <name> --song songs/free/<track>.mp3 --out out/music_demo/<x>.mp4`.

## Sources
- **YouTube Audio Library:** monetization OK and never claimed. Some tracks need a credit.
  There's very little phonk, and it downloads only from inside YouTube Studio.
  https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882
- **NoCopyrightSounds (NCS):** monetization OK with the exact credit in the description. Tracks
  are in Content ID; NCS says creators who follow its usage policy get no claims or strikes. If one
  appears, fix the credit and dispute. It has a Brazilian phonk genre and downloads without a
  login. https://ncs.io/usage-policy
- **Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod):** CC BY 4.0, credit required. Occasional third-party false claims;
  dispute those. https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html
- **Pixabay Music:** free, no credit needed, but some uploaders register Content ID, and it blocks
  scripted downloads.
- **Uppbeat:** the free tier gives a per-download credit tied to your account, and needs a login.

## Recommendation
Default to NCS Brazilian phonk with the credit block. Use Kevin MacLeod for comedic beats. Keep
the YouTube Audio Library as the zero-risk fallback. Use the Shorts audio picker (trending songs,
added by hand in the app) for occasional reach tests.
